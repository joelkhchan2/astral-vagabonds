Astral Vagabonds

Battle Stations

Ship combat, player quick reference

Each round

1

Side acts. Your ship's side goes, then theirs.

2

Assign crew. Everyone aboard and free gets put on a station below.

3

Resolve. Make each station's roll.

4

Free crew act normally. Anyone not on a station fights, casts, or does anything else as themselves.

Words you need to know

Crew pool
Everyone aboard, able-bodied, and not already on a station.
Station
A job you can put crew on this round.
Cost
How many crew a station needs. Short by even one, it does nothing.
Committed
Once you're on a station, that's your whole round.
Threshold
A hit below this number does nothing to the hull.
Boarding
Two different things. Helm can lock the ships together. Jumping ship yourself, below, is separate.

Stations

1
Helm
Fly, and pick one: Evasive Action, Full Burn, or lock with a target ship.
2
Ram
One ram attack. Your ship takes half that damage back. Replaces Helm this round.
2-3
Ballista
Fire the gun. Cost depends on the ship, ask your DM.
1
Engineer
Patch the hull, DC 12 Technology/Int, once per fight each.
1
Coordinator
Give an ally advantage, impose disadvantage on an attack against the ship, or +1d4 to an ally's roll.
−1
Personal Boarding
Leave the pool, jump to the other ship, fight there on your own.
Not enough crew for a station this round? It just doesn't fire. No partial credit, pick something else to do.
