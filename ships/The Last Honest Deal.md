# The Ship (currently unnamed)

> The crew bought her at session 0 and chose **not to name her yet**, flying under Captain Vane's good name instead. The DM's working title, from the yard-seller's line that this was "the last honest deal you'll ever make," is *the Last Honest Deal*. Update this doc if and when they christen her.

## Ship Profile
**Class / Profile:** Sturdy Trader (bought prior-service from Danforth Certified Hulls).
**AC:** 19, plus Plating I. **Damage threshold:** 20 (a single hit under 20 does nothing).
**HP:** 250
**Speed:** ~45 ft tactical (Trader 55, −10 for Sturdy), plus a Trim Rig that adds 5 ft and holds attitude unhelmed; 100 million miles/day in open space.
**Crew capacity:** 11 (running with 7).
**Cargo:** 20 tons.
**Air:** base 120 days (no life-support upgrades bought). The crew has sworn off any job longer than 60 days rather than risk the clock.

## The Build
Bought and outfitted at session 0 for roughly 35,650 gp of the 50,000 budget.

| System | Choice |
|---|---|
| Hull | Sturdy Trader, 20,000 |
| Helm | Coilworks Mk IV (standard), 8,000 |
| Armor | Plating I (+1 AC) |
| Weapons | 2 ballistae, blunt ram |
| Weapon upgrades | Sureshot Rotator (aim = bonus action), Ready Magazine (load = bonus action) |
| Ammunition | 50 standard shots |
| Mobility | Trim Rig (+5 ft, holds attitude unhelmed) |
| Life support | none (base 120-day air) |
| Signature feature | none chosen |
| Fittings / instruments / aftermarket | none |

**Gunnery note:** with Sureshot Rotator and Ready Magazine on the ballistae, aiming and loading are both bonus actions, so a single gunner can fire a ballista in one action once it is set up rather than the usual three-action load-aim-fire.

## Money
Started with 50,000 gp. After the ship and outfit, about **14,350 gp** remained in the account. Aerion also drew **10,000 gp from his personal trust fund**, kept separate as an emergency reserve, for about 24,350 available in total. Operating cost is all-inclusive: **500 gp/month for up to 6 crew, +100 gp/month per additional crew member**, covering wages, maintenance, food, and air, deducted each session. Hull repairs are 20 gp per hit point on top of that.

## History
Aerion's previous ship blew up when the cook took his nightly smoke beside pre-loaded cannons. The ship was insured; the payout, plus some family money and Aerion's trust-fund draw, bought this replacement. Tack, the engineer, saved the tattered flag from the old ship and reattached it to this one. Most of the old fourteen-person crew lost faith and left after the explosion; the loyal few stayed, and this is their fresh start.

## Layout (loose, fill in as play needs it)

### Upper deck (above the gravity plane)
- **Helm room:** the spelljamming helm (a magic throne you attune to and steer by thought, wearing the helm-piece), navigation charts, Estra's card table. Aerion's manifests and Orena's route maps cover the surfaces.
- **Main deck:** rigging, the two ballista positions (port and starboard), open working space.
- **Lookout:** an elevated watch position; Oz and Orena keep it.
- **Galley:** where Finny holds court and the Manifest Board printout gets pinned.

### Lower deck (below the gravity plane, gravity inverted)
- **Crew quarters:** berths for the seven, close.
- **Captain's quarters:** Estra's, sparse.
- **Cargo holds:** inventory tagged and tracked because Aerion insists.
- **Engineering:** Tack's workshop, organized chaos, every component named.
- **Sick bay / galley stores:** Brass's, doubling as her medical cabinet, which she restocks out of her own wages at Bral.

## The ship as character
She is battered, improbable, and held together by stubbornness and Tack's care, and she mirrors the crew: near-broke, loyal, and somehow still flying. The creaking is a running bit (Tack says she is talking). Good ship moments to deploy over the campaign: the first serious hull damage and the scramble to save her, a moment where she seems to respond to the crew, and the day someone offers to replace her and the crew's reaction shows how they feel about home.
