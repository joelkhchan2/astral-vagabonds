The Ship as a Character

"She's just talking." A timber ship, she creaks. Tack insists it's her asking for attention and goes to fix a plank. Pure flavor — but if the creaking ever stops, something is very wrong.
Tack's flag. He saved the tattered colours from the old ship and re-stitched them onto this hull — her memory of the crew she replaced.
The empty berths. Complement 11, running 7. A made-up bunk nobody uses is a hook waiting to be loaded.
No name. She flies unnamed, on the Captain's reputation. Whether the crew ever christens her says exactly how they feel about home.
