# Aerion Kyoya Windsong (Priscilla's PC)

**Race:** Astral Elf · **Class:** Samurai Fighter, level 5 · **Background:** Merchant · **Alignment:** Neutral Good · **Age:** 132 · **Height:** 6'3"
**Shipboard roles:** Bosun, Quartermaster, Cargo Handler.

## Who he is
The overlooked youngest son of House Windsong, a trading house. He has the mind and temperament for the family business but is the least-noticed of the sons, and the family is quietly happy to have him off-world and out of the way. He is fine with that; being unwatched suits him. He keeps to himself, is hard to read, and does not enjoy long conversations (his ideal one lasts under four minutes). Favorite food is plain white rice or a piece of toast with nothing on it. A bad cook with a bad sense of direction.

## What he wants
To make his own fortune and, secretly, to **buy out and take over his father's company** on his own terms. He has been saving toward that for far longer than the five years he has been aboard this crew. He would not tell anyone how much he has or what it is for.

## Family (House Windsong)
- **Father, Vaelros Windsong** (525) -- runs the trading house; arrogant, capable, and dismissive of his youngest.
- **Mother, Aerynna Nightbreeze** (445) -- of House Nightbreeze, one of the five founding houses (the financiers and insurers). She divorced Vaelros when Aerion was ten and left; the marriage was arranged and they despised each other.
- **Brothers:** Aeric (258), Aeris (234), Aeravellis (182). Aerion is the youngest of four.
- **Uncle, Vaelren Windsong** (300) -- his father's youngest brother, a "space samurai" adventurer who trained Aerion (and, at the same camp, Orena) in the sword. Aerion is closest to him.

The family is among those competing for the **vacant fifth seat** on the board of founding houses. What exactly House Windsong offers for that seat is Priscilla's to decide.

## Flurry
A young silver dragon Aerion found as an egg on a treasure hunt with this crew about 4.5 years ago and reluctantly, then secretly devotedly, took responsibility for. He dotes on her, dresses her up, and would deny all of it. **She is a baby: she does not fight and should never be in combat.** Under the surface she carries **lunar astral dragon blood** (an ancestor back in the line), which is latent for now and mostly shows as an uncanny sensitivity to the astral -- see `npcs/Flurry.md` for her heritage, her role as a GM "barometer" for the living sky, and the late-game payoff.

## How he came to this crew
He joined this (non-family) crew about five years ago because it looked like the most successful treasure-finding outfit around, and his aim was to stockpile money. They found Flurry and a hoard early, then failed job after job into near-destitution, and he stayed anyway because, though he will not say it, he is attached to them. When his old ship blew up he used the insurance, some family money, and 10,000 gp from his trust fund to buy the new one, and re-met his old friend Orena and hired her on.

## Traits and hobbies
Scrupulous and slow to trust. Favorite dish: plain white rice or dry toast. Hobbies: working the ship's books, doting on Flurry, reading, plotting the takeover of his father's business, and writing a young-adult fantasy series about seafaring on the astral plane.
