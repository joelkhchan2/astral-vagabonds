# Sable

## The Surface Read
Tortle deckhand-gunner, hired session 4 on a nat-20 impromptu mop test. Self-described as the fastest turtle this side of space, tops 20 mph, built like a wall, surprisingly quick, surprisingly warm. Trained by a mentor she calls **Splinter**, a reclusive **ratkin** sensei, the joke landing on purpose (a rat who trained a tortle instead of turtles), and whether he's actually as good as she claims is deliberately never confirmed.

**Voice tic:** narrates her own exploits mid-task, sometimes in the third person ("Sable does it again"). **Physical tic:** taps her own shell like a lucky charm before acting.

## Motive and ambition
A real reputation, a legend that's actually true, not just self-told.

## Weakness
Exaggerates everything. Bravado sometimes writes checks her abilities can't quite cash yet, and there's a real insecurity underneath the swagger she'd never admit to out loud.

## Strength
Genuinely trained, solid in a fight, real tortle toughness (the shell, the ability to withdraw and weather a hit). Fiercely loyal once she's bonded with someone, not performative about that part at all.

## Want
To go back someday and have Splinter actually mean it when he says he's proud of her, whatever "going back" would even look like.

## Sidekick build, Warrior (level 6)
*[Reconstructed from the 2018 Unearthed Arcana Sidekicks playtest, not the final published text. Verify against the book before it matters at the table.]*

**Why Warrior (changed from Expert at level 5):** her strength is a solid fight and a shell, not skirmishing. The old Sneak Attack and Expertise are gone. "Fastest turtle" lives in Acrobatics proficiency and the bit itself.

- **Base:** tortle on a commoner chassis plus six sidekick levels. Proficiency +3.
- **AC** 17 (tortle natural armor) · **HP** 45 (7d8 + 14) · **Speed** 30 ft. · **Initiative** +0
- **Scores:** STR 16 (+3), DEX 10 (+0), CON 14 (+2), INT 8 (-1), WIS 13 (+1), CHA 10 (+0). Level 4 ASI took STR +2, level 6 took CON +2.
- **Saves:** CON +5 (sidekick save proficiency).
- **Skills:** Athletics +6, Acrobatics +3, Perception +4 (passive 14). Proficient with all armor, shields, and weapons.
- **Tortle:** Hold Breath (1 hour), Claws, Natural Armor, **Shell Defense** (action: withdraw into her shell for +4 AC and advantage on STR and CON saves, but she is prone, speed 0, has disadvantage on DEX saves, and is incapacitated; bonus action to emerge).
- **Second Wind:** bonus action, regain 1d10 + 6 HP. Once per short or long rest.
- **Danger Sense:** advantage on DEX saves against effects she can see.
- **Improved Critical:** crits on 19 or 20.
- **Extra Attack:** two attacks with the Attack action.
- **Attacks:** Handaxe +6, 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing. Claws +6, 5 (1d4 + 3) slashing. Pistol (DMG) only +3, range 30/90, 5 (1d10) piercing, which suits a gunner whose bravado writes checks she can't cash.
