# Vaelren Windsong

## The Surface Read
Aerion's uncle, 300 years old, Vaelros's youngest brother, the **space samurai** who walked away from the family business to fly on his own terms. Level 18 Samurai Fighter. Loud Hawaiian shirt, socks and sandals, orange wine in hand, and nothing about him reads as a threat until he draws. Still active, still sharp: captain of the *Windsong*, a genuine, earned 20th on the Manifest Board (`factions/The Manifest Board.md`), far above the crew's own rank and close enough to the top ten to matter.

**Two names.** *Space samurai* is what he is and what the whole sector calls him. ***The Storm Wall*** is a rarer name, used only by people tied to the Chained Lanterns and the Gate (below). If a stranger says it, they were there, or their family was. Hearing it is information.

**Voice and tic:** unhurried, amused, exacting. He answers a question with a better question and will not draw his sword to prove a point he can make with a look. *"Don't show me the form. Show me you understand why the form exists, and then break it correctly."* (He learned the question habit from his eldest brother, which he would hate to hear.) **He reads every contract aloud, twice, as a joke.** Nobody knows why yet.

**Physical tic:** easy, loose posture that reads as retired until the moment it isn't. Session 5 added a second register underneath the Hawaiian shirt: he's aware of his own rank and station even off duty, and his private room at the Compass Rose carries active wards he never mentions out loud.

**His want:** to have actually left, and to prove to himself it's possible to fly well without his brother's name attached to it. The Fifth House bid (below) has quietly become the same want wearing a bigger coat.

## The Ronin
House Windsong is the clan, Vaelros is the lord, and Vaelren is the masterless swordsman who left. That is the frame to play him through. He kept a foot in the house's orbit for a century (he was a guest at the gala five years ago), and cut loose for good only two years ago. The longer story is below.

## History, in order
Keep this as a DM reference. The players get it a piece at a time (see **Reveal order**).

### The Chained Lanterns and the Peace (about 180 years ago)
- **The Chained Lanterns:** a line of independent wildspace stations strung along a lane, each close enough to see the next lamp, so the whole thing reads as a long chain of lights. They lit the lane free for anyone and ran the only refuge stations on it. Every lamp-keeper answers for the lamp beside them. The chain holds because each link keeps its own word.
- **The war:** Windsong wanted that lane for itself. A toll dispute turned violent. Vaelren (about 120) commanded the house escort wing and held **[the Gate]**, the chain's central link (placeholder name), for eleven days. His sword threw wind and force across the lane against volleys and boarders. The Lanterns called him **the Storm Wall**, because nothing crossed.
- **The surrender:** he gave his word as a samurai to the Lantern envoys. No harm to their people, and their stations keep their own charters.
- **How he "lost":** he left the paper to Vaelros (about 345 then), because Vaelros was the one who was good at paper. Vaelros never lied. He asked one question: *"Did you promise them autonomy, or did you promise them safety?"* The treaty kept the letter of Vaelren's word and gutted the rest. The stations became tributary. The house took the lane and the credit.
- **The exit:** he asked to be released from command and Vaelros agreed warmly. The family calls that "eased out." He calls it leaving, and over the next century that became true.
- **Rebuilding:** every ship and crew was house property on paper, so he started again from zero as a mercenary captain. That is where the "basically unlimited money" comes from, and why he buys and burns ships like disposables. He learned what ownership can be used for.
- **Vaelros was never the better swordsman.** He was the better paper. Samurai gets a courtier's polish too (Elegant Courtier), so Vaelren can play court. Vaelros plays it better.

### The camp (about 12 to 17 years ago)
A hard, warm training camp on the quieter drifts, run like a dojo. He was strict because he was still carrying the weight, and he taught the form to youths so they could hold their own word. Orena came to learn the blade, and Aerion was sent there as the overlooked youngest.

### The gala (about 5 years ago)
He is a guest at a Windsong function, entertaining people, when Vaelros gives Aerion the ultimatum and says of the Samurai path: *"Look at where it got him. Do you want to inherit his same end?"* Aerion leaves the house soon after. Vaelren is still inside the orbit.

### The heir war, and the night he left (about 2 years ago, adjustable)
- **The heir war is informal and escalating.** No formal contest exists. House custom settles heir quarrels with duels "to the yield," with a witness who vouches for the terms. The main contenders are **Aerion's three brothers**, with **Aeric as the main heir** (`factions/House Windsong.md`). Cousins can be pulled in, as session 4 established.
- **The witness:** Vaelros asked Vaelren to witness the heir duels, because his word is the one the families trust, and to guarantee that no one dies. He agreed, because he thought he could protect his nieces and nephews.
- **Vaelma:** a cousin of Aerion's, about 195, daughter of one of Vaelros and Vaelren's middle siblings (the fourth of five, about 340, unnamed and offscreen). She was killed in a duel and revived. She came back "tweaked," never quite the same. (The DM's session 4 throwaway about "old Velma," now properly named.)
- **The repeat:** Vaelros asked the same shape of question as at the Peace: *"Did you promise nobody would die, or that nobody would stay dead?"* Vaelren saw that he had walked into the same trap twice, with the same word.
- **The break:** he left that night, with no ceremony. This is the "I just left" he told Aerion in session 4. It answers why now and not when Aerion left five years ago: nobody had asked for his word again until now.

### Since
The shirts, the shanty, the retired jolly uncle. He set the weight down fast and has not picked up anything since. The Fifth House is the first thing that might make him.

## The Grudge, and Why It Stays Latent
He holds a real grudge against Vaelros, and it sits under the jokes ("one way or another, he's going down"). A samurai's word binds him even when it was turned against him. He promised the Lanterns and the families, and those promises still stand. He cannot move against his brother without breaking his own code. The honorable route is to out-build him, which makes the Fifth House the only path his code allows. It also left him hating house politics more than ever.

## The Sword
A **Windsong heirloom** he has always carried. **It sings:** when drawn, it sounds a single note in wind, which may be where the family name comes from. Players hear it before they see what it does.
- **Powers:** *wind wall* and *wall of force* (the dome), on charges. Exact numbers wait for the stat block rebuild.
- Vaelros has a technical claim to the blade as house property. Nobody has pressed it. Held thread, usable any time.
- A ronin carrying the clan's sword is the whole character in one image.

## History with Aerion and Orena
He **trained Aerion to fight** when nobody asked him to, and it was at his camp that Aerion met **Orena**, who came to learn the sword; the two trained together and became lifelong friends. Whether he trained Aerion out of kindness or out of some quieter intent is his to reveal, and he may not know himself. The Atlas line from session 4 is him describing a posture he recognizes: *"You've stood the same way since you were twelve, like the whole world falls unless you hold it up. Nobody's going to blame you for setting it down."*

**Confirmed, session 4: the break from House Windsong is total.** His own words: "I'm not a Windsong anymore." He recommends out-of-work crew to the *Night Fury* when they need a hire, which is where session 4's three candidates (Tomlin, Judo, Sable) actually came from.

He's kept a pet unicorn phoenix he calls **Fury**, a clear, deliberate mirror of Flurry he's never acknowledged out loud. Fury regrows a feather whenever one is plucked, and each feather works as a one-use sending stone, good for one message back to him from anywhere. He handed over five of them total by the end of session 5. Fury burns up whatever ship she's aboard, which is why he bought a galleon and then a metal rowboat.

## The Fifth House
Live as of session 4, now the main vehicle for Aerion's endgame. Half as a joke, Aerion and Orena asked whether Vaelren would be interested in starting a house of his own to claim the vacant fifth seat. He took it seriously. A joint Persuasion check succeeded, and he agreed, in principle, to start the paperwork. This isn't a separate path from Aerion's old buyout plan, it's the same ambition through a faster door. Full scene: `sessions/Session 4.md`. **Locked: Vaelros stays unaware for now** (`sessions/Threads, Session 5 and Beyond.md`).

**Why a man who hates house politics would do this.** His principle is **no proxies: whoever makes a promise carries it themselves.** It is the opposite of what Vaelros did to him. The Fifth House is the Chain reborn, a house where each member answers for their own light and rank comes from work, as on the Manifest Board. The paradox is real and he knows it: a seat is the only place a rule like that gets leverage. It also rhymes with the Vane Compact and the campaign's question of who owns the roads.

**The pen.** He will not let anyone else hold the pen on his paperwork. Handing it to someone would be a real act of trust. Whether anyone takes it, and who, is for the table to decide.

## Session 5: Prudence Hale, and minding the Vellum/Carrow box
Found at the Compass Rose in the middle of a drink with **Prudence Hale** (`npcs/Prudence Hale.md`), House Nightbreeze's senior underwriter, the same woman who'd just offered Aerion and Orena 20,000 gp for anything they had on House Vellum. On a natural 20 Perception check he noticed the two of them listening in and invited them to sit rather than make a scene of it, easy with the ambush in a way that read as genuinely unbothered.

**The connection, corrected:** Prudence isn't an ex-wife of his. She's been married four times over three men, divorced from all four, and one of those three was a man of House Nightbreeze, which is what makes her something like an ex-cousin-in-law to the Windsongs by marriage, not blood, and not to Vaelren personally. He's dry and plainly wary of her regardless: *"I trust her as far as I could throw her. It's pretty far. But I don't throw women."* The source of that wariness is left open (see **Parked**).

**Minding the box.** Estra entrusted him with the Vellum/Carrow correspondence box (not her own chest, which stays separately with her, `campaign/Story Spine.md`) while the crew heads into House Vellum territory, reasoning he's trusted and more than strong enough that nobody's taking it from him. He agreed without hesitation, and he showed **no familiarity with Vellum or Carrow** when he held it. Whether he looked inside is still his to reveal or not.

## Playing him
- **Easy and warm** with the crew, genuinely fond of both PCs, but never soft when it actually matters. The wards on his room and his instant read on being watched are both real: underneath the shirt, he never stopped being dangerous.
- **Lines to use:**
  - *"A promise you didn't carry yourself is just somebody else's paperwork."*
  - *"My brother has never once lied to me. Remember that when you're deciding what it's worth."*
  - To anyone offering a deal on his behalf: *"I'll read it myself. Twice."*
- **The Iroh beat.** The old general under the retired uncle comes back only when someone offers a deal on his behalf, or uses the Storm Wall name for a promise he didn't make. The joking stops. The shirt stays on. The camp voice returns: quiet, exact, a little terrible. Use it once or twice a campaign. It reads as dread because the tone before it was warm.
- **Warmth half.** The Chained Lanterns survived, and some of them remember him fondly. They have a song about the man who held the Gate, and it ends before the treaty. Vaelma is alive and changed, and she is a possible cameo.
- **Don't resolve** what he thinks of Prudence beyond wary amusement.
- **A clean route back into any scene** at the Rock of Bral: feeding him news, asking after the chest, or just a drink at the Compass Rose.

## Reveal order
Seed, don't explain. Let the pattern assemble.
1. He reads contracts aloud (already visible).
2. Someone says "Storm Wall."
3. He gives Aerion one line about who holds the pen.
4. The full story comes out only if a PC asks the right question.
