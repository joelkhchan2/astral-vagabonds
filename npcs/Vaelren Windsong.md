# Vaelren Windsong

## The Surface Read
Aerion's uncle, 300 years old, Vaelros's youngest brother, the "space samurai" who walked away from the family business to fly on his own terms. Loud Hawaiian shirt, socks and sandals, orange wine in hand, nothing about him reads as a threat until he draws. Still active, still sharp: captain of the *Windsong*, a genuine, earned 20th on the Manifest Board (`factions/The Manifest Board.md`), far above the crew's own rank and close enough to the top ten to matter.

**Voice and tic:** unhurried, amused, exacting. He answers a question with a better question and will not draw his sword to prove a point he can make with a look. *"Don't show me the form. Show me you understand why the form exists, and then break it correctly."*

**Physical tic:** easy, loose posture that reads as retired until the moment it isn't. Session 5 added a second register underneath the Hawaiian shirt: he's aware of his own rank and station even off duty, and his private room at the Compass Rose carries active wards he never mentions out loud.

**His want:** to have actually left, and to prove to himself it's possible to fly well without his brother's name attached to it. The Fifth House bid (below) has quietly become the same want wearing a bigger coat.

## History with Aerion and Orena
He **trained Aerion to fight** when nobody asked him to, and it was at his camp that Aerion met **Orena**, who came to learn the sword; the two trained together and became lifelong friends. Whether he trained Aerion out of kindness or out of some quieter intent is his to reveal, and he may not know himself.

**Confirmed, session 4: the break from House Windsong is total.** Not just eased out, not just distant. His own words: "I'm not a Windsong anymore." Recommends out-of-work crew to the *Night Fury* when they need a hire, which is where session 4's three candidates (Tomlin, Judo, Sable) actually came from.

He's kept a pet unicorn phoenix he calls **Fury**, a clear, deliberate mirror of Flurry he's never acknowledged out loud. Fury regrows a feather whenever one is plucked, and each feather works as a one-use sending stone, good for one message back to him from anywhere. He handed over five of them total by the end of session 5 (one on request, four more pulled off Fury directly after).

## The Fifth House
Live as of session 4, now the main vehicle for Aerion's endgame. Half as a joke, Aerion and Orena asked whether Vaelren would be interested in starting a house of his own to claim the vacant fifth seat. He took it seriously. A joint Persuasion check succeeded, and he agreed, in principle, to start the paperwork. This isn't a separate path from Aerion's old buyout plan, it's the same ambition through a faster door: uncle and nephew building a house real enough to take the seat Vaelros is openly campaigning for, which makes Aerion's new house and his father's old one structural rivals for the same chair. Full scene: `sessions/Session 4.md`. **Locked: Vaelros stays unaware for now** (`sessions/Threads, Session 5 and Beyond.md`).

## Session 5: Prudence Hale, and minding the lockbox
Found at the Compass Rose in the middle of a drink with **Prudence Hale** (`npcs/Prudence Hale.md`), House Nightbreeze's senior underwriter, the same woman who'd just offered Aerion and Orena 20,000 gp for anything they had on House Vellum. On a natural 20 Perception check he noticed the two of them listening in and invited them to sit rather than make a scene of it, easy with the ambush in a way that read as genuinely unbothered.

**The connection, corrected:** Prudence isn't an ex-wife of his. She's been married four times over three men, divorced from all four, and one of those three was a man of House Nightbreeze, which is what makes her something like an ex-cousin-in-law to the Windsongs by marriage, not blood, and not to Vaelren personally. He's dry and plainly wary of her regardless: *"I trust her as far as I could throw her. It's pretty far. But I don't throw women."*

**Minding the chest.** Estra entrusted him with her locked chest (the Compact's chart and casualty records, `npcs/Captain Estra Vane.md`) while the crew heads into House Vellum territory, reasoning he's trusted and more than strong enough that nobody's taking it from him. He agreed without hesitation. Whether he's curious enough to look inside it himself before the crew gets back is an open question worth holding in reserve, not yet decided.

## Playing him
- Easy and warm with the crew, genuinely fond of both PCs, but never soft when it actually matters. The wards on his room and his instant read on being watched are both real: underneath the shirt, he never stopped being dangerous.
- Don't resolve what he thinks of Prudence beyond wary amusement. Let a future scene decide whether that wariness is personal history or just professional instinct about her house.
- He's a clean, low-effort route back into a scene any time the crew is at the Rock of Bral: feeding him news, asking after the chest, or just a drink at the Compass Rose all work without new setup.
