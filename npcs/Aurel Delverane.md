# Aurel Delverane

## The Surface Read
Thirty-one, younger daughter of a founding house that's never quite forgiven her for choosing fieldwork over a boardroom. Runs survey and acquisition out of the Farthing Station personally, hands dirty, not delegated.

**Her true appearance:** compact, quick-moving, dark hair cropped short and practical, weathered from real fieldwork rather than a desk. Nobody outside her own family has seen this face in years.

**Voice tic (as herself):** narrates her own observations out loud, half to herself, half to whoever's nearby, a habit from working alone too long.

**Physical tic:** chart-ink on at least one finger, never quite washed off between legs. This survives under the disguise too, a real tell for anyone who ever looks closely at her hands as "Wyndham."

**Want:** to be the one who's right, about the Windward Edge, about the Stilling, about any of it, before anyone senior takes her seriously. Everything else she does, including the alias below, is in service of this one thing.

**Standing canon:** she and the crew are neighbours and neither knows it yet, she works the Windward Edge, they work the Verge. Flagged early as the single most likely person in Wildspace to become a real ally. Open Water, Open Season (`sessions/Session 4 Prep.md` §5(C), `sessions/Session 5 Prep.md` §6) is her survey job, still picked but not yet flown as of session 5.

**She also captains the *Farthing*, ranked 3rd on the Manifest Board** (`factions/The Manifest Board.md`), third despite a third the hull size of the ships above her, because fresh route data scores enormously. This is a serious, capable operator, not a desk surveyor with a side hobby, which is exactly why the disguise below has to exist: her own ship and crew are far too recognizable to use for anything she doesn't want traced back to her.

## Her second life: Wyndham Aldane

**Wyndham Aldane is Aurel Delverane, in person, under a different name.** Not a double, not an agent reporting to her, her. "Hands dirty, not delegated" is her whole professional reputation already, this is simply how far that actually goes: she doesn't send someone else to watch a crew work and wait for a summary, she goes herself, and the only way to do that without her own name attached is to not be herself for the duration.

**How she pulls it off, mechanically.** Aurel is a genuinely formidable wizard, seventeen levels of it, which tracks with actually captaining a top-3 ship into dangerous frontier water for years, kept almost entirely private (full stats: `rules/Stat Blocks.md`). Disguise Self is a first-level spell, trivial for her, and she simply keeps it running through every waking hour of a stint, the height, build, hair, and face the crew knows as Wyndham are not her own. Dispelling it would show a face only her own family has seen in years.

**Why it fools even her own office.** Nobody at the Farthing Station has ever been in a room with both "Aurel" and "Wyndham" at once, because Aurel is never present as herself while Wyndham is aboard a ship. Business with her office runs through a proxy (**Pell Varnish**, below) specifically so her own name stays off the direct transaction. There is nothing to catch, because the two identities have never once overlapped in front of a witness.

**Decided: she is not a plant, and she is not working with anyone.** The investigator-versus-plant question once left open for Wyndham is resolved. She isn't reporting to Vellum, she isn't quietly aligned with any other investigator chasing the same case, and she isn't sharing this with her own house either. This is entirely hers, alone, by choice, not because nobody else is looking, because she doesn't trust anyone else with it yet, including her own family.

## What she's actually chasing

Two tracks, same person, same underlying drive, kept carefully separate because one is professionally respectable and the other isn't.

- **The Aurel track, legitimate:** chart data on the Windward Edge's standing surf creeping inward, a visible, local symptom of the Stilling. She has pattern (specific stretches that used to be reliable and aren't, routes her own office has quietly stopped recommending without officially delisting them) but not mechanism, and isn't chasing one yet. This is fieldwork her family tolerates because it's at least a job.
- **The Wyndham track, fringe:** a real, serious thesis that the Tide carries accumulated memory, not just nutrients, that kindori carry memory as much as light, that a current is in some sense worn into being by everyone who's traveled it (`campaign/Story Spine.md`, the GM-only deeper truth, Tier 3+). She didn't invent the idea, she's one of the only people trying to build a rigorous case for it instead of leaving it as sailors' superstition, and it would be genuinely embarrassing for a founding house's daughter to be caught chasing it under her own name.
- **Why Estra, specifically.** A captain who ran the first real fleet to attempt the nomadic method, fifteen years of lived migration and current data charted by hand before anyone had a working theory, is exactly the field evidence the Wyndham thesis needs and cannot get anywhere else (`npcs/Captain Estra Vane.md`, the Locked Chest). Aurel doesn't know the chest exists. She knows the *Commodore* rumor, same as the rest of the Bral Reach, and she's patient enough to have booked a real stint aboard on nothing but a hunch that the rumor has more underneath it.

**Funding for the Wyndham track:** Cordelia Leaftully, quietly, out of her own discretionary budget, not the whole Leaftully family, whose older branches already consider wildspace fauna study beneath them. Partly real professional curiosity on Cordelia's end (kindori song as a memory signal would validate her own fauna research too), partly solidarity, she knows exactly what it is to be the family's embarrassing branch and has sympathy to spare for another one. **This is not secure funding.** If Cordelia's own family ever finds out what she's spending money on, it ends for both of them. Aurel's real deadline on this track is Cordelia's own standing, not a calendar.

This also means Wyndham's eventual return doesn't need a new hook: the Leaftully Institute is already an established, recurring thread. A second Institute job, a chance meeting there, or Cordelia mentioning "Aldane" by name are all clean, low-effort ways back in.

## The future hook, banked, not due yet

Once the crew's done real survey work for her, flown Open Water, Open Season, and come back with usable data, Aurel may eventually want to go further, not just charting the symptom but actually investigating the Stilling itself, using the crew as the field team she's never had backing to field. **This doesn't need to come into play soon.** When it does, it's a natural escalation of a relationship already built on real trust, not a cold pitch. She has no idea yet how deep the mystery actually goes, House Vellum's role, the memory-current layer confirmed rather than theorized, any of it, she'd be investigating as a field scientist chasing a real pattern, same as always, not as someone already in on the secret. Whenever the Wyndham alias itself eventually comes apart in front of the crew, that's a separate, bigger beat worth deciding deliberately rather than letting slip early.

## Why an outside crew, not her own

The posting is exactly what it says: a chart-accuracy survey job (`sessions/Session 4 Prep.md` §5(C)). What she privately does with the returned data afterward, cross-checking the Edge's actual flow speed against her own years of position readings, stays entirely in her own head and never gets explained to the crew. Two real reasons she's sourcing this outside her own house rather than sending a Delverane survey ship to re-run it, now joined by a third:
- **Her own house's ships and staff are a visible, logged channel.** An unusual, precise re-survey run through official Delverane channels gets noticed, questioned, and possibly taken away from her or buried before she's ready. An independent crew's contract is one ordinary posting among dozens.
- **The crew has Orena.** Her own instrument-literate staff reads the water with tools. Orena reads it the way the water itself does, a qualitative sense no amount of in-house survey equipment produces.
- **She wants to be there in person, and only an outside crew lets her be there as someone else.** Sending a Delverane ship means staying behind at the office and waiting for a report. Booking passage on an independent charter under a different name means she gets to watch it happen, ask her own questions, and read the water herself, the thing she actually wants most.
- **Her own ship is simply too famous to use.** The *Farthing*, ranked 3rd on the Manifest Board, is a known, watched hull. Taking it anywhere near a hunch she isn't ready to explain would draw exactly the attention she's trying to avoid. An obscure, far-lower-ranked independent crew is the one kind of vessel nobody would ever think to connect to her.

**Keep this entirely GM-side.** The crew should finish the job believing it's exactly what it was sold as, and it genuinely is, from their end.

## Pell Varnish, the office proxy

Already established as a Farthing Station clerk who processes funding contracts (`factions/House Delverane.md`); this is where he earns a real scene. **Pell is who the crew actually deals with for anything involving Aurel's office in person**, the posting, the contract terms, and eventually the survey payout, never Aurel herself.

**Voice tic:** over-explains apologetically, sentences running long when he's flustered, which is often. "So if you'll just, sorry, one moment, it's the stamp again, it's always the stamp, give me just one second here."

**Physical tic:** sleeves perpetually pushed up and perpetually sliding back down, ink-cuffed from the contract stacks, a desk that looks like it's losing a slow war against paperwork.

**Want:** nothing ambitious. He'd just like the stack to stop growing before the day's actually over, once, even one time.

**If anyone asks where Aurel actually is**, play it tired and genuinely unbothered, not suspicious: *"She's not in today. Might not be in tomorrow either, hard to say with her some weeks. If I had to bet, she's off chasing one of her 'quick looks' again, and we'll get the full report whenever we get it. Always worth it when she does, mind. Just never on anyone's schedule but hers."* He has no idea there's anything to hide, because as far as he knows there isn't, this is just what working for Aurel Delverane is actually like.

## Payment, and the irony nobody but her is in on

At the end of Open Water, Open Season, **two separate payments land, not one.** The 3,100 gp survey fee comes through Pell, official, on the books, for the job as posted. Separately, whatever observation-stint fee covers this leg (following the same shape as session 4's 900 gp/16-day arrangement) comes through as "Wyndham's" passage payment, personal funds, off any Delverane ledger. **Aurel is quietly paying the crew twice, out of two different pockets that are both actually hers**, for the privilege of getting to do the same job once, in person, from both ends at once. Worth banking as a future gut-punch for Aerion specifically, if his ledger work or Reading the Ledgers ever traces both payments back to the same ultimate source, that's the moment the whole thing threatens to come apart, and it should be his discovery to make, not handed to him.

## What a rumor or research check turns up

Table-facing, available any time the crew wants context before or during Open Water, Open Season (Orena's Library Research, Aerion's business sense or Reading the Ledgers, straight dockside gossip, whatever fits the moment). **None of this touches the Wyndham connection.** A check on "Aurel Delverane" surfaces only her public, legitimate reputation; a check on "Wyndham Aldane" (if anyone thinks to run one) surfaces only the Institute-funded researcher cover, equally real as far as public record goes. Nothing links the two names short of an actual scene where the disguise fails.

- **DC 10, common knowledge:** she runs survey and acquisition for House Delverane personally out of the Farthing Station, hands-on rather than delegated, which working crews who deal with her directly respect and her family's own senior circle quietly finds a little embarrassing. Also common knowledge at this tier: she's a real wizard, not just a surveyor, and uses it the way anyone would expect a solo frontier operator to, wards, utility, staying alive out there alone. Nobody finds that remarkable on its own, it's just part of knowing who she is.
- **DC 15, house gossip:** the younger daughter of the Delverane line, passed over for the family's actual boardroom track, chose fieldwork instead, and the house has never quite forgiven the choice. A mild, real friction, surfaces at family functions more than business ones.
- **DC 20, known among surveyors and frontier crews specifically, not the houses:** she's personally charted more of the Verge-adjacent frontier than anyone else her age, including re-running a stretch alone after the survey ship that originally held it went missing out there. People who actually do this work respect her completely. People who sit in her family's boardrooms barely know her name.

## The mask slipping, a banked reveal beat

**If a real combat encounter during this voyage ever puts "Wyndham" in genuine personal danger, let her drop the act for exactly one turn.** Not a scripted certainty, a trigger: the moment an ordinary passenger would actually be in danger of dying, she ends the threat herself, decisively, with a single high-level spell, **Disintegrate** is the clean pick, no gore, the threat is simply gone, rather than a fight she visibly wins over several rounds. Then she's immediately, unnervingly composed again, as if it cost her nothing, because it didn't.

**Good candidates, never forced, GM's live call:** anything on the Windward Edge table that could plausibly reach a passenger directly, Thalassa's boarding raid (#6) or a Githyanki raiding party (#14) read well; the Longscar climax (#20) should stay the PCs' own fight and isn't the right spot for this, that's their session to win. Reserve this for a different, smaller real threat.

**What it should feel like at the table:** not a rescue, a *tell*. One beat of "that was not a passenger's reaction" before she's back to qualifying her sentences and taking her glasses on and off like nothing happened. A clean Insight check (DC 15) afterward reads that she's not even slightly rattled by something that should have rattled anyone, a real, concrete piece of "more than she's letting on" rather than vague unease.
