# Aurel Delverane

## The Surface Read
Thirty-one, younger daughter of a founding house that's never quite forgiven her for choosing fieldwork over a boardroom. Runs survey and acquisition out of the Farthing Station personally, hands dirty, not delegated.

**Voice tic:** narrates her own observations out loud, half to herself, half to whoever's nearby, a habit from working alone too long.

**Physical tic:** chart-ink on at least one finger, never quite washed off between legs.

**Want:** to be the one who's right about the Windward Edge's surf creeping inward before anyone senior takes it seriously.

**Standing canon:** she and the crew are neighbours and neither knows it yet, she works the Windward Edge, they work the Verge. Flagged early as the single most likely person in Wildspace to become a real ally. Open Water, Open Season (`sessions/Session 4 Prep.md` §5(C), the session 4 companion's tab panel-c, `sessions/Session 5 Prep.md` §4) is her survey job and her first real on-screen scene, still picked but not yet flown as of session 5.

## What she actually knows

A working field surveyor, not a theorist. She has real, repeated data on the Windward Edge's standing surf creeping inward, a visible, local symptom of the Stilling, though she doesn't have the mechanism and isn't chasing one. What she has is pattern: specific stretches of chart that used to be reliable and aren't anymore, routes her own office has quietly stopped recommending without officially delisting them. She's noticed what everyone at her level notices and nobody senior wants written down.

## The future hook, banked, not due yet

Once the crew's done real survey work for her, flown Open Water, Open Season, and come back with usable data, Aurel may eventually want to go further: not just charting the symptom but actually investigating the Stilling itself, using the crew as the field team she's never had backing to field. **This doesn't need to come into play soon.** The players may want more small contracts first, or the International Space Dog Show thread, or anything else entirely, and that's fine, this sits patiently until it's wanted. When it does come up, it's a natural escalation of a relationship already built on real trust, not a cold pitch. Worth remembering she has no idea yet how deep the mystery actually goes, House Vellum's role, the memory-current layer, any of it, she'd be investigating as a field scientist chasing a real pattern, same as always, not as someone already in on the secret.

## Loose threads, not yet decided

- Whether she ever learns about House Vellum's own forty-year cover-up, and if so, how she reacts, a career field surveyor finding out the houses have known and sat on it would likely land hard.
- Whether her own house connections ever become relevant (founding house, younger daughter, never specified further).
- The derelict *Quiet Verge*'s navigation instrument (`sessions/Session 5 Prep.md` §2) is a real, sellable item to her specifically, a built-in reason to actually see her office again regardless of whether Open Water, Open Season gets flown first.
