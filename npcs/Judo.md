# Judo

## The Surface Read
Hadozee scout-gunner, hired session 4, barely two days off a washout when he interviewed. Eager, young, glide membranes he hasn't quite grown into yet. Claims he can hold three guns at once; nobody's tested it under fire.

**Voice tic:** narrates his own mistakes out loud before anyone else can call them out, pre-deflecting criticism. **Physical tic:** flexes and stretches his glide membranes when nervous, not unlike cracking knuckles.

## The washout
Washed out of a **Stoneward-sponsored Route Warden trainee program**, over a reckless-flying incident. Nobody was badly hurt. It was bad enough to end his placement anyway.

## Motive and ambition
Redemption, concretely: one real mission he knows was flawless, not just gets told was fine.

## Weakness
Overcorrects into caution after the washout, hesitates when boldness is actually what's needed, afraid of repeating the exact mistake that cost him his placement.

## Strength
Sharp eyes, a genuinely gifted gunner, quick reflexes once he actually commits to a move.

## Want
To stop needing anyone else's permission to believe he's good at this.

## Sidekick build, Warrior (level 6)
*[Reconstructed from the 2018 Unearthed Arcana Sidekicks playtest, not the final published text. Verify against the book before it matters at the table.]*

**Why Warrior:** he is the crew's ranged gunner. The old Archery style (+2 to hit) is homebrew and was dropped, since the UA Warrior has no fighting style.

- **Base:** Scout chassis (3d8) plus six sidekick levels. Proficiency +3.
- **AC** 16 (studded leather, DEX +4) · **HP** 49 (9d8 + 9) · **Speed** 30 ft. · **Initiative** +4
- **Scores:** STR 10 (+0), DEX 18 (+4), CON 12 (+1), INT 11 (+0), WIS 13 (+1), CHA 10 (+0). Level 4 and 6 ASIs took DEX +2 each.
- **Saves:** DEX +7 (sidekick save proficiency).
- **Skills:** Perception +4 (passive 14), Acrobatics +7, Survival +4. Proficient with all armor, shields, and weapons.
- **Hadozee:** glide membranes and Hadozee Dodge (reaction, reduce damage taken by 1d6 + proficiency). *Verify the species text against the Astral Adventurer's Guide.*
- **Second Wind:** bonus action, regain 1d10 + 6 HP. Once per short or long rest.
- **Danger Sense:** advantage on DEX saves against effects he can see.
- **Improved Critical:** crits on 19 or 20.
- **Extra Attack:** two attacks with the Attack action. It replaces the Scout's Multiattack and does not stack with it.
- **Attack:** Pistol (DMG), +7 to hit, range 30/90, 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing.
