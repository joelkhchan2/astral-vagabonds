# Judo

## The Surface Read
Hadozee scout-gunner, hired session 4, barely two days off a washout when he interviewed. Eager, young, glide membranes he hasn't quite grown into yet. Claims he can hold three guns at once; nobody's tested it under fire.

**Voice tic:** narrates his own mistakes out loud before anyone else can call them out, pre-deflecting criticism. **Physical tic:** flexes and stretches his glide membranes when nervous, not unlike cracking knuckles.

## The washout
Washed out of a **Stoneward-sponsored Route Warden trainee program**, over a reckless-flying incident. Nobody was badly hurt. It was bad enough to end his placement anyway.

## Motive and ambition
Redemption, concretely: one real mission he knows was flawless, not just gets told was fine.

## Weakness
Overcorrects into caution after the washout, hesitates when boldness is actually what's needed, afraid of repeating the exact mistake that cost him his placement.

## Strength
Sharp eyes, a genuinely gifted gunner, quick reflexes once he actually commits to a move.

## Want
To stop needing anyone else's permission to believe he's good at this.

## Sidekick build, Warrior (level 5, approximate, verify against the Essentials Kit before table use)
- Proficiency +3, HP ~44, AC 15.
- **Extra Attack:** two attacks with the Attack action.
- **Fighting Style:** Archery, reflavored Gunnery, +2 to hit with ranged weapon attacks.
