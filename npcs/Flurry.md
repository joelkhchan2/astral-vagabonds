# Flurry -- Aerion's silver dragon

> Aerion's baby silver dragon: comic relief, the crew's shared child, and -- quietly -- a small piece of the living sky that loves him. Stat block in `rules/Stat Blocks.md`. **Non-combat, a baby, PG. That never changes.** She's Priscilla's, so the big beats below (the heritage reveal, the grown phasing moment, the final-voyage role) get floated to Priscilla before they land at the table.

## The basics (canon)

A young silver dragon Aerion found **as an egg on a treasure hunt with this crew ~4.5 years ago**, in a hoard they turned up early (before the outfit's luck ran out). Now ~2 years old -- "a puppy with a breath weapon." He reluctantly, then secretly devotedly, took her on; he dotes, dresses her up, and denies all of it. She **will not fight** (hard rule -- see the stat block's *Devoted* clause) and is frightened of loud things. Cold breath, silver scale, understands Common, speaks Draconic.

## The heritage (GM canon -- latent, revealed slowly)

**One of Flurry's parents or ancestors was a lunar astral dragon.** She reads as an ordinary silver, but the bloodline is part-astral, and that is the quiet engine under everything interesting about her.

Why lunar fits so cleanly it barely needs explaining:
- **Cold to cold.** Silver dragons breathe cold; **lunar astral dragons** (`references/Astral Sea Bestiary.md`) breathe cold and immobilize. Same element, no seam.
- **Same palette.** Silvers are creatures of sky, air, and silver-white scale; lunar dragons drift by cold moons and ice-locked asteroids, pale and phasing. She looks like both because she *is* both.
- **Astral-native blood.** Lunar dragons phase in and out of reality and are at home in Wildspace and the Astral Sea. That is where her astral-sense comes from -- inherited, not learned.

**What the heritage explains, all at once:**
1. **Her astral-sense (the barometer -- see below) is native, and sharper than any ordinary silver's.** She feels the Tide and the Guttering because lunar blood reads the living currents by instinct -- she's a tide-gauge, feeling the water thin or go still before any instrument.
2. **Why a silver egg was out in Wildspace at all.** Silvers are a Material-Plane species; this line half-belongs to the void, so the egg being *out here* is the first thread of her origin, not a plot hole.
3. **A latent phasing tell.** Frightened or deep asleep, baby Flurry sometimes half-*phases* -- goes briefly translucent, hard to focus on, there-and-not. Harmless now, played for uncanny charm. Grown, it becomes a real trait and a late-game payoff.

**Deliberately left open** (do not decide until the campaign asks): *which* ancestor (a parent one generation back, or further up the line) and *whose hoard* the egg was in, and how it got there. The heritage is locked; the specifics are a thread you can cash into the main plot later (a guttered system, one of Estra's lost routes, an ancient wonder) or keep as warm mystery.

## The barometer -- Flurry as a GM tool

**She is a barometer, not an oracle.** She gives you a direction to point the players' attention; she never hands them the answer. She's an animal attuned to the *texture of the sky* -- the way a real animal senses a coming storm or a tremor before the ground moves -- and she can't tell you why. The crew spends Tier 1 assuming she's just being a dramatic baby, and slowly realizes she is **right every time.** That "the dragon knew first" turn is the whole payoff; don't rush it.

**Tells you can deploy:**
- **Guttering nearby / a route dying:** she goes still and small, hides in Aerion's coat, won't eat. A happy dog gone quiet -- the eerie version.
- **Kindori song / the sky at its healthiest:** she lights up -- chirps, presses to the viewport, breath-frost on the glass. A bridge to Orena: the dragon and the navigator both feel the good sky, one by blood, one by ear.
- **Something astral aboard (an invisible drifter, the Dreameater):** the fixed stare, hackles up, tracking a thing no one else can see. (Already used -- `sessions/Episode - The Dreameater.md`.)
- **Fear / the truly wrong:** the half-phase. When even Flurry flickers out, the table should feel it.
- **The Tide returning / a region relit (late game):** she's the first to *know it worked* -- joy before the instruments confirm it. A hopium beat.

Use her sparingly and she stays special. "Flurry won't come out of the stable and nobody knows why" is a chill, not a solution.

## Thematic role -- why she matters to the story

**Flurry is Aerion's mirror, twice over.**
- **Belonging.** Aerion is an astral elf who feels he doesn't belong to his own family, raising a dragon who half-belongs to the sky itself. Two beings of uncertain belonging who chose each other -- found family made literal.
- **Ownership.** His wound is being treated as a *line item*, an asset his family owns and doesn't see. To everyone else in Wildspace, a silver dragon is a priceless asset -- people will look at Flurry exactly the way his father looks at him. His refusal to let her be a possession is him practicing, on her, the freedom he's fighting to win for himself.
- **Lunar, specifically, mirrors his shadow-king nature.** The moon rules by reflected light -- cold, quiet, influence without the blaze. That's Aerion (contrast a solar dragon's open fire). His dragon is the same kind of power he is.

And she's the crew's shared child: the **outfits gag** (`npcs/Crew Comedy Bits.md`) is the found-family thesis in one bit -- Aerion secretly knits her into scarves and bows, and the crew's act of love is to *never mention it.*

## Growth and the late game

She scales with the crew: wyrmling now, potentially *young* by the campaign's end -- power that visibly tracks the party's own climb. Keep the non-combat rule locked the whole way; the built-in payoff is the stat block's *"someone hers is about to die"* clause -- spend it **once, late,** at maximum earned weight (the baby the whole crew protected finally, terribly, protects them). Silver dragons can take humanoid form when grown, so a very-late beat where Flurry briefly speaks or shifts is available if you ever want the gut-punch. Fully optional, and very late.

**The image the campaign is building toward:** the final voyage into a guttered dark region (see `campaign/Potential Endings.md` B/D), with a grown, lunar-blooded Flurry at Orena's side -- the dragon reading the living roads by inherited instinct while the navigator reads them by ear. The pet who couldn't fight, revealed as a small living piece of the thing the whole campaign was about.

## Guardrails

- **Non-combat, a baby, PG -- always.** The heritage expresses as *sensitivity and mystery,* not power; she gets more attuned, never scarier. No dragon-in-peril horror, no killing her for pathos -- the tone won't take it (`campaign/Story Spine.md`).
- **She's Priscilla's.** Float the heritage reveal, the grown/phasing moment, and any "someone wants the dragon" plot to Priscilla first -- it's really Aerion's story.
- **Barometer, not oracle.** She points; the players solve. Never let Flurry hand them a conclusion.
