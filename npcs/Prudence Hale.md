# Prudence Hale

## The Surface Read
Tall astral elf, stylus in one hand, an abacus in the other that she fiddles with out of habit whether or not she's actually calculating anything. Senior underwriter of House Nightbreeze, badge number 9068, offers it unprompted before anyone asks. Polished, direct, and entirely unbothered by being told no.

**Voice tic:** states her business plainly and names her own motive before anyone can accuse her of hiding it. *"I am a person who likes to hedge my risks, so to speak."* *"My motives are for the interest of House Nightbreeze. I'll be working looking forward to working with you."*

**Physical tic:** the abacus, worked constantly, a visible tell that she's always pricing something, including the conversation she's currently having.

**Her want:** whatever Great-Aunt Veshara's actuaries have priced House Vellum's exposure at, and she is personally assigned to close that gap. Junior underwriters at Nightbreeze gossip about why she got sent on a job this small, correctly sensing it's bigger than it looks (`factions/House Nightbreeze.md`).

## Session 5: the offer, and the family wrinkle
Approached Aerion and Orena directly at the Float Market and offered a signed check for **20,000 gp** for anything they had on House Vellum from Reefside, no questions about how they got it. Named, correctly, that the crew had been tailed. Aerion and Orena told her the evidence had blown up and declined, convincingly enough (a 24 against her Insight) that she concluded Estra plays things closer to the chest than she'd assumed, rather than that she'd been lied to outright. Reported to Estra afterward; the standing offer is withdrawn once declined, not evergreen.

**The family wrinkle, corrected:** she is not an ex-wife of Vaelren's. She's been married four times across three different men, divorced from every one of them, and one of those three was a man of House Nightbreeze itself, which is what makes her something like an ex-cousin-in-law to the Windsongs by marriage, not blood, and a known, wary quantity to Vaelren specifically rather than a stranger. Found later that same night at the Compass Rose having a drink with him, easy and familiar in a way that reads as old history rather than a chance meeting.

**Insight reads her as inscrutable.** Orena tried to read her directly and came away with nothing; Prudence turned it back around unprompted: *"I see right through you, Arena... Doubt. Doubt of yourself."* She isn't bluffing when she says this. Whatever she's actually after, she's good enough at her job that a flat read on her is the correct read, not a failure.

## Playing her
- Never a mustache-twirler. She's polite, patient, and will respect a no without posturing: *"I will respect your decision. Good day."* The threat is in how much she already knows, not in pressure.
- She answers to Great-Aunt Veshara Nightbreeze, not to Aerynna (`factions/House Nightbreeze.md`). Her offers are the house's actual hedge against House Vellum's possible fall, not freelance ambition.
- Recurring now, not a one-scene guest. Clean ways back in: a second offer once the stakes have changed, a scene with Vaelren that reveals more of their history, or just her reappearing anywhere Nightbreeze business and the crew's own happen to overlap.
