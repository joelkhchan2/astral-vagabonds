# Orena (Gabrielle's PC)

**Race:** Water Genasi · **Class:** Bard/Paladin, level 5 · **Age:** ~30 · **From:** Leilon, on the Sword Coast (Material Plane), between Neverwinter and Waterdeep.
**Shipboard roles:** Navigator, Helmsman-in-training, Scout.

> Name spelling: the DM once wrote "Irena," but every primary source (Gabrielle's own notes and the session transcript) uses **Orena**, so that is what this folder uses. Flag if it should be Irena and it is a one-word global swap.

## Who she is
A water genasi from the coastal city of Leilon. Back home she grew up among constant travelers and their tales, and she started a small six-person guild of childhood friends, the **Wayfinder Knot**. She is the navigator who wants to become a helmsman, and she is the newest aboard this crew.

## The lost crew (her hook)

> **Full detail, GM-side truth, and the survivors' current fate: `npcs/The Wayfinder Knot.md`.** What follows is what Orena herself believes and has told people; the fuller picture (including that four of the five are alive, together, and doing well) is hers to eventually learn, not hers to know now.

The Knot went to sea chasing the **Stillwater Vocalise**, a legend sailors already told each other, a song said to rise from a patch of dead-still water on certain nights. Orena charted where the stories placed it. They found their still night, windless, silent, exactly like the tales promised, and the song was real. It grew clearer as the calm deepened, and then it pulled, not stilled: her whole crew and their ship went, all at once, somewhere she couldn't follow. She was at the rail when it happened, already half over it trying to place the sound, and the water took her a different way than it took the ship. She was the navigator; they were lost on her watch. She does not know whether they are dead, scattered, or something else, and she means to find out.

**She believes it's because she's water-touched, that her genasi blood is what let the water carry her clear instead of taking her with the rest.** She has never been able to prove this and never will be able to on her own; it's the only explanation she has, and she has built a great deal of her sense of purpose on it being true. Whether it actually mattered, or whether she simply happened to be closer to open water when the current split, is not something to confirm or deny at the table until it earns a real answer.

**Why she can't just go back and look.** She's already tried, in the most literal sense available to her: retracing the water near where it happened. It taught her two things, both dead ends. First, whatever thinned there wasn't a fixed door, it was tied to something that had its own ending, and by the time she was steady enough to go looking, there was nothing left to find. Second, it was open ocean at night with no landmarks; she never had precise enough bearings to return to in the first place, even setting the first problem aside. That double failure is what turned her search from "go back to the spot" into something else entirely: an old rumor that the lost are sometimes found not by returning to where they vanished, but by learning to read the place they vanished *into*. That rumor is the actual reason she sought out passage into Wildspace and the Astral Sea at all, this ship and this crew are the continuation of that search, not a detour from it.

She was picked up by Aerion's ship not long after arriving in Wildspace, about **6 months ago**, when he re-met her and got her the job. She has no leads yet worth chasing, only the growing conviction that the skill this crew keeps asking of her, reading the living sky rather than a chart, is the same skill she'll eventually need to find them.

## Downtime
Mapping where the Stillwater Vocalise, the other artifacts of the tales she has heard, and above all her lost crew might be.

## How she knows Aerion
As teenagers they both trained under Aerion's uncle, the "space samurai" **Vaelren Windsong**. Orena wanted to learn the sword. A traveler's tale of the famous space samurai and his camp drew her there; she met Aerion, they were made to share a bunk, and they became lifelong friends. Years later, after Aerion's ship blew up and he needed a new one, they re-met and he recruited her.

## Notes
- She is training on the helm, so the ship always has a second pilot besides Captain Vane. Piloting requires an attuned spellcaster holding concentration, which her Bard/Paladin build supports.
- Whether her being "of the water" is why the Vocalise didn't take her with the rest is genuinely open, not settled fact, see "The lost crew" above. Worth keeping in mind for water- and sound-themed encounters either way.
