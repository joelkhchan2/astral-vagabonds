# Tomlin Underhill

## The Surface Read
Lightfoot halfling, hired session 4 on a nat-20 shepherd's pie. Warm, instantly likeable, carries a **Bag of Holding that's nothing but spices and seasonings**, and shares from it freely with anyone who asks. Family to Maude "Mod" Underhill, calls her Grandmama without a hint of irony, tangled up somewhere in five hundred years of family tree that implies. **The crew didn't know any of that when they hired him**, they hired the pie.

**Voice tic:** offers food or a taste of something as a greeting, even mid-crisis. **Physical tic:** always has a pinch of some spice between two fingers, testing it against his tongue or the air before he commits to a thought.

## Motive and ambition
Wants to become a genuinely great cook in his own right, independent of the family name entirely. The *Night Fury* is where he actually earns it, not a stepping stone to somewhere grander. Someday, maybe, a real kitchen of his own, but that's a long way off and he knows it.

## Weakness
Overgenerous to a fault. Gives food and spices away even when it costs him, hates conflict, will avoid a confrontation rather than have one, even when having it would serve him better.

## Strength
Real warmth that wins people over fast, and he's genuinely skilled, not a joke replacement for Brass, an actual upgrade in the galley. The bag's spice stock is also just useful, barter, morale, a gift that costs him nothing to give and means something to receive.

## Want
To be loved for the cooking itself, not the name behind it.

## With Brass
She stepped down gratefully and permanently once he landed the job, by her own account, relieved. What's less said out loud: she still hangs around the galley "supervising" even though she isn't cooking anymore, possessive of the space if not the job. The real torch-passing moment is hers to have, not his to force, watching someone genuinely good take the job she loved being bad at.

## Sidekick build, Expert (level 5, approximate, verify against the Essentials Kit before table use)
- Proficiency +3, HP ~27, AC 13.
- **Expertise:** Persuasion, cook's utensils.
- **Jack of All Trades:** adds half proficiency to ability checks he isn't otherwise proficient in.
- **Helpful:** can take the Help action as a bonus action.
