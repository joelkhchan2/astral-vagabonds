# Wyndham Aldane

## The Surface Read
Tall, human, long red hair, glasses she takes off rather than puts on when she's actually paying attention to someone instead of performing polite interest. A dusty navy-blue cloak that's seen real travel, not a costume. Posted premise: an independent study of crew dynamics, pure observation, no agenda. Paid 900 gp for a 16-day stint aboard the *Night Fury* (session 4, off-screen, background income), which means she's already had two full weeks of real, unscripted access to this crew. The table's own read at the time: not dishonest, but more than she's saying.

**Voice tic:** talks like she's always mid-footnote, qualifying her own sentences before anyone else can get to it first. "Which is to say, probably, though I'd want to check that." "Not that it means anything on its own, obviously."

**Physical tic:** glasses off, not on, when she's actually listening. On, she's being polite. Off, she's paying attention.

**Her want, plainly:** she needs real, usable field data before her funding runs out. This is not performance, it's genuinely close to true.

## What she's actually studying

**The spine of it: memory, encoded in the Tide.** Aldane is a real, serious scholar of the Astral Sea's stranger half, the theory that the living current is partly made of accumulated thought and memory, that kindori carry memory as much as nutrients, that a "trail worn by enough minds passing through" is not just a metaphor for a current but close to literally what one is (`campaign/Story Spine.md`, the GM-only deeper truth, Tier 3+). This is fringe even among people who take wildspace fauna seriously. She did not invent the idea, but she's one of the only people trying to build a rigorous case for it instead of leaving it as sailors' superstition.

**Why she cares about this crew, and about Estra specifically.** The posted premise, crew dynamics, isn't a total lie, it's cover that happens to be true enough to hold up. What she's actually here for is Estra. A captain who ran the first real fleet to attempt the nomadic method, fifteen years of lived migration and current data, charted by hand before anyone had a working theory, is exactly the kind of field evidence Aldane's thesis needs and cannot get anywhere else (`npcs/Captain Estra Vane.md`, the Locked Chest). Aldane doesn't know the chest exists. She knows the *Commodore* rumor, same as the rest of the Bral Reach, and she's patient enough to have booked a 16-day ride on nothing but a hunch that the rumor has more underneath it.

**The Vellum question, deliberately unresolved.** Aldane is "more than she's saying" in one more direction, and which one is a live decision, not locked:
- **Investigator (current lean).** She's independently chasing the same cover-up the crew already has a thread on, forty years of House Vellum quietly managing the Stilling's paper trail (the Marsh letters, the Amberwake file, `campaign/Story Spine.md`). If this is true, she and the crew are, without either side knowing it yet, working the same case from different ends.
- **Plant.** She's quietly feeding what she learns to a Vellum handler who wants to know who else is sniffing around what the house has spent decades burying. If this is true, her charm is real but her reports are also real, and somebody at Vellum is reading them.
Leave this open at the table. Decide live, whenever it actually matters to a scene, not in advance.

## Who's paying for this

**The Leaftully Institute, specifically Cordelia Leaftully.** Not the whole family, whose older, more respectable branches already consider wildspace fauna study beneath them (`sessions/Session 3 Prep.md`). Cordelia backs Aldane's even more eccentric memory-current thesis quietly, out of her own discretionary budget, partly out of real professional curiosity (kindori song as a memory signal would validate and deepen her own fauna research, not just Aldane's) and partly because she knows exactly what it is to be the family's embarrassing branch and has some sympathy to spare for someone further out on the same limb. **This is not secure funding.** If the rest of the Leaftully family ever gets wind of what Cordelia's actually spending money on, it ends, for both of them. Aldane's real deadline is Cordelia's own standing, not a calendar.

This also means Aldane's eventual return doesn't need a new hook: the Leaftully Institute is already an established, recurring thread (`sessions/Session 3 Prep.md` flags Cordelia herself as "a real client... a loose thread worth a decision later"). A second Institute job, a chance meeting at the Institute itself, or Cordelia mentioning Aldane by name are all clean, low-effort ways back in.

## Playing her

- She's genuinely likeable, not a mustache-twirler. The crew dynamics cover isn't a lie, she really did spend two weeks watching how this crew works, and she liked what she saw.
- She asks real questions and listens to the answers, which is most of why the table's own Insight read landed as "not dishonest" rather than "suspicious." The gap is what she doesn't ask, not what she says.
- If directly confronted about Estra, she won't lie outright, she'll qualify, footnote, and redirect, the voice tic doing real work here, buying time rather than ever quite answering.
- Don't resolve the Vellum question for her in advance. Let a scene decide it.
