# Wyndham Aldane

**Wyndham Aldane is Aurel Delverane, in person, under a disguise.** Full identity, true motive, the mechanics of how the alias holds up, and her stat block all live at `npcs/Aurel Delverane.md`. This file is the performance layer, how to actually run "Wyndham" at the table for as long as the disguise holds.

## The Surface Read, as played
Tall, human, long red hair, glasses she takes off rather than puts on when she's actually paying attention to someone instead of performing polite interest. A dusty navy-blue cloak that's seen real travel, not a costume. Posted premise: an independent study of crew dynamics, pure observation, no agenda. Paid 900 gp for a 16-day stint aboard the *Night Fury* (session 4, off-screen, background income), which means she'd already had two full weeks of real, unscripted access to this crew before session 5. The table's own read at the time: not dishonest, but more than she's saying.

**Session 5: boarded again, on-screen this time, for the Windward Edge leg.** Settled into her designated quarters without friction, polite about the areas marked off-limits. The active observation stint started for real that session, not off-screen background anymore. Session 6 continues it, a few days in now, not a first sighting.

**Voice tic:** talks like she's always mid-footnote, qualifying her own sentences before anyone else can get to it first. "Which is to say, probably, though I'd want to check that." "Not that it means anything on its own, obviously." A deliberate performance, not how she talks as herself.

**Physical tic:** glasses off, not on, when she's actually listening. On, she's being polite. Off, she's paying attention. **One tic survives the disguise and isn't a performance at all:** chart-ink on at least one finger, never quite washed off, the one thing that's actually her and not Wyndham. A real clue if anyone ever looks closely at her hands.

**Her want, as played:** she needs real, usable field data before her funding runs out. This isn't a total lie, it's genuinely close to true, just not the whole truth of why she's here.

## Playing her

- She's genuinely likeable, not a mustache-twirler. The crew dynamics cover isn't a lie, she really did spend two weeks watching how this crew works, and she liked what she saw.
- She asks real questions and listens to the answers, which is most of why the table's own Insight read landed as "not dishonest" rather than "suspicious." The gap is what she doesn't ask, not what she says.
- If directly confronted about Estra, she won't lie outright, she'll qualify, footnote, and redirect, the voice tic doing real work here, buying time rather than ever quite answering.
- **The Vellum question is resolved, decidedly no.** She isn't a plant and isn't working with anyone else on this. Don't play her as if she might be reporting to a handler, that thread is closed.
- During anything genuinely dangerous (the void scavver fight, for one), her composure can read as slightly too calm for a passenger who's never seen one of these before. That's real, she's a capable wizard with real defenses if it comes to that, not a tell to overplay, just a seed.
