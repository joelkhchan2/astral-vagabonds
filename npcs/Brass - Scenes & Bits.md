# Brass: Scenes & Bits

A drawer of ready-to-run moments for Brassica "Brass" Thorngage: medical, food, and character. Pull one when a session needs warmth, a crew B-plot, or a slow step along her arc. Nothing here is required and none of it runs in order, except the arc ladder in section 4, which should climb slowly.

Who she is and her secret: `Brass.md`. Cooking and Gut Check mechanics: `The Galley.md`. Stat block: `Stat Blocks.md`. Running gags: `Crew Comedy Bits.md`.

**Playing her, the short version:**
- **Voice tic:** answers the question you should have asked. "Your arm's fine. You're dehydrated. Drink this."
- **Physical tic:** clocks the exits when she walks in. Writes on a napkin mid-conversation and pockets it.
- **Want:** to be needed, so nobody ever decides she's optional.
- **Rule of thumb:** she's fast and funny by daylight. The real Brass shows up at 0300, in sickbay with the door shut, or when someone else is hurt.

**What's canon so far (sessions 1 and 2):**
- Octopus night, a natural 20, loved by everyone. Aerion offered to fund future octopus nights.
- Orena bought Brass a food basket as a gift. Brass hugged her, for real.
- Brass's stated goal: get Aerion to finish three of her meals (Operation Clean Plate, 0 of 3). The yogurt parfait is next.
- At Bral, Brass traded a nod with someone in the shadows by a streetlight. Unnamed. Keep it unspent until you want it.
- Gary "Sugary" of the Burnouts, a sushi and hibachi chef, was floated at the table as her sous-chef during the failed recruitment, but **Brass has not met or interacted with Gary yet.** She wasn't at the alley. The Burnouts are rivals of the crew; any history between Brass and Gary starts with their first scene together.

## 1. Medical moments

**1.1 The diagnosis at a glance.** *Use: any port scene, as a clue-delivery device.*
Mid-conversation with a stranger (a dockhand, a factor, a rival captain), Brass cuts in with a diagnosis nobody asked for. It's right. It's also information: a sleepless clerk, a captain hiding a limp, a guard who's been drinking on shift.
- **Brass:** "Sorry. Go on. You've got a cracked rib on the left, by the way. You keep turning your whole body to look at people."
- **Use it for clues:** the tell she spots is something the PCs can pull on (why is the Factor's hand shaking; who did that to the dockhand).
- **Offer:** either PC can follow up on what she saw, or ignore it. She won't push.

**1.2 Triage under fire.** *Use: after any real fight, or mid-fight when a crew member drops.*
The comedy switches off. Brass goes quiet, fast, and certain. She gives orders to people who outrank her and nobody questions it.
- **Read-aloud (when someone goes down):** The noise of the fight goes muffled for a second, because Brass is suddenly beside you. You smell iron and the sharp green of whatever's in her satchel. Her hands are already working. "Stay with me. Tell me something boring."
- **Mechanics:** she'll burn *healing word* and Preserve Life without hesitating, which gives her **disadvantage on tomorrow's Galley Roll** (`The Galley.md`). Let the table notice the cost.
- **After:** she shakes, once, and only when she thinks no one is looking. If a PC sees it, that's theirs to act on.

**1.3 The checkup.** *Use: a quiet day aboard, or after a PC takes a bad hit.*
Brass insists on a proper examination. She's thorough, blunt, and weirdly tender about it.
- **For Orena:** she notices Orena isn't sleeping enough (the viewport, the maps). "You listen out there all night, you'll hear things. Some of them won't be real. Some of them will. Either way, sleep." Whether Orena tells her what she hears is Gabrielle's call.
- **For Aerion:** she notices he's underweight. **This is the reveal under Operation Clean Plate:** it was never just a bit. "It's not a bet. It's a prescription. I just made it funny so you'd let me." Save this one until the running joke has had a few sessions to breathe. How he takes it is Priscilla's.
- **For Flurry:** Brass is not a vet. She does it anyway, very seriously, with a stethoscope that makes Flurry giggle-hiss. "Heart's strong. Lungs are... cold. Is that normal? Nobody tell me, I don't want to know."

**1.4 The case she can't fix.** *Use: a port job, or a stranger brought aboard. Moves the license thread.*
A patient needs something beyond her spells: a restricted remedy only a licensed physician can sign for, or a procedure a port infirmary won't let her perform. She knows exactly what to do. She isn't allowed.
- **The ask:** the infirmary clerk wants a license number. Brass says a fake one without blinking, and it doesn't work.
- **Solution paths:** find a licensed physician who'll sign (Dr. Quill Ashworth, the surgeon the crew didn't hire at the Yard, is still in the world); forge the paperwork (Finny has opinions); pay someone off (Aerion's ledger); or Brass does it off-book in the crew's own sickbay and takes the risk.
- **What it seeds:** the first crack in "I don't want the license." She wanted it for this.
- **Brass:** "Paper. It's always paper. I could do this in my sleep and some clerk with ink on his cuffs says no."

**1.5 The Revivify scroll.** *Use: any quiet moment after session 2.*
Orena bought a *revivify* scroll (475 gp). Brass finds out, asks to see it, and inspects it like a jeweler.
- **Brass:** "Who sold you this? No, it's good. It's real. I'm just asking so I know who to trust next time." Then, quieter: "You've got about a minute with this thing. One minute. You want me to walk you through it?"
- **Offer:** a short lesson, if Orena wants one. Brass treats it like the most important minute of anyone's life, because it will be.

**1.6 Brass the patient.** *Use: after a fight where she went down, or a port fever. Pairs with the Galley Coup in section 5.*
Brass gets sick or hurt, and has to be treated by someone else. She is the worst patient aboard. She diagnoses her own care out loud and critiques every bandage.
- **Brass (from the cot):** "That's too tight. No, don't loosen it, it's supposed to be too tight. I'm just telling you I noticed."
- **The real beat:** being needed is how she stays safe. Needing someone is terrifying. If a crew member sits with her anyway, she runs out of jokes around the second hour.

**1.7 Kraken.** *Use: a Burnouts rematch, or a port where their paths cross.*
Ken "Kraken" of the Burnouts is a lapsed former doctor, now fighting as a rigger. Brass never finished her license; Ken lost his. After a scrap, Brass patches up the Burnouts too, because she always does, and ends up doing it beside Ken.
- **Ken:** "You're good. Where'd you train?" **Brass:** "Here and there." **Ken:** "Yeah. Me too, eventually."
- **What it offers:** a mirror. He's who she'd be if she stopped running and it went wrong anyway. They don't become friends. They might become something like colleagues.

## 2. Food moments

**2.1 The cook-off: Brass vs. Sugary.** *Use: any port with a market or festival. Keeps the Burnouts rivalry warm and funny. Doubles as their first meeting.*
Brass and Gary "Sugary" have never met. The crew knows him; she only knows he's the one who baked the bait cookies, which offends her professionally. They cross paths at a market and one challenges the other over something small (who fed the dockhands better; a comment about her risotto; those cookies). A market stall owner offers a judging table and a 50 gp pot.
- **Format:** three rounds (breakfast, street food, a dessert). Each round, both cooks roll a d20; high roll wins, best two of three. **Gary rolls with advantage** (trained showman, knives flipping, fire).
- **Crew involvement:** anyone who helps Brass (sous-chef, working the judges, a little sabotage) gives her advantage for that round, which cancels Gary's. Get caught sabotaging and Brass forfeits the round, furious. Boy Boy McGee plays the same games for Gary.
- **Every NPC gets a beat:** Finny runs the book on it. Oz volunteers as a judge and tries to be scrupulously fair, which makes both cooks hate him. Tack judges by sensor. Estra eats Gary's food, says "adequate," and walks off.
- **Gary:** "Your knife work's sloppy." **Brass:** "My knife work's for stitches, pal."
- **After:** whatever the result, they swap one recipe. Neither admits it.

**2.2 Feeding a crowd.** *Use: a job with refugees, a starving colony, a stranded crew, a wreck's survivors.*
Brass cooks for forty people on almost nothing, and it's the best thing she's ever made. She's quick and unbothered and has clearly done this before.
- **The tell:** she knows exactly how much grain feeds how many for how long, without doing math. Aerion might notice she beat his numbers.
- **Seed (optional, GM only):** she learned this the night she cut the neogi hold loose. She had to feed the people she freed while they ran. It's the reason she hates being called a good cook. Don't explain it. Let the table notice she goes quiet when someone thanks her.
- **Brass:** "Don't thank me. Pass the bowls down. Kids first, then the ones who won't ask."

**2.3 Octopus night returns.** *Use: any port with a fish market.*
A stall has octopus. The price is steep (8 gp, fresh). Brass sees it, looks at Aerion, and says nothing, pointedly.
- **Offer:** Aerion's earlier offer to fund octopus nights is his to honor or not. The ledger is his.
- **If it happens:** Hall of Fame dish, so the Galley Roll has advantage (`The Galley.md`). If it comes out anything less than a Classic, Brass takes it harder than any injury this campaign.

**2.4 A taste of Leilon.** *Use: a quiet evening aboard. Orena's spotlight, with a chance to fail warmly.*
Brass asks Orena what they ate back home in Leilon, or what the Wayfinder Knot cooked on their ship. If Orena wants to, she can teach Brass the dish, or cook it herself.
- **Mechanics:** if Orena cooks, she rolls d20 + Wisdom on the Galley table (`The Galley.md`). A fail is part of the scene. Brass eats it anyway and rates it honestly.
- **What's underneath:** a guild meal is a family memory. Brass knows the Knot is gone, and knows food is how you keep people. She won't say that. She'll just write the recipe on a napkin and put it in the pocket where she keeps the important ones.
- **Brass:** "Say it again, slower. How much salt? No, how much did *they* use."

**2.5 The mystery ingredient.** *Use: after a port visit, a derelict salvage, or a trade with a strange vessel.*
Brass acquires something no one can identify: a fruit that hums, a jar of blue grain, a slab of meat from something that lives in the Astral. She intends to cook it.
- **Tack runs a sensor pass:** "Edible. Probably. I'd put it at seventy percent."
- **Solutions:** Nature or Arcana DC 13 to identify; Flurry's canary test (she eats it, or she doesn't); or just cook it and call a Gut Check at DC 13.
- **Possible payoffs:** it's delicious and worth money at the next port (a lead for Aerion's ledger); it gives everyone mildly shared dreams one night (wonder, not dread); or it's the crew's first Galley Incident of the season.

**2.6 Baram Oyle's tray.** *Use: a port where the crew bumps into another ship's galley.*
Baram Oyle, the ex-navy dwarf cook the crew passed on at the Yard (`The Yard.md`), is now feeding someone else's crew: nutritionally perfect, zero waste, and joyless. He and Brass meet. He finds her wasteful. She finds him bleak.
- **Baram:** "Four hundred and forty calories. Complete protein. Next." **Brass:** "Does anybody on your ship smile at breakfast?" **Baram:** "Smiling is not a nutrient."
- **What it offers:** a reminder of the road not taken. The crew could have had reliable food. They got Brass. Whether anyone says out loud that they'd choose her again is up to them.
- **Optional:** Baram's crew, starved of joy, quietly asks Brass for a recipe. She gives it for free.

**2.7 The Suggestion Box responds.** *Use: a cutaway, any session.*
Brass finally answers the suggestion box. She reads every entry aloud at dinner. "More butter" (Finny, signed): she serves him a stick of butter on a plate. "More coffee" (the mug's handwriting): she refills Estra without a word. Her own "IOU - Brass" napkin: "Not a suggestion. Leave it in there."

## 3. Character moments

**3.1 The napkin with your name on it.** *Use: when a PC helps her with something, or when Aerion finally pulls the thread.*
A PC finds one of Brass's napkins with their own name on it. It isn't a debt. It's a contingency: where they'd go if the ship went down, who owes Brass enough to hide them, a port with a doctor who'd take them.
- **What it means:** Brass has been planning everyone's escape, not just her own. She's folded the crew into the net.
- **Offer:** whether the PC confronts her, keeps it, or puts it back is entirely theirs. If they ask, Brass: "Somebody has to know where the exits are. It's just me."

**3.2 The wrong name.** *Use: a port, when the crew is somewhere Brass hasn't been in years.*
Someone across a market calls out a name that isn't Brass. She doesn't turn around. She doesn't speed up either. It is a very good performance.
- **Insight DC 14:** she heard it. She knows who it was.
- **The follow-up:** later, she says the person was mistaken. If a PC points out she never looked back, she says, "Exactly."
- **This is a rung on the arc ladder** (section 4), not a crisis. The stranger may be harmless.

**3.3 The overdue monograph.** *Use: arrival at Bral or any port with a library or academy.*
Brass has a borrowed medical text that's eleven years overdue. The library is at this port. She wants to return it without anyone seeing her face.
- **Solution paths:** a PC returns it for her (the librarian recognizes the book and asks after the borrower); she sneaks it back (Stealth DC 12, very silly); or she walks in and pays the fine, which is ruinous and absurd, and a real choice to be seen.
- **Brass:** "It's not about the fine. The fine's the easy part."

**3.4 Someone cooks for her at 0300.** *Use: after a session that ran hot, or after 1.6.*
Brass comes into the galley at 0300 and someone is already there. They made something. It's probably bad.
- **Offer:** any crew member can be the one, NPC or PC. Oz is the natural default (he'll have made tea and toast and folded a napkin into a swan). If a PC wants it to be them, it's theirs.
- **The beat:** Brass sits down. She eats every bite. She doesn't make a joke. That's the whole scene.

**3.5 I know a guy, and the guy wants something.** *Use: whenever Brass's "I Know a Guy" (1/port) is used and you owe the table the cost.*
The stat block says it always costs something, and she mentions what at an inconvenient moment. Some costs, ready to spend:
1. The guy needs a crate carried two ports on, no questions. It's cheese. It's very illegal cheese.
2. The guy's kid has a cough. Brass goes, and it takes the whole evening.
3. The guy wants the crew to lose a hand of Liar's Berth to a specific person at the Compass Rose (`Downtime Games.md`).
4. The guy wants a signed copy of the Manifest Board printout. Finny has one.
5. The guy wants Brass to cook for his wedding. It's tomorrow. There are two hundred guests.
6. The guy just wants to know where Brass is flying next. She won't tell him. The favor is suddenly more expensive.

**3.6 The license board sits.** *Use: mid-campaign, once 1.4 and 3.3 have run.*
A licensing examination is being held at a port on the crew's route. Brass could sit for it. She's more than ready. Sitting means writing her real name in a registry.
- **The choice is hers,** and the DM's to play, but the crew can weigh in: Aerion can cost it out, Orena can say what she thinks, the others will have opinions.
- **Brass:** "A license is a name in a book, and a name in a book is an address."
- **This is the payoff for the whole license thread.** Don't spend it until the neogi threat has either surfaced or been faced (section 4), so the choice is real.

## 4. The arc ladder (the neogi debt, climbing slowly)

Her secret, in `Brass.md`: she cut loose a neogi slave hold, lost her license placement doing it, and has run from the debt ever since. The trigger is a neogi ship, or someone recognizing her. Climb one rung every few sessions at most. Seed, don't explain.

1. **A bad ingredient.** A market sells spice or dried meat that came off a neogi hold. Brass won't cook with it and won't say why. She just puts it back, very carefully, like it's hot.
2. **The wrong name** (3.2). Someone knows her from before. Maybe harmless.
3. **The napkin net lights up.** One of her contacts sends word through someone in the web: a question was asked about a medic matching her description. Brass goes very quiet, doubles her napkin work, and starts cooking better (she always does when she's scared).
4. **The neogi hull.** `Tables.md` encounter 12: a neogi ship running parallel, not engaging. Brass is in sickbay with the door shut. Someone should go check on her.
5. **The test.** The trigger from `Plot Threads.md`: does she run to keep the crew clear, or does the crew refuse to let her? Found family, put on the scale. The crew's answer is the players' to give.

**Keep the tone honest.** Neogi are slavers and the stakes are real. Keep them mostly offscreen until rung 4, and land every step back in warmth: the scene after a rung should be a meal.

## 5. Joint scenes for both PCs

Built so Orena and Aerion work the same problem from two directions (the standing design principle in `CLAUDE.md`).

**5.1 The Galley Coup.** *Use: pairs with 1.6, when Brass is laid up for a day or two.*
Someone has to cook for seven. The NPC crew volunteers in the least helpful way possible. The two leads are the most competent people available, and that is a low bar.
- **Aerion's side:** the stores, the costs, what's actually left in the hold (and whether 14-C is food).
- **Orena's side:** the actual cooking, if she wants it, or the crew wrangling.
- **Mechanics:** whoever cooks rolls d20 + Wisdom on the Galley table (`The Galley.md`). A helper gives advantage. Brass heckles from sickbay through the wall.
- **NPC beats:** Oz is reliably Solid and bored by it. Finny puts butter in everything. Tack offers to "tune" the stove and it gets hotter than any stove should. Estra doesn't help and eats whatever they make.
- **Brass (through the wall):** "Is that the good pan? That's the good pan. Put it down."
- **What's at stake:** nothing but pride, and Brass's discovery that the crew can manage without her for a day, which is her worst fear and, maybe, the first step past it.

**5.2 The provisioning run.** *Use: any port restock.*
Brass sends both PCs to buy the week's stores with a napkin shopping list. The list is in her private shorthand.
- **The puzzle:** half the list is ingredients. The other half is sickbay supplies disguised as ingredients ("red bread" is a styptic powder). Aerion reads the prices; Orena reads the people. The stallholders know Brass.
- **Checks:** Investigation DC 12 to decode the shorthand; Insight DC 13 to notice a stallholder is nervous when they hear Brass's name (optional arc-ladder rung 3 seed); Persuasion or haggling to beat the list's budget (Aerion's playground).
- **Payoff:** if they bring back everything, **advantage** on the next Galley Roll and a hug from Brass. If they improvise, she cooks it anyway.

**5.3 The judging panel.** *Use: the cook-off (2.1).*
Both PCs are asked to judge. Brass is their crewmate. Gary is better at presentation. The food is closer than anyone expected.
- **Offer:** how they vote is theirs. Finny has money on it. Brass is watching.

- "Tonight's special is Starfall Risotto. Don't ask what fell."
- "Hold still. No, stiller. That's it. You're very good at this."
- "I know a guy. He's terrible. You'll love him."
- "Eat. Then complain. In that order."
- "You'll live. Not today's problem."
- "Oh, that's broken. Want me to tell you before or after you finish the story?"
- "That's not a cold. Go lie down. Take the soup."
- (at 0300, sliding a plate over) "Couldn't sleep either."
- (on the morning roll) "Look, the dice don't know me."
