## Notes (Granola AI summary)

### Character Backgrounds and Timeline
- Ship has been active for three years; one character joined six months ago
- Both characters trained under the same uncle before the ship's founding
- The six-month character joined after the ship was already established, so wasn't part of buying it
- Established enough that crew won't question authority, especially after a successful early mission

### Ship Hierarchy
- Captain: older figure (50s–60s), retired adventurer, brought in by the family as experienced leadership
  - Framing: "here's your ship, let's see what you do with it"
- One character positioned as second-in-command (vice captain)
- Other character: navigator, with ambition to train up as helmsman
- Bosun role also discussed: crew manager / chief of staff equivalent

### World Structure: The Five Families
- Governing body covers populated systems in the astral sea, not the whole universe
- Five ruling families (currently four, one lost under unclear circumstances)
- Each family specializes: mercantile finance, military power, invention (astral skiffs), etc.
- One character's father is actively vying for more influence, possibly the vacant fifth seat
- Families control trade routes, tax passage, and run security in established settlements
- Large "Wild West" zones exist outside governed space

### Privateers vs. Pirates
- Privateers: contracted by families/companies for missions, operating within the system
- Pirates: outside the system, pillaging or exploiting it without membership
- Group consensus: prefer privateer framing over pirate vagabond

### World Buy-In and Character Motivations
- All players happy with the structured political world; adds established lore to work with
- Characters don't need to know their place in the world yet: finding that is part of the story
- One character's dragon (silver, named Flurry) noted as a personal anchor
- Joining the crew serves dual purposes: finding lost crew members and needing paid work
