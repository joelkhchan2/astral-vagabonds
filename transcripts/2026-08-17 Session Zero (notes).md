## Characters and Backstories

- **Arion (Orion):** half-elf, 525 years old, joined an established trading company 5 years ago
  - Goal: secretly buy out his father's merchant company; saving as much gold as possible
  - Reads the Dragon Master series; writing his own young adult fantasy series in downtime
  - Has a daughter (Florite/Flurry): a baby, non-combatant, found on a treasure hunt
  - Dislikes when people talk too long (shared trait with another character)
- **Priscilla's character:** astral elf, ~31 years old, previously a navigator
  - Joined the crew 6 months ago after the previous helmsman retired to start a family
  - Training to become helmsman under the captain
  - In downtime: mapping rumored artifact and crew locations
  - Family is wealthy, largely indifferent to her activities; has access to a trust fund
- **Shared backstory:** both characters trained together as teenagers at a space combat camp
  - Camp run by Arion's uncle (Uncle Winsome), a famed swordsman
  - Camp took place on the material plane (Forgotten Realms); uncle visited with Priscilla in tow
  - Priscilla later traveled to the astral plane as an adult, partly inspired by that experience

## The Crew's Situation

- Original ship blew up (the cook is blamed); insurance paid out
- Crew went from ~14 members to a skeleton crew of 5-6 loyal survivors
- Currently docked at the Rock of Brawl, destitute, rebuilding from scratch
- Arion partially funded the new ship using family money (framed as trust fund spending)
- Starting gold: 50,000 (insurance + family contribution); additional family funds available with future consequences

## The New Ship

- **Ship type:** Sturdy Trader (20,000 gp)
  - AC 19, speed 45 ft (base 55 ft minus 10 ft penalty), 250 HP
  - Damage threshold 20 (enemies must deal 20+ damage to harm the hull)
  - Crew capacity: up to 11
- **Helm:** Standard Mark I (upgradeable later)
- **Armor:** Plating I (single hit cannot disable two systems; absorbs first 4 damage per fight; 600 gp to replace)
- **Weapons:**
  - Two ballistas, each upgraded with Sure Shot and Fast Load (aim + shoot as bonus action; loading as bonus action)
  - Blunt ram (for collision/boarding)
- **Ammunition:** 250 standard shots per ballista (5 gp each)
- Remaining budget after outfitting: ~14,350 gp (plus Priscilla's 10,000 gp trust fund reserve)
- **Ship name:** provisionally "The Last Honest Deal" (the GM's working title; the crew is open to it)

## Spelljammer World Mechanics

- Every solar system exists inside a "marble" (a bubble); outside is the Astral Sea (wild space)
- Ships are powered by a Spelljammer Helm: a throne-like chair the helmsman attunes to and must physically occupy
  - Top speed: 7 million km/h; captain is the backup helmsman
- Ships generate their own gravity; crew can walk on any surface, including the underside
- Air bubble lasts 120 days baseline; life support upgrade adds 60 more days (skipped for now)
- Astral currents form from repeated travel along the same routes (mind-powered ships shape the sea itself)
  - Popular routes become faster; navigational data is a tradeable currency

## Economy and Income

- **Income sources:**
  - Contracts: fixed courier or hauling jobs (steady but modest)
  - Prize jobs: high-risk, high-reward missions others won't take
  - Navigational data: log new routes, anomalies, systems, and sell to the Cartographers' Consortium
- **Ongoing costs:**
  - Hull repairs: 20 gp per HP of damage (a bad fight can cost more than a month's income)
  - Skeleton crew of 6: 500 gp/month; crew of 8: 800 gp/month
  - Loans, favors, and political capital also factor in
- Reputation tracked on the Manifest Board at the Rock of Brawl (based on contract completion and reliability)

## The Five Founding Houses

- **House Vasara:** oldest and richest; controls the most navigational data (and likely hoards some)
- **House Tessen:** security and enforcement; the known mercenaries
- **House Delverine:** inventors, engineers, and explorers (ship manufacturers)
- **House Nightbreeze:** financiers and insurers
- **Fifth seat:** vacant after one house collapsed; multiple factions competing for it, including Arion's family
