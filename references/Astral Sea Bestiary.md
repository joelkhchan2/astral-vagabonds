# Astral Sea Bestiary

Creatures native to the Astral Sea, or plausible to encounter drifting through it. Compiled for the *Astral Vagabonds* campaign — excludes demons, devils, Abyss-native fiends, and overtly religious/deity-worshipping celestials per campaign constraints.

**Confidence key:**
- **[Verified]** — cross-checked against a live published 5e stat-block source (aidedd.org, 5e SRD, official book scan). Table-ready as-is.
- **[Reconstructed]** — creature and CR confirmed to exist, but the exact numeric stat block could not be pulled from an accessible source (paywalled PDFs, blocked scans). Numbers below are mechanically consistent estimates anchored to confirmed data points (CR, key traits, partial stats). Good enough to run tonight; spot-check against the physical book if you own it before treating as canon.
- **[No 5e stats]** — no published 5e conversion exists. A reflavor suggestion is given instead of invented numbers.

Most "Boo's Astral Menagerie" and "Astral Adventurer's Guide" entries are official WotC content (bundled in the *Spelljammer: Adventures in Space* 2022 boxed set), not third-party, despite sometimes being mislabeled online.

### Astral Dragon — Lunar **[Reconstructed — confirmed CR anchors only]**
*Boo's Astral Menagerie*
Dragon family. Confirmed: wyrmling CR 2, ancient CR ~19; young/adult interpolated (young ≈ CR 6, adult ≈ CR 13).
Approx HP: wyrmling ~40, young ~130, adult ~230, ancient ~350-400.

Drift near cold moons and ice-locked asteroids, moving in and out of phase with reality; ambush hunters that root prey in place before closing.
- Cold Breath (recharge 5-6): cone, Dex save, cold damage + immobilize/entangle on fail
- Phase (bonus action): semi-incorporeal, resistance to nonmagical physical damage while concentrating
- No need to breathe; darkvision 120-240 ft.
- Adult/ancient: Legendary Resistance (3/day), legendary actions incl. Treacherous Ice (difficult terrain)

### Astral Dragon — Solar **[Reconstructed — confirmed CR anchors only]**
*Boo's Astral Menagerie*
Confirmed: wyrmling CR 3, ancient CR 20; young/adult interpolated (young ≈ CR 7, adult ≈ CR 14).
Approx HP: wyrmling ~70, young ~160, adult ~280, ancient ~450+.

Born and lair inside living stars, migrating between suns. Encountering one usually means the party strayed somewhere they weren't meant to go.
- Photonic Breath: originates from a chosen point in range, not the mouth — supercharged *fireball*-style radiant burst
- Immune to radiant; vulnerable to fire (tactical hook)
- Flyby: no opportunity attacks when flying away after attacking
- Nebulous Thoughts: resistance to telepathic detection
- Adult/ancient: Siege Monster, legendary actions
