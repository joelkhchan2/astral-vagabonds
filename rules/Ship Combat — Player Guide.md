# Ship Combat, Player Guide

When the *Night Fury* gets into a fight, you don't just roll initiative as your own character, you take a **station** on the ship and fight *her*. This is the plain-language version for the table. (Full rules: `Rules & Systems.md` §8. Current ship state: `ships/Night Fury.md`.)

Two things up front:

- **Stations aren't classes.** Anyone can crew any station, and you can switch between fights, or even mid-fight if the fiction allows (crossing the deck costs your *movement*, not your action). Grab whichever seat the moment needs.
- **Combat is expensive, not deadly.** Every point of hull damage costs **20 gp** to repair afterward, and a bad scrap can eat a month's pay. The ship is tough (she ignores any hit under **20 damage** and has good armor), so the real goal is usually to **win fast and clean**, or talk or outfly your way out, rather than trade broadsides. Losing the fight rarely kills you. Losing it *slowly* bankrupts you.

---

## The stations

### Helm, 1 crew

Flying the ship is **free**, steering doesn't cost your action or your movement, so every round the Helm gets a free action to spend on **one maneuver**, or you can skip the maneuver and do something else (fight, cast, help another station).

**One maneuver per round:**

| Maneuver | What it does | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Evasive Action** | Attacks against the ship have **disadvantage** until your next turn | Your own weapons fire at disadvantage this round, nobody can aim while she's juking |
| **Full Burn** | The ship's speed **doubles** this round (close, run, or disengage) | No drawback to the ship itself. If you **Ram on your next turn**, that attack has **advantage**, the momentum you just built carries through the hit |
| **Boarding Action** | A contested Piloting/Dexterity check locks onto a ship your size or smaller, so people can cross | Both ships are grappled, neither can Full Burn or disengage until someone breaks it |

*Estra flies by default, Orena's training up as the second pilot, and in a pinch anyone attuned can take the chair. You can't hold a concentration spell while flying, piloting takes concentration of its own, and taking damage risks losing the helm.*

**The Ram isn't a Helm maneuver.** It used to be, it isn't anymore, it's its own station below.

### Ram, 2 crew

One attack at the ram's listed damage. The ship takes **half of that back**, applied after her own damage threshold. This replaces the Helm's 1 that round, it isn't stacked on top, somebody has to actually steer into the hit.

### Gunnery, per weapon

The *Night Fury* carries five ballistae, all named: **Gertrude** and **Susan ("Angel")** are upgraded and cost **2 crew each** to fire; **Kevin ("Ford")**, **Lightning**, and a fifth still argued over are at the baseline **3 crew each**. Firing more than one in a round costs each separately, no shared discount, firing all five at once would cost 13 total.

### Engineer, 1 crew

Not full repairs, that's gold and downtime afterward. **In the fight**, an Engineer can spend their action on an **Emergency Patch**: a Technology or Intelligence check, DC 12. On a success, the ship regains **hit points equal to your level**, once per fight per Engineer. It's a bandage, it keeps the hull up long enough to finish, but it **doesn't lower the repair bill**. (Very Tack.)

### Coordinator, 1 crew

Usually whoever isn't piloting or gunning. Spend your action to do **one** of these per round:

- Give **one ally advantage** on an attack or check this round
- Impose **disadvantage** on one attack aimed at the ship or a crewmate
- Give **+1d4** to one ally's attack roll or check this round, stacks with advantage or disadvantage from other sources

Only one Coordinator benefit per round, and only if someone's actually doing it. It's not a passive bonus.

### Personal boarding, removes 1 from the pool

Once ships are close (grappled by a Boarding Action, or just flown alongside on purpose), anyone can spend movement and an Athletics or Acrobatics check to physically cross and fight on the other deck as an individual. Doing this pulls you out of your own ship's pool for the round, you're fighting your own fight now, not crewing a station.

## On your turn, quick version

1. **Which seat am I in?** (Helm / Ram / Gunnery / Engineer / Coordinator, you can move to a new one, that's your movement.)
2. **Do the station thing:**
   - Helm → pick one maneuver, or fly straight and do something else
   - Ram → one attack, half the damage comes back to the hull
   - Gunnery → add your action to a ballista's firing crew
   - Engineer → Emergency Patch (once per fight), or other repairs the DM allows
   - Coordinator → one benefit: advantage, disadvantage, or +1d4
3. **Watch the repair meter.** Damage the ship takes is a bill you pay later. Evasive Action, ending the fight quickly, or not fighting at all are all valid ways to protect the account.

## Good to know

- **Turn order is by side** (the DM's "side initiative"), so the crew tends to act together, coordinate out loud before the ship's turn.
- **The ship ignores small hits.** Anything under 20 damage bounces off the hull (that's her damage threshold). Little guns can't hurt her, big ones can.
- **You can't hold a concentration spell while flying.** Piloting takes concentration of its own, and taking damage can knock you off the helm, so a caster in the chair is committed to flying.
- **Winning isn't always shooting.** Boarding, outrunning, or refusing the fight are on the table every time, and usually cheaper.
