# Ship Combat — Player Guide

When the *Deal* gets into a fight, you don't just roll initiative as your own character — **you take a station on the ship** and fight *her*. This is the plain-language version for the table. (Full rules: `Rules & Systems.md` §1 for the canon basics and §8 for the station system.)

Two things up front:

- **Stations aren't classes.** Anyone can crew any station, and you can switch between fights — or even mid-fight if the fiction allows (crossing the deck costs your *movement*, not your action). Grab whichever seat the moment needs.
- **Combat is expensive, not deadly.** Every point of hull damage costs **20 gp** to repair afterward, and a bad scrap can eat a month's pay. The ship is tough (she ignores any hit under **20 damage** and has good armor), so the real goal is usually to **win fast and clean** — or talk/outfly your way out — rather than trade broadsides. Losing the fight rarely kills you. Losing it *slowly* bankrupts you.

## The four stations

### 🪑 Pilot — whoever's in the helm-chair
Flying the ship is **free**: steering doesn't cost your action or your movement, so every round you get a free action to spend on **one maneuver** (chosen when the ship's turn comes up), or you can skip the maneuver and do something else (fight, cast, help another station).

**One maneuver per round:**

| Maneuver | What it does | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Evasive Action** | Attacks against the ship have **disadvantage** until your next turn | Your own weapons fire at disadvantage this round — nobody can aim while she's juking |
| **Full Burn** | Ship's speed **doubles** this round (close, run, or disengage) | Attacks against the ship have **advantage** until your next turn — you're a fat target going that fast |
| **Ram** | Make one attack with the ship's **blunt ram**, no gun crew needed | The ship takes **half** the damage it deals, back onto her own hull |
| **Boarding Action** | Lock onto a ship your size or smaller (contested piloting check) so people can cross over | Both ships are grappled — neither can Full Burn or disengage until someone breaks it |

*Orena is the crew's helm-in-training, so this is usually her seat — but the Captain flies backup, and in a pinch anyone attuned can take the chair.*

### 🎯 Gunner — the weapon crews
The *Deal* carries **two ballistas** and the **blunt ram**. A ballista normally needs a crew and takes a few actions to load–aim–fire — **but ours are upgraded** (Sureshot + Ready Magazine), so aiming and loading are quick, and a small crew can get shots off fast. As a gunner you spend your action feeding that firing sequence. More hands on the guns = more shots per round. (Tack knows the guns; Aussie hits hard — either makes a fine gunner.)

### 🔧 Engineer — keeping her standing
Not full repairs — that's gold and downtime afterward. **In the fight**, an Engineer can spend their action on an **Emergency Patch**: a check (DC 12); on a success the ship regains **hit points equal to your level**, once per fight per engineer. It's a bandage — it keeps the hull up long enough to finish, but it **doesn't lower the repair bill**. (Very Tack.)

### 🧭 Coordinator — running the fight from the deck
Usually whoever isn't piloting or gunning. Spend your action to do **one** of these per round:

- Give **one ally advantage** on an attack or check this round
- Impose **disadvantage** on one attack aimed at the ship or a crewmate
- Let one station (Pilot, Gunner, or Engineer) **act again** out of turn order, once

Only one Coordinator benefit per round — and only if someone's actually doing it. It's not a passive bonus. (Priscilla, who runs the crew, is a natural here — but so is anyone directing traffic.)

## On your turn — quick version

1. **Which seat am I in?** (Pilot / Gunner / Engineer / Coordinator — you can move to a new one, that's your movement.)
2. **Do the station thing:**
   - Pilot → pick one maneuver, or fly straight and do something else
   - Gunner → add your action to a weapon firing
   - Engineer → Emergency Patch (once per fight), or other repairs the DM allows
   - Coordinator → one benefit: advantage / disadvantage / let a station go again
3. **Watch the repair meter.** Damage the ship takes is a bill you pay later. Evasive Action, ending the fight quickly, or not fighting at all are all valid ways to protect the account.

---

## Good to know

- **Turn order is by side** (the DM's "side initiative"), so the crew tends to act together — coordinate out loud before the ship's turn.
- **The ship ignores small hits.** Anything under 20 damage bounces off the hull (that's her damage threshold). Little guns can't hurt her; big ones can.
- **You can't hold a concentration spell while flying.** Piloting takes concentration of its own, and taking damage can knock you off the helm — so a caster in the chair is committed to flying.
- **Winning isn't always shooting.** Boarding, outrunning, or refusing the fight are on the table every time — and usually cheaper.
