# The Galley (Brass's cooking and the Gut Check) **[HR]**

Brass cooks breakfast and dinner aboard the *Night Fury*. She is a medic first and a cook under protest, and the food is a gamble the crew has made peace with. Two d20s: one for how the cooking went, one for the dish and what it does to you. A bad dish means a Con save. The best dishes are a small Heroes' Feast.

Character material, scenes, and arc beats for Brass live in `Brass - Scenes & Bits.md`. Her stat block is in `Stat Blocks.md`.

---

## 1. The Galley Roll (two d20s)

**When:** once a day, when Brass cooks. Roll both dice openly so the table can groan or cheer.

**Roll 1, the cooking.** Which table the meal comes from.

| 1st d20 | Result |
|---|---|
| **1–5** | **Bad table.** On a natural 1 it's a **Galley Incident**: roll 2 has disadvantage, the Gut Check is DC 15, and roll on "make it worse." |
| **6–15** | **Fine table.** |
| **16–20** | **Good table.** On a natural 20 it's a **Brass Classic**: roll 2 has advantage, and the dish goes in the Hall of Fame. |

**Roll 2, the dish.** Roll a d20 on that table. It gives you the dish and its effect together. **Higher is always better**, on every table. **One roll for the whole table**: it's one meal, so everyone who eats gets the same dish and effect, and each person makes their own save where one applies.

**How long effects last:** "next" effects last until used or until your next long rest. Temporary hit points last until your next long rest and don't stack with other temp HP (keep the higher). Anything else says how long it lasts.

**Modifiers on roll 1 only.** **Advantage** when she has good ingredients (a fresh restock, or a gift like Orena's basket) or a helper in the galley. **Disadvantage** when stores are stale or she spent the day healing people. Both cancel, as normal.

**At 0300,** off the clock, skip roll 1. Roll on the Good table for whoever couldn't sleep.

**Someone else cooks:** roll 1 is d20 + their Wisdom modifier. Roll 2 is the same.

**Using a d4 for roll 1:** 1 Bad, 2–3 Fine, 4 Good. Roll 2 is still a d20.

### The Hall of Fame

Classics that happened at the table. When a Hall of Fame dish is cooked again with the right ingredients, roll 1 has **advantage**. It never becomes automatic; Brass has tried.
- **The Octopus** (session 1, before departure from Bral). A natural 20, universally loved, in front of witnesses. Aerion offered, unprompted, to fund future octopus nights. That offer is his to keep or quietly forget; if a port sells octopus, mention the price and let the ledger decide. Run it as a Good-table result of your choosing, or roll.

## 3. The Gut Check

A **Constitution saving throw** against a meal. **Pass and the effect doesn't happen.** Everyone who ate rolls at once.
- **Bad table:** DC 12, or DC 15 on a Galley Incident.
- **Fine table, results 1–10:** DC 10.
- **Anything else a sensible person wouldn't eat** (port street food, leftovers of unknown age, Brass's hot sauce): DC 10 for iffy, 13 for nasty, 15 for the hot sauce. Use Queasy on a fail.

**Effects:**
- **Poisoned:** the normal condition.
- **Queasy:** disadvantage on your next Con save. Ends on a short rest.
- **Sluggish:** speed −10 ft. Ends on a short rest.
- **Loud Stomach:** disadvantage on Stealth checks. Ends on a short rest.
- **Fish Breath:** disadvantage on your next Charisma check.

**Cast-Iron Stomach.** Every Bad-table meal you eat is a mark on Brass's napkin tally, pass or fail. Skipping the meal doesn't count. At **5 marks** you have **advantage on Gut Checks, and on any save against poison from food or drink, permanently.** Brass announces each mark at breakfast like a promotion, and the fifth like a graduation.

**Brass eats first.** If she fails her own save, she says so ("...okay. That one's on me.") and anyone can skip the meal.

**The crew:** Estra +2, Oz +2 (thanks her anyway), Finny +1 (Lucky, rerolls 1s, complains loudest), Brass +1. **Tack** doesn't eat, but sets a place and reads out a sensor verdict ("Carbon content: ambitious"). **Flurry** doesn't roll. She'll eat a boot, so if she won't touch the plate, everyone saw. Orena and Aerion roll their own Con saves, and whether they eat a Bad meal at all is their players' call.

## 4. Brass's Tonic

Where the medic meets the cook. During a short rest, Brass brews a tonic from sickbay stores: it **ends any one meal effect** (Poisoned, Queasy, Sluggish, Loud Stomach, Fish Breath). One per person per long rest. It tastes terrible and works anyway. Brass: "Good. That means it's working."

## 5. Operation Clean Plate

Established in session 2: Brass's real goal isn't the license, it's getting Aerion (who eats almost nothing but white rice and dry toast) to finish three of her meals. **Count: 0 of 3.** A plate counts only if Priscilla decides Aerion finishes it. Never assume or script it. Her attempts, roughly escalating:
1. **The yogurt parfait.** Already announced. Layered in a glass so he can see nothing is hidden in it.
2. **Plain Rice, Improved** (Good table 9), with the optional dish placed a careful distance away.
3. **A toast flight.** Four toasts, four breads, dry. She wrote tasting notes.
4. **Congee.** It's technically rice. She'll argue the point.
5. **Octopus again**, on his own tab, since he offered.
6. **Nothing.** She stops trying for a week, leaves a plate at his station at 0300, and never mentions it.

If the count ever reaches 3, see `Brass - Scenes & Bits.md`.

## 6. Running it

- **Roll it in the cold open.** One number sets the mood and gives every NPC a line.
- **Don't roll every meal.** Once per in-game day at most, and skip it on busy nights.
- **Counterweight a hot session.** If last session ran dark, let the 0300 galley happen or nudge toward Good.
- **Failure is the fun.** A failed Gut Check is a warm, silly beat, never a punishment.
