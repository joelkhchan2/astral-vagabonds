# House Delverane

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics. See `npcs/Aurel Delverane.md` for the house's own field surveyor, already in play, and the crew's most likely real ally in all of this.

The youngest founding house, roughly eighty years old, and the poorest by a wide margin. Not primarily instrument makers, that's a secondary, vertically-integrated business. **House Delverane's real engine is funding and backing independent adventuring parties across Wildspace in exchange for a cut of what they recover**, survey data, treasure, first-contact rights, whatever a sponsored party actually finds. Delverane Instruments (the Standard Set, the Deepline Suite, the Brightchair helm) exists because the house needs to equip the parties it funds anyway, and sells the surplus to everyone else.

**Naming convention, going forward: generation-split, like House Windsong.** Aurel's generation carries "Au-." Her father's generation carries "Il-."

## Ilgard Delverane, the head of house

Aurel's father. A field surveyor himself once, before duty pulled him back to run the house. He never forbade her choice out of contempt for fieldwork, he's watching his own daughter keep the life he gave up, and he's never fully worked out whether what he feels about that is pride or envy. That's why he's hard on her specifically, not because he thinks she's wrong, because he thinks she's right and it costs him something every time he's reminded of it.

**Want:** a real Board vote, finally, won by sheer volume of proof rather than argument nobody with the votes has ever had to listen to.

**Voice tic:** narrates in survey language even off the clock, same habit as Aurel's own, except his runs backward, "that's a pattern I've seen before," where hers runs forward, "that's a pattern worth watching."

**Physical tic:** still keeps a field survey kit in his own office, decades out of date. Never uses it. Never puts it away either.

**Current plan.** Delverane doesn't need to win the Board through debate, Ilgard is betting on volume: hundreds of independently funded adventuring parties across Wildspace, each paying a cut of what they recover, each one more real-world data than anything Vellum's static archive can match. If the model keeps scaling, the house doesn't need permission to prove the collaborative approach works, it'll already have, just not under that name yet. **This is a quiet, structural precursor to the campaign's actual cure**, Delverane's funded parties are already doing in miniature what the nomadic network eventually does at scale, and Ilgard has no idea that's the shape of what he's building.

## Scale

**Fleet:** nineteen Swift Traders and Cutters, the house's own core oversight and operations fleet, cheap and fast and constantly out. This is not the real measure of the house's reach.

**The real scale is the funded network**: somewhere in the range of **300 to 600 actively sponsored independent parties** across Wildspace at any given time, survey teams, treasure hunters, frontier explorers, each operating under a Delverane funding contract. **Farthing Station, Aurel's own office, is one outpost among several**, not a separately branded guild, just House Delverane operating directly. This is the actual source of the house's influence, far larger than its own fleet or net worth alone would suggest, and the reason it stays a real contender despite being the poorest founding house.

## The house's relationships

- **Delverane and Vellum:** one-directional contempt. Vellum blocks every real-time-data reform without bothering to disguise it as anything but a vote.
- **Delverane and Stoneward:** mutual distrust. Dagna reads Delverane's open-data instinct as a security risk; Ilgard reads Stoneward's enforcement contracts as a tax on the very frontier Delverane is trying to keep alive.
- **Delverane and Nightbreeze:** the house most likely to eventually back Delverane's model, once the premium math says so, and not one day before.
- **Delverane and the crew:** already live, through Aurel, and Ilgard doesn't know it yet. Keep that gap deliberate.
