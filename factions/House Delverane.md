# House Delverane

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics. See `npcs/Aurel Delverane.md` for the house's own field surveyor, already in play, and the crew's most likely real ally in all of this.

The youngest founding house, roughly eighty years old, and the poorest by a wide margin. Instrument makers and frontier cartographers, outvoted on everything, increasingly loud about it, and the only founding house that actually works the Windward Edge where the Stilling shows first.

## Mirabel Delverane, the head of house

Aurel's mother. The connection `npcs/Aurel Delverane.md` leaves open ("whether her own house connections ever become relevant") is this: Mirabel is the one who's never quite forgiven Aurel for choosing fieldwork over a boardroom seat, not out of contempt for the work, out of a parent's fear that the house can't afford to lose a Board-capable voice when it's already outvoted on everything that matters.

**Want:** a real vote. Not sympathy, not a seat at the table, an actual majority willing to adopt real-time collaborative data before the archive model finishes starving the frontier it's supposed to be charting.

**Voice tic:** thinks out loud in survey language even in a political argument, margins of error, confidence intervals, "that's not a fact, that's a sample size of one," a habit that reads as pedantic to the other houses and is actually just how she's wired.

**Physical tic:** chart-ink on at least one finger, same as Aurel's, never quite washed off between sessions at the ledger. The family trait is real; whether it ever gets noticed as such by anyone at the table is open.

**Current plan.** She can't get the votes to reform the Pale Reach from inside, so she's stopped trying to and started building the alternative quietly, through exactly the kind of independent operators the archive model was built to make irrelevant. She doesn't know it yet, but Aurel's own working relationship with the *Night Fury* (`sessions/Session 5 Prep.md`'s Open Water, Open Season) is precisely the kind of real-world proof of concept Mirabel's been looking for and hasn't found a credible test case for. The alliance `factions/Houses & Holdings.md` already flags as "geographic before it's ideological" starts here, through the daughter, whether or not Mirabel ever finds out her own house's future is being quietly test-driven by a 340th-ranked independent crew.

**Guttering stance, restated plainly:** adapt or die, and she means it literally. Delverane's fleet is cheap and fast and constantly out because standing still is the one thing the house genuinely cannot afford.

## The house's relationships

- **Delverane and Vellum:** one-directional contempt. Vellum blocks every real-time-data reform without bothering to disguise it as anything but a vote.
- **Delverane and Stoneward:** mutual distrust. Ysolde reads Delverane's open-data instinct as a security risk; Mirabel reads Stoneward's enforcement contracts as a tax on the very frontier Delverane is trying to keep alive.
- **Delverane and Nightbreeze:** the one house that might actually help, eventually. Nightbreeze follows the money, and Delverane's model is cheaper than Vellum's the moment claims outrun premiums. Neither house has said this out loud to the other yet.
- **Delverane and the crew:** already live, through Aurel, and the house's own senior figure doesn't know it yet. Keep that gap deliberate.
