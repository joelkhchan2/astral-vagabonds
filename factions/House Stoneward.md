# House Stoneward

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

The military-adjacent founding house. They sell the crew their own weapons (Stoneward Defensive Solutions makes the ballistae on every price sheet in Wildspace) and profit directly from exactly the danger the Stilling creates. Not evil, a security contractor that has worked out, consciously, that a frightened Wildspace is a paying one.

## Dame Ysolde Stoneward, Warden-Marshal

Oversees the Route Wardens, the toll-gate patrols, and the house's mercenary recruitment through its Outer Planar contacts. Career security, three generations of it, and genuinely good at the job, the house's wealth is earned competence, not just inherited leverage.

**Want:** turn the house's temporary, danger-driven contracts into permanent jurisdiction. Every crisis Stoneward responds to should leave behind infrastructure that outlasts the crisis.

**Voice tic:** speaks entirely in risk-assessment language, "that reads as an elevated threat corridor," "escort is the recommended posture," never a flat statement where a hedge will do.

**Physical tic:** taps two fingers against her sword hilt while thinking, never draws, just taps, a habit everyone who's served under her has learned to read as "she's calculating," not "she's about to fight."

**Current plan.** Lobbying the CIV to make escort fees mandatory, not optional, on any route that's logged a Stilling-adjacent anomaly in the past year. Framed entirely as a safety measure. It is also a new, permanent toll dressed as one, and she knows it. Separately, she's quietly expanding the house's mercenary pipeline through a mercane banking contact (the same mercane network House Nightbreeze already deals in, a real point of overlap between the two houses) against the possibility that void scavver culls need to scale back up.

**Guttering stance, restated plainly:** opportunity, openly. She wants the Stilling to keep being dangerous exactly long enough to justify a bigger enforcement remit, and not one day longer than that.

## The house's relationships

- **Stoneward and Vellum:** comfortable, mutually reinforcing. Both are status-quo powers with a shared interest in the archive's authority meaning something.
- **Stoneward and Delverane:** contemptuous, one direction. Ysolde reads Delverane's push for open, shared data as a security risk, not an innovation, information that flows freely is information nobody can be billed to protect.
- **Stoneward and Nightbreeze:** transactional, through the shared mercane banking channel, cordial without being warm.
- **Stoneward and the crew.** No direct angle yet, background for now. The crew buys Stoneward's weapons at every port without knowing any of the above; that's the quiet irony worth keeping live rather than resolving early.
