# House Stoneward

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

The military-adjacent founding house. They sell the crew their own weapons (Stoneward Defensive Solutions makes the ballistae on every price sheet in Wildspace) and profit directly from exactly the danger the Stilling creates. Not evil, a security contractor that has worked out, consciously, that a frightened Wildspace is a paying one, and the largest personnel footprint of any founding house even though Vellum has more money on paper.

## Dagna Stoneward, Warden-Marshal

A stone dwarf, career security, three generations of it, genuinely good at the job, the house's wealth is earned competence, not just inherited leverage. Oversees the Route Wardens, the toll-gate patrols, the arms business, and, personally and quietly, the Quiet Wardens (below).

**Want:** turn the house's temporary, danger-driven contracts into permanent jurisdiction. Every crisis Stoneward responds to should leave behind infrastructure that outlasts the crisis.

**Voice tic:** speaks entirely in risk-assessment language, "that reads as an elevated threat corridor," "escort is the recommended posture," never a flat statement where a hedge will do.

**Physical tic:** carries a worn piece of space obsidian, turns it over in one hand while she calculates. A sufficiently close look (DC 15-18, nothing a casual glance reveals) identifies it as a fragment of the same ancient dodecahedron type the crew encountered in `sessions/Session 3.md`, one of several such relics apparently out there, not necessarily that exact one. **Dagna encountered one herself, personally, at some unplaced point in her own past, and asked it something.** Both the question and the answer are her own secret, genuinely unresolved, not just withheld. Leave this unplaced for now, available whenever it's useful.

**Current plan.** Lobbying the CIV to make escort fees mandatory, not optional, on any route that's logged a Stilling-adjacent anomaly in the past year. Framed entirely as a safety measure. It is also a new, permanent toll dressed as one, and she knows it.

## The Quiet Wardens

Dagna's own project, run personally, not delegated. A deliberately invisible embedding program: loyal Stoneward operatives placed inside the security details of every other founding house, and inside parts of the CIV's own enforcement apparatus, the same Route Warden-adjacent authority that boards and inspects independent crews like the *Night Fury*.

**This is a deterrent, not a coup.** Nobody is planning to pull the trigger. The point is insurance and leverage, knowing exactly what every other house's security actually looks like from the inside, in case it's ever needed. **The roster is exact and precisely tracked, internally.** Dagna's command knows its own numbers, that's basic competence, not mystique. What's secret is that no outsider, no other house, no CIV auditor, has ever seen that roster or confirmed the network exists as a formal body.

## The four Dreadnoughts

**Deliberately never seen in the same place**, by design, so no one in Wildspace can accurately gauge Stoneward's real military strength: *Bastion*, *Oathkeeper*, *Stonegate*, and *Keystone*. The hard-power backstop behind the Quiet Wardens' soft leverage, the thing that makes the deterrent credible without ever needing to be used.

## Scale

- **~3,000** actual Route Warden crew and toll-gate garrison staff across the 31 patrol hulls and the Tollmarch's checkpoints.
- **~10,000+** in arms manufacturing and logistics, the industrial base behind Stoneward Defensive Solutions, spread across multiple foundries and depots at major ports.
- **An unknown-to-outsiders, precisely-tracked-internally** Quiet Wardens count, embedded across every other founding house and parts of the CIV.
- **Reach:** an arms depot or factor's office at essentially every major port Wildspace has, plus toll infrastructure at every chokepoint in the Tollmarch specifically.

## The house's relationships

- **Stoneward and Vellum:** comfortable, mutually reinforcing. Both are status-quo powers with a shared interest in the archive's authority meaning something, and neither knows the other's deepest secret.
- **Stoneward and Delverane:** contemptuous, one direction. Dagna reads Delverane's push for open, shared data as a security risk, information that flows freely is information nobody can be billed to protect.
- **Stoneward and Nightbreeze:** transactional, through the shared mercane banking channel, cordial without being warm.
- **Stoneward and the crew.** No direct angle yet, background for now. The crew buys Stoneward's weapons at every port without knowing any of the above; that's the quiet irony worth keeping live rather than resolving early.
