# House Vellum

> Economic data (net worth, fleet, territory): `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

The oldest founding house, and the one the campaign's actual antagonism runs through. Not because every Vellum is a villain, most of the house genuinely believes its own stewardship myth, but because the man actually running its quiet enforcement has a body count and nobody above him knows it.

## Dame Elowen Vellum, the seated head

Holds the house's actual Board chair. Sincere, procedural, old-money in the way that never has to raise its voice because the room already assumes it's right. She believes, without performing it, that Vellum's archive is the only thing standing between Wildspace and chaos, and that the house's wealth is a trust, not a prize. She is Alaric's niece.

**Want:** the archive's authority preserved intact, handed to the next generation exactly as she received it.

**Voice tic:** answers a question with the policy that governs it rather than her own opinion, "the Archive's position is," even when nobody asked for the Archive's position.

**Physical tic:** reads correspondence with the letter held slightly away from her body, as if checking it for a hidden second page, a habit from forty years of discovering that the real information is never in the first paragraph.

**What she doesn't know.** She has never been told what actually happened to House Carrow, and genuinely believes the official account. If she ever learns her uncle ordered a murder and has been quietly managing the fallout for forty years, in her own house, under her own seat, that's a real scene, played as betrayal from inside her own family's myth, not a villain's unmasking.

**Current plan.** Fighting the Fifth House bid on pure procedure: slow-walking every filing Vaelren's new house submits, citing precedent, demanding documentation nobody else's bid was ever asked for, never once saying out loud that she's afraid of what a serious challenger does to a Board that's run unopposed for forty years.

## Alaric Vellum, the man with glasses

Full account of what he's done: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`'s House Carrow section, and `npcs/Captain Estra Vane.md`. He killed Osric Carrow and crushed the Vane Compact a generation apart, for the same reason both times, and it has never once cost him anything.

**Want:** the archive's business model intact, permanently, by any method that doesn't trace back to him personally.

**Voice tic:** never answers the accusation in front of him, answers the one underneath it. Asked "did you know," he answers "what would knowing have changed."

**Physical tic:** straightens whatever's nearest to hand, a stack of papers, a crooked picture frame, mid-conversation, without seeming to notice he's doing it.

**Current plan, separate from Elowen's and more urgent.** He doesn't know whether the Reefside lockbox's contents survived, and he doesn't know who has them if they did. He's watching for exactly one thing: any sign that someone, anyone, has lined up Ivor Marsh's plea against the Archive policy 12.4 dates. Nothing louder than that yet. He has not connected the *Night Fury* to Reefside with any confidence; the house's working theory (per `campaign/Story Spine.md`) is still that the case was destroyed in the wreckage.

## Perrin Hale, the factor

Vellum's actual crew-facing presence. Established at the session 4 gala flashback, caught mid-negotiation with Aerin Nightbreeze over paperwork that hadn't crossed his desk yet, visibly flinching at her veiled threat about "our peaceful relationship." A mid-level factor, not a believer, not a cynic either, a man who does the job competently and goes home and doesn't think about the house's politics more than he has to.

**Want:** to keep doing exactly this job, unremarkably, for another thirty years.

**Voice tic:** apologizes before delivering any piece of information that might disappoint, "I'm afraid the position is," before he's even said what the position is.

**Physical tic:** straightens his cuffs before any exchange that matters to him, a tell any observant player could learn to read.

**Why he matters later.** He is the house's most human, most breakable face. If the crew ever needs a crack in Vellum, a nervous, decent, overworked factor who's seen more than he's supposed to and never asked about it is a far more playable way in than confronting Alaric directly. Nothing about him is settled canon yet beyond what's above, he's a resource to build toward, not a plot already written.

## The house's relationships

- **Vellum and Nightbreeze:** an uneasy, mutual dependency. Nightbreeze backs Vellum's denial because the subscription model still pays, while quietly building leverage (`factions/House Nightbreeze.md`) against the day it doesn't.
- **Vellum and Stoneward:** comfortable. Both are status-quo powers; Stoneward does the muscle work Vellum would rather not have its own name on.
- **Vellum and Delverane:** contemptuous, one direction only. Vellum blocks every real-time-data reform Delverane proposes and doesn't bother disguising it as anything but a vote.
- **Vellum and Carrow:** the buried crime underneath everything else. Nobody at Vellum except Alaric knows the full shape of it.
