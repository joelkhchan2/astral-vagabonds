# House Carrow, the empty chair

> Economic data and the confrontation scene itself: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people. Deliberately bare-bones per standing decision, build further only when the thread actually gets pulled.

The fifth founding house, gone forty years, officially to mismanagement. Actually: the Amberwake corridor died first, House Vellum watched it happen and said nothing, and when Osric Carrow worked out the truth and threatened to expose it, Alaric Vellum had him killed.

## Osric Carrow, the last head (deceased)

Nothing more needed beyond `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`'s account: a cartographer who followed his own data somewhere nobody wanted him to go, confronted the man responsible directly instead of going to the Board first, and paid for that choice within days.

## Vesper Carrow, the descendant

A surviving branch of the house that read what happened to Osric correctly and went to ground on purpose rather than scatter in a panic, same shape as how Vellum crushed the Vane Compact a generation later: a slow, patient, deniable dismantling that leaves no single dramatic blow to point at. Forty years patient.

**Current plan, kept narrow on purpose.** Vesper has spent years quietly rebuilding a paper trail that would corroborate exactly what the crew's own lockbox already proves, old shipping manifests, secondhand witness accounts, anything that survived the corridor's collapse outside Vellum's own archive. Neither side knows the other has half of the same evidence. Nothing more is decided, who Vesper actually is day to day, what resources the remnant has, whether they have allies, is intentionally unbuilt until the thread gets pulled.

**What this sets up, not yet in motion.** Two separate parties, the crew and Vesper's remnant, independently assembling the same case against Vellum from different directions. When and how those two threads meet is a real future beat, not decided now.
