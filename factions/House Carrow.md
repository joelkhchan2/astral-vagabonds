# House Carrow, the empty chair

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. The confrontation scene itself (Osric and Alaric Vellum) is also there. This file is the people, the scattering, and what survives.

The fifth founding house, gone forty years, officially to mismanagement. Actually: the Amberwake corridor died first, House Vellum watched it happen and said nothing, and when Osric Carrow worked out the truth and threatened to expose it, Alaric Vellum had him killed.

**Naming convention: generation-split, like the other houses.** Osric's own generation carries "Os-." His children's generation carries "Ash-."

## Osric Carrow, the last head (deceased)

A cartographer who followed his own data somewhere nobody wanted him to go, confronted the man responsible directly instead of going to the Board first, and paid for it within days (`factions/Houses & Holdings.md` for the scene itself).

## The scattering, immediate, then Vellum's erasure, slow

**The family split within days of his death**, not a slow unraveling, a fast, deliberate decision the moment the surviving heads understood what had actually happened and how little protection the Board would offer. They split on purpose, uncoordinated, so no single capture would expose the rest, and agreed, before parting, to reunite someday for a reckoning, keeping occasional, careful, infrequent contact in the meantime, never enough traffic for anyone to trace.

**Vellum's erasure was the separate, slower process.** Over the following months and years, not overnight: quietly buying out news outlets so the house stopped appearing in anything printed, striking the name from official charts and records, using the same Archive-policy machinery that already let them blame Carrow's "certification lapse" for the Amberwake's death. Two different timelines, immediate flight, patient institutional erasure, both true at once.

## The three branches

1. **Odalys Carrow**, Osric's widow. Not born into the house, married in, and took the name and its weight more fiercely than most people born to it ever do. Fled with a handful of loyal retainers to a far frontier port under a new identity. Survival and quiet remembrance, not an active plan, pure vengeance-in-waiting.
2. **Asher Carrow**, known today as **Vesper**, Osric's own child. Deliberately chose a name that breaks the family's own naming convention the moment she went into hiding, exactly the precaution someone evading a house that erases records would take. The most active branch: quietly rebuilding a paper trail that mirrors the crew's own lockbox evidence, unaware the crew has half the same case already.
3. **Osmund Carrow**, Osric's cousin, same "Os-" generation. Didn't flee to hide and grieve, fled to rebuild personal power under a new identity, using the house's old connections and knowledge for his own ambition rather than collective revenge. The one branch that might not want the same ending the others do, if Vellum ever fell, Osmund might prefer to inherit the wreckage quietly rather than see justice done in public.

**However many others actually survived stays deliberately unconfirmed**, even to these three, matching how little any of them really know about each other's situations. Each surviving branch likely has a retainer or ally of their own, **Odalys travels with a handful of loyal retainers who fled with her**, and **Osmund has recruited at least one ally benefiting from his ambition without knowing its full source**, both left unnamed for now, built out only when a specific scene needs them.

## Where the remnant stands

Not a functioning house with a public position, these are hunted branches, not an institution.

- **The Stilling:** irrelevant to them personally, their war is with Vellum specifically, not the broader crisis.
- **The players:** no awareness of each other at all, yet.
- **Captain Estra:** none, no connection established.
- **Other houses:** not applicable, they don't operate at that level anymore.
- **Politics and rumor:** the one rumor that persists, faintly, in old-timer gossip: "didn't Carrow have people who got out?" Nobody's ever confirmed it.

## Banked future scene: the memory vial

Not placed yet, built for whenever the crew actually meets one of the three branches above and earns enough trust to be shown it.

**What it is.** A sealed measure of true elemental water, drawn and bound at the moment the branches agreed to split, holding the actual memory of that pact: who went where, the promise to reunite, and the method they settled on for staying in occasional, careful contact. Playing it back works the same way the session 3 dodecahedron did (`sessions/Session 3.md`), a real vision, not a description of one.

**Why everyone who learns of it wants it.** To whichever branch holds it, the one surviving record of their own shared promise, worth more than treasure. To Vellum, a map, every other branch's location, spoken aloud by the heads themselves in the act of fleeing, **and** evidence that implicates Vellum directly, the branches naturally said why they were fleeing in the same breath as where. Recovering it would let Vellum silence every remaining Carrow and bury the one surviving record of what Alaric actually did, in a single find. It is dangerous to hold and dangerous to lose, for every party who knows it exists.
