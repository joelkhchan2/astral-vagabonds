# House Darkshade

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

Not a founding house, a chartered member house, behind Windsong in net worth (7.1M to 9.2M) and the most willing of the three fifth-seat contenders to play dirty for it. Already live at the table: Vance Orleth goaded Vaelros at the session 4 gala, and the Darkshade retainer Aerion beat decisively in that same duel was carrying the house's own colors when he lost.

## Vance Orleth, the Demi-Prince

Senior figure of House Darkshade, title and manner both performed for maximum effect, a man who goads rivals in public because he's calculated, correctly, that it costs him nothing and occasionally rattles someone into a mistake. Already established on the page: he needled Vaelros in front of the whole gala about "what of your others," the line that put Aerion in the dueling ring in the first place.

**Want:** the fifth seat, by whatever means actually works, reputation, sabotage, or genuine merit, roughly in that order of preference.

**Voice tic:** compliments an opponent's weakness directly to their face, framed as admiration, "how refreshing, to see a house field its least considered son and still come away with a show."

**Physical tic:** never stops smiling during a confrontation, the specific, practiced kind of smile that's doing more work than the words are.

**Current plan, already seeded.** Per `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`'s existing line, Darkshade is "currently the most likely source of an anonymous complaint to the CIV about a certain 348th-ranked vessel." That's live and already true as of session 4: Aerion's decisive win over Darkshade's retainer, in front of the whole gala, is a real, personal humiliation Vance doesn't forget, and the *Night Fury*'s own operation (crew count, ranking, anything irregular Darkshade's people can find) is now a target of opportunity, not because the crew matters to the fifth-seat race on its own merits, but because embarrassing Windsong by any available thread serves Vance just fine.

## The house's relationships

- **Darkshade and Windsong:** openly adversarial, the session 4 gala already played this straight. Direct personal friction, not background politics.
- **Darkshade and Crestfall:** contemptuous, one direction. Corvina looks down on Vance's tactics; Vance considers her approach to be losing slowly and politely, which he finds more contemptible than losing badly.
- **Darkshade and Vellum:** transactional. Vance has no particular loyalty to the status quo, he'll use whatever lever Vellum's archive data gives him against a rival, and offer nothing back that costs him anything.
- **Darkshade and the crew:** live, not background. The one house on this list already pointed, specifically, at the *Night Fury*, through Aerion's own duel.
