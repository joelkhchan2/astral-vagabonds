# House Darkshade

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

Not a founding house, a chartered member house, behind Windsong in net worth (7.1M to 9.2M), the poorest of the three fifth-seat contenders, and the only one willing to play dirty for it. **"Darkshade" is a house title, a domain name, not the family's actual surname.** The ruling family's real name is **Orleth**, and not every member of the house carries it, the same way a noble title doesn't require every retainer to share a bloodline.

## What the house actually deals in

Not goods. **Leverage.** Information brokering, blackmail material, favors owed, rumor and gossip, sometimes true, sometimes just useful. This is why Darkshade is the poorest of the three contenders in raw gp and still genuinely dangerous, leverage punches above its weight in a way plain wealth doesn't. **Instability is good for business**, chaos creates demand for secrets, leverage, and favors, which means "unbalance things further" isn't just personal ambition for this house, it's the market expanding.

## Vance Orleth, the Demi-Prince

Senior figure of House Darkshade, title and manner both performed for maximum effect. Already live at the table: he needled Vaelros in front of the whole session 4 gala about "what of your others," the line that put Aerion in the dueling ring, where Aerion decisively beat the Darkshade retainer carrying a flaming blade called Smokehouse.

**Want:** the fifth seat, by whatever means actually works, reputation, sabotage, or genuine merit, roughly in that order of preference. Underneath that, more honestly, he wants the whole Board destabilized enough that houses need exactly what Darkshade sells.

**Voice tic:** compliments an opponent's weakness directly to their face, framed as admiration, "how refreshing, to see a house field its least considered son and still come away with a show," a habit that reads less like cruelty and more like a man advertising his own product, he's telling you he knows your weak point, and that he could have used it worse.

**Physical tic:** never stops smiling during a confrontation, the specific, practiced kind of smile that's doing more work than the words are.

**Current plan**, already live per `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`: Darkshade is "currently the most likely source of an anonymous complaint to the CIV about a certain 348th-ranked vessel." Aerion's decisive win over Darkshade's retainer in front of the whole gala is a real, personal humiliation Vance doesn't forget, and the *Night Fury*'s own operation is a target of opportunity now, not because the crew matters to the fifth-seat race on its own merits, but because embarrassing Windsong by any available thread serves Vance just fine. No heir on the books yet, the house stays lean and personal around Vance himself for now.

**Castor Bram**, Vance's right hand and enforcer.

**Lint**, a dockside informant at the Rock of Bral, sells rumors to Darkshade and probably others too, doesn't know or care who's really listening.

**Pavo**, a Black Parade performer secretly loyal to Darkshade (lightly touched, the troupe itself stays banked, below).

## Where the house stands

- **The Stilling:** doesn't care either way, except that instability sells.
- **The players:** live and adversarial, the anonymous CIV complaint (`factions/Houses & Holdings.md`) is already in motion.
- **Captain Estra:** the one house that might actually know. Darkshade's information network plausibly has already connected "Captain Estra Vane" to "former Vane Compact Commodore," unused leverage sitting on a shelf.
- **Politics and rumor:** the house everyone assumes is behind any rumor that can't be traced, which is sometimes true and sometimes just useful cover.

## The Black Parade

Banked, GM-only, not surfaced at the table yet. House Darkshade's own retained troupe of space clowns (the same creature type as the Velvet Comet, `sessions/Session 3.md`, but a distinct, separate troupe, not the same organization). Where the Velvet Comet are broke and desperate, sympathetic raiders one bad season from collapse, the Black Parade is **well-funded and deliberate**, Darkshade's money keeps them useful instruments rather than desperate ones. Leans into the name's own weight, funeral-procession aesthetic, mourning colors, a troupe that performs like something's already died and everyone's invited to watch it happen again.

**Some performers are actually Darkshade agents**, minor family or loyal retainers living a double life inside the troupe, giving Vance real boots on the ground for jobs a noble house can't be seen doing itself. The anonymous CIV complaint against the *Night Fury* is a plausible example of their work. Funding moves through intermediaries, laundered and distanced from Vance personally, and he also keeps a looser, informal reputation among other troupes generally, the Black Parade isn't his only tool, just his most trusted one.

A named leader (the Ringmaster/Strongman equivalent, "the Director" or "the Mourner") is left unbuilt until the thread actually gets pulled.

## Scale

A small, unglamorous legitimate fleet, under ten hulls, nothing worth noting on its own. The real size of the operation is a diffuse, hard-to-map network of informants, brokers, and favor ledgers spread across Wildspace, overlapping with but not limited to the Black Parade. Deliberately hard to count, the opposite of Stoneward's precisely-tracked Quiet Wardens, nobody, including Vance, may know its full extent.

## The house's relationships

- **Darkshade and Windsong:** openly adversarial, the session 4 gala already played this straight. Direct personal friction, not background politics.
- **Darkshade and Crestfall:** contemptuous, one direction. Corvane looks down on Vance's tactics; Vance considers her approach to be losing slowly and politely, which he finds more contemptible than losing badly.
- **Darkshade and Vellum:** transactional. Vance has no particular loyalty to the status quo, he'll use whatever lever Vellum's archive data gives him against a rival, and offer nothing back that costs him anything.
- **Darkshade and the crew:** live, not background. The one house on this list already pointed, specifically, at the *Night Fury*, through Aerion's own duel.
