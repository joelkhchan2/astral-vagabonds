# House Crestfall

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

Not a founding house, a chartered member house, richer than Windsong (11.4M to Windsong's 9.2M) and entirely a Sallow Chain operation. One of the contenders `factions/Houses & Holdings.md` names for the vacant fifth seat, alongside Windsong and Darkshade. Their whole pitch is stability and volume, which is also their whole problem: everyone already has stability and volume, Crestfall just has slightly more of it.

## Dame Corvina Crestfall, the head of house

Comfortable old money, and knows it, in a way that reads as gracious rather than smug right up until she doesn't get what she wants. Genuinely believes the Board doesn't need a frontier-capable fifth seat, it needs a reliable one, and that Crestfall's decades of clean, boring, profitable Chain operation is a stronger case than anyone else's.

**Want:** the fifth seat, specifically for Crestfall, won on the argument that the Board has had enough excitement for one century and could use a house that simply doesn't make mistakes.

**Voice tic:** measures everything against "the old numbers," a specific, nostalgic comparison to how things were done a generation back, usually to imply the present is getting carelessly loud.

**Physical tic:** carries an antique coin from the house's founding, turns it over in one hand while she listens, never while she talks.

**Current plan.** Corvina can't out-innovate Windsong's frontier pitch or Delverane's planar reach, so she's not trying to. She's quietly using her connections to slow-walk procedural recognition of any new claimant to the seat, Vaelren's Fifth House bid included, on the theory that a known, boring quantity (more Crestfall-shaped stability) benefits from nothing happening quickly. She would rather the seat stay empty another decade than see it filled by anyone more interesting than she is.

## The house's relationships

- **Crestfall and Windsong:** quiet, mutual, mostly unspoken rivalry for the same seat. No open hostility yet, both houses would rather let the other overextend first.
- **Crestfall and Darkshade:** contemptuous, one direction. Corvina considers Darkshade's tactics beneath a serious contender and has said so, carefully, in rooms where it would get back to Vance Orleth.
- **Crestfall and Vellum:** comfortable. Corvina's entire platform is Vellum's own stewardship argument, just applied to a member house instead of a founding one.
- **Crestfall and the crew:** no angle yet. Background, for now, exactly the kind of house that would never notice a 340th-ranked independent crew exists, which is itself the point of her.
