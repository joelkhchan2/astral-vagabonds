# House Crestfall

> Economic data: `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`. This file is the people and the live politics.

Not a founding house, a chartered member house, richer than Windsong (11.4M to Windsong's 9.2M) and entirely a Sallow Chain operation, straightforward hauling and trade volume along the safest, most profitable lanes in Wildspace. One of the contenders for the vacant fifth seat, alongside Windsong and Darkshade. Their pitch is stability and volume, and their real strategy is patience: outlast everyone else and inherit by default.

**Naming convention: generation-split.** The head's generation carries "Cor-." The heir's generation carries "Sha-."

## The history behind the name

Roughly the same era House Carrow fell, Crestfall also reached for frontier ambition, overextending into less stable, less charted routes chasing the same kind of growth Carrow had. The early tremors of the Stilling hit their overextended positions hard, and they came within a hair of the same fate. They pulled back just in time, retreated entirely into the safe, boring Sallow Chain core, and watched Carrow actually go under reaching for exactly what they'd almost died reaching for too. Every bit of the house's caution since is a lesson learned from watching someone else pay the price they nearly paid themselves, the "crestfall" the name quietly commemorates, whether or not anyone outside the house remembers why.

## Corvane Crestfall, the head of house

Old enough to remember the near-collapse firsthand. Genuinely boring day to day, real caution, not a performance, but patient and ready to act fast and quietly the moment any rival's weakness shows.

**Want:** survive long enough to inherit by default. Not ambition in the usual sense, patience weaponized.

**Voice tic:** answers questions about risk with a specific, remembered number from the near-collapse, decades old, recited exactly, "we lost eleven ships that season, I have never forgotten the count."

**Physical tic:** keeps a small, cracked piece of the house's original heraldic crest, damaged in the collapse and never repaired, on his desk as a permanent reminder. Touches it before any decision that risks the house's position.

**Current plan.** Slow-walking procedural recognition of any new claimant to the fifth seat, Vaelren's bid included, on the theory that delay costs him nothing and stability always wins a long game, while watching House Vellum's position for the first real sign of weakness. He will not gamble the house. He will absolutely scavenge, and he's willing to give a small, deniable push if a safe, untraceable opportunity ever presents itself.

## Shalendra Crestfall, the heir

Corvane's daughter, "Sha-" generation. Name only for now, built out whenever a scene calls for her.

## More of the house

**Corvassa Crestfall**, Corvane's sibling, same "Cor-" generation, more risk-tolerant than he is. Real internal tension about whether the house's patience is wisdom or just fear dressed up as strategy.

**Hensley Dray**, non-family, runs the actual hauling fleet's logistics.

**Captain Bellwick**, a Crestfall ship captain, reliable, boring, exactly the house's whole brand in one person.

**Finch**, a young, eager dockhand, recently hired, loyal without quite understanding that loyalty here means staying unremarkable.

## Where the house stands

- **The Stilling:** quiet caution bordering on denial, mirrors Vellum's position without the ideology behind it.
- **The players:** none. Genuinely beneath notice at their current rank.
- **Captain Estra:** unaware, she was beneath Crestfall's notice even at her peak.
- **Politics and rumor:** famously boring to gossip about, which Corvane considers a feature, not a failure.

## Scale

A real, solid trade and hauling fleet working the Chain's safest, most profitable lanes, roughly 15-20 hulls, nothing flashy, nothing built to fight, built to move volume reliably for decades. Personnel-wise, a few thousand across warehousing, brokerage, and crew, comfortably mid-sized, nothing like Vellum's archive network or Stoneward's arms industry.

## The house's relationships

- **Crestfall and Windsong:** quiet, mutual, mostly unspoken rivalry for the same seat. No open hostility yet, both houses would rather let the other overextend first.
- **Crestfall and Darkshade:** contemptuous, one direction. Corvane considers Darkshade's tactics beneath a serious contender and has said so, carefully, in rooms where it would get back to Vance Orleth.
- **Crestfall and Vellum:** comfortable for now, Corvane's entire platform is Vellum's own stewardship argument, just applied to a member house instead of a founding one, but he's watching Vellum's position more closely than he lets on.
- **Crestfall and the crew:** no angle yet. Background, for now, exactly the kind of house that would never notice a 340th-ranked independent crew exists, which is itself the point of him.
