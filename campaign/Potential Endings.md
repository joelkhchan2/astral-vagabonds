# Potential Campaign Endings

These are not mutually exclusive -- the final ending will likely combine elements from multiple options. The right ending depends on player choices, character arcs, and what feels earned by the time we get there. Documented here as reference points, not commitments.

## A: "The Launch"
**Core emotion:** Hope, construction. "We built something."

The campaign ends with the formal launch of the open navigation network as the new infrastructure of Wildspace. Climax is a summit where factions negotiate the network's governance. Priscilla architects the framework. Gabrielle's bestiary data provides the scientific breakthrough that makes the network viable, then she leads the expedition to recover her lost crew as proof of concept. Estra watches from the background as her principle -- "chart it, share it" -- is spoken by strangers who don't know where it came from.

Final scene: Vagabonds back on the ship. Network is live. "Where to?" Nobody knows. They pick a direction nobody's charted yet.

**Pale Reach:** Reform or coexistence. | **Estra:** Lives (watches her dream built).

---

## B: "The Rescue"
**Core emotion:** Adventure, homecoming. "We went further than anyone thought possible, and we brought people home."

The campaign's final arc is the most dangerous voyage anyone has ever attempted -- into Drift-shifted currents to recover Gabrielle's lost crew. The network, the Compact rebuild, everything was building toward this. A small fleet navigates the new Astral using Gabrielle's bestiary data, Priscilla's network, and ancient instruments. The voyage itself IS the network's launch -- real-time data from every ship transmitted back as they fly.

Gabrielle finds her crew, alive and changed. Some come home, some stay -- she has to accept who they are now. Priscilla's network gains more coverage of the new Astral from this single voyage than the Pale Reach's entire archive. Estra captains or is honored by the voyage she spent her whole career making possible.

Final scene: The fleet returns. The deep Astral is chartable. Gabrielle's recovered crew join the fleet. Route data filed openly, freely, for everyone.

**Pale Reach:** Left behind, irrelevant. | **Estra:** Either (voyage works both ways).

---

## C: "The Succession"
**Core emotion:** Bittersweet, growth. "We became the thing we needed."

The Broker calls in favor #3: Estra must surrender her CIV charter. Walk away. This forces the question the entire campaign built toward -- can the Vagabonds exist without Estra? The crew chooses Gabrielle as captain. Priscilla runs everything else. Estra lets go -- the hardest thing she's ever done.

Gabrielle's moment mirrors and inverts her backstory: she lost a crew she couldn't save, now a crew chooses her. Priscilla gets what she always wanted -- to be the person without whom nothing works, from the bosun's post and the ledger. Estra trusts that what she built can stand without her.

Final scene: The ship, under new command. Coffee stain still on the captain's chair. The ship creaks. "She's just talking." "I know." They fly.

**Pale Reach:** Inherited problem for the new leadership. | **Estra:** Dies or retires (the point is letting go).

---

## D: "The New Map"
**Core emotion:** Awe, understanding. "The universe is stranger and more beautiful than we thought."

The crew reaches the Empty Frequency source -- not just a weather station but a complete record of the Astral's current systems spanning millions of years. The revelation: the currents aren't just physics. They're shaped by the civilizations that travel them. The Astral Sea responds to thought. It's listening. Routes aren't psychic residue -- they're a conversation between the medium and the minds that move through it.

Gabrielle's bestiary is vindicated as the first field guide to a living, responsive medium. Priscilla's network becomes a way for civilization to consciously participate in shaping the Astral. Estra's philosophy was a description of how the Astral actually works -- charting IS sharing, every journey IS maintenance.

Final scene: The crew at the viewport. Same silver void. Now they know it's listening. Reeve: "Reeve always wondered if it could hear us. Now Reeve knows." They chart a course, and for the first time, they understand what that means.

**Pale Reach:** Irrelevant (you can't monopolize a conversation). | **Estra:** Lives (she deserves to see the truth).

---

## E: "The Fleet"
**Core emotion:** Triumph, expansion. "We became something bigger than ourselves."

Everything at once -- the Drift's most chaotic phase, the Pale Reach's last play, the Broker's final favor, and Gabrielle's crew finally within reach. The Vagabonds marshal everything they've built: every alliance, every ship, every piece of data, every favor on Brass's napkins. Gabrielle commands a multi-ship fleet action. Priscilla's architecture makes it possible -- every ship in formation is a line in her Index of Accounts. Estra sees the Compact reborn, better than before.

Final scene: The fleet charting routes in formation. Tack talking to the engine, Finny cooking for fifty ships, Oz writing thank-you notes to allied captains. A new ship joins -- one of Gabrielle's recovered crew at the helm. Estra's mug on the table, whether she's there or not.

**Pale Reach:** Absorbed, outmaneuvered, or broken. | **Estra:** Either (fleet is the legacy).

## Design Note
The best ending combines elements. B's rescue voyage could be the vehicle for D's revelation. C's succession could happen within E's fleet action. A's summit could follow B's voyage. Let the campaign tell us which combination feels right. Build toward all of them; commit to none until Tier 3.
