Delivered at Real, Documented Expense

To Thistle

regarding the matter of the teapot
Three Weeks of Deliberation Preceded This Letter

I hope this letter finds you well, truly, and I mean that, though I understand if you find it hard to believe given how long I let this sit before finally, graciously, deciding to put it to rest.

I did not break it. I wish to state that plainly and for the record. I merely returned it slightly warmer than I received it, a consequence of ordinary use, which you have chosen to interpret as "ruining the temper of the clay," a phrase I did not know applied to teapots until you said it to six different people at the market, loudly, within my hearing range, on purpose.

I am sorry. I am sorry that you are the way that you are about a teapot. I am sorry that this letter costs me actual coin to send via a crew with, I am told, a documented history of boarding hostile vessels, because apparently that is what it takes to get a simple acknowledgment delivered without you reading something into my tone.

There was no tone. There is tone now, because you have exhausted me into one.

Return my strainer.

Ansel

With Apologies · a mountain, a molehill, 75 gp well spent
