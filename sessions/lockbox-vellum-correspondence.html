Archive File 147-C · Amberwake, Withdrawn

The Vellum Correspondence

recovered from a sealed courier case, provenance unconfirmed
VELLUM HOUSEOffice of the Archive, Sallow Chain
To: the Office of the First Archivist 14th of Stormwane, RY 147

I am filing this above the ordinary channel because I do not trust the ordinary channel to move quickly enough.

The Amberwake corridor has required four corrections to its plotted line in under a year, the last two within a season of each other. Two ships have arrived late against headings I certified myself, and I do not believe either captain erred. I have walked the sightline off the Corvath Shoal three times this month. It does not resolve the same way twice.

I understand the caution around a public advisory on a corridor this valuable. I am asking regardless. I would rather cost the Archive a season of subscriber confidence than read a name I certified for in a loss report.

The full correction history is attached. I would ask that someone senior to me actually read it.

Ivor Marsh Registrar, Amberwake Desk
NOTICE OF RECORDOffice of the Archive, Vellum House
File: 147-C, the Amberwake RY 148, Emberfall

The Amberwake corridor is stricken from the public record, effective immediately. All charts, subscriber editions, and future printings are to omit the corridor without amendment or explanation. Existing chart-holders are not to be issued a disruption notice.

Direct all inquiries to the Registrar's office.

By order of the Archive.

two sheets, one ribbon, filed together for forty years
