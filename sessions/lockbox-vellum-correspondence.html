Archive File 147-C · Amberwake, Withdrawn

The Vellum Correspondence

recovered from a sealed courier case, provenance unconfirmed
VELLUM HOUSEOffice of the Archive, Sallow Chain
To: Registrar I. Marsh, Archive Office 14th of Stormwane, RY 147

Registrar Marsh,

Filing the third correction to the Amberwake plot this season, and I would rather be filing a fourth than say what I actually think, which is that the corridor has moved again and I cannot promise the next set of bearings will hold either.

Two independent ships report arriving late against Warden-issued headings, both blaming crew error, and I do not believe either of them made an error. The old sightline off the Corvath Shoal no longer resolves at all. I have walked the plot myself, twice, and I get a different answer each time I take it.

I am not asking the office to alarm anyone. I am asking for a proper survey vessel out here before I file a fourth correction that some captain trusts with their whole crew.

T. Yarrow Surveying Warden, Amberwake Station
INTERNAL ROUTING SLIPArchive Office, Sallow Chain
Forwarded
Attached: Warden Yarrow's report, 14th of Stormwane Routed to: the First Archivist

Yarrow is not an alarmist and I have known him twelve years. That said, we hold three other corridors on the frontier showing the same early pattern (files 112, 119, 138), and in each of those the correction settled inside a season and traffic resumed without incident.

My recommendation: continue internal monitoring, hold Yarrow's report as file record rather than public advisory, pending further characterization of the pattern. A subscriber notice this early, on a corridor this valuable, would cost the Archive more in confidence than the delay would cost in risk.

I would rather be wrong quietly than wrong in front of every chart-holder in the Chain.

I. Marsh Registrar, Archive Office
OFFICE OF THE FIRST ARCHIVISTVellum House
To: Registrar I. Marsh Re: File 147-C

Registrar Marsh,

Agreed. Hold the Amberwake file as internal record. No subscriber notice, no public revision, until the pattern resolves one way or the other. I trust your judgment on the timeline.

Separately, and unrelated to the above: have the acquisitions office make quiet inquiries with House Carrow's creditors regarding the Amberwake's attached route rights and contracts. If the corridor is troubled and the pattern resolves within a season as you describe, then there is nothing to act on. If it does not resolve, those rights will be worth considerably less to Carrow than to us, and less again once anyone else notices what you have just described to me. Approach this quietly and early. There is no advantage in waiting for Carrow to reach the same conclusion.

Burn After Reading
P. Vellum First Archivist
ARCHIVE OFFICE, ANNUAL FILE REVIEWfive years following

Amberwake file (147-C) formally closed and withdrawn from circulation per the First Archivist's standing order. Corridor confirmed non-recoverable as of this review. Acquisitions confirms full transfer of the attached route rights, completed at favorable terms during House Carrow's dissolution proceedings.

Cross-reference: public statement on the Amberwake's loss, filed under House Carrow, mismanagement. No Archive involvement noted or required.

File closed.
