One Last Job.

The last job-of-the-week session for now. Session 5 opens the longer arc; tonight stays a simple, self-contained mission like the others, but it's the sendoff for the format, not a session that needs to seed the next tier's plot. Three postings sit at the top of the Board, uncommitted, pick live at the table.

Both leads get a validation beat tonight, carried by what's already built, not a bolted-on extra scene.

The one rule that outranks this page

Never decide for the players. Orena and Aerion are Gabrielle's and Priscilla's. This page gives situations, options, and spotlight opportunities, never what they buy, say, feel, or choose. Violet boxes flag exactly where the next move belongs to a player.

How to use this page

Plum blocks are GM-only. Blue Read aloud boxes are narration to say or paraphrase. The Job is tabbed, all three postings are live options, pick whichever lands at the table. Narration follows rules/DMing Craft.md.

Cold Open: the Flashback, Then Home Port

Open here, before anything present-day. Not Aerion's private memory, a real shared cold open the whole table watches, the same move session 1 and session 3 both used to start on a charged image rather than a recap. The night Aerion actually chose to leave the family business, two beats, then a hard cut to the present.

Beat one, the function

A real House Windsong event, board members or allied houses present, a sparring showcase at its center. Aeric and Aeris both fight, Champion-clean, exactly the display the house wants. Aerion isn't on the roster; if he pushes to be included, Vaelros allows one token bout, against a house guard or a hired second, no real stakes, no real respect, a courtesy match, not a test. Being offered the undercard is the insult, he doesn't need to lose badly for it to land.

Beat two, the ultimatum, same night, right after, still raw

Guests gone, Vaelros finds him alone.

Vaelros"We move goods through open space. A man who can't defend what's his doesn't keep it long, that's not philosophy, that's arithmetic. Your brothers understand that. Whatever you're doing with Vaelren's blade, it isn't that."
Vaelros, sharper"And look at where it got him. We eased him out for the house's own good, and you want to inherit his same end?"

The ultimatum: give it up, commit fully to the business, or lose his place in it. Vaelros's words, the brothers' postures (Aeric uncomfortable with a win he didn't ask for, Aeravellis visibly struggling), the room itself, all usable as-is. Everything Aerion actually says or does in response stays open, don't script his words.

Cut hard to the present

The usual "years later" snap, no lingering transition needed. Arrival back at the Rock of Bral, played warm. Rank update lands here, Finny's party, the usual homecoming texture. This is also where crew hiring actually happens, not improvised cold later.

The rank update

344th, confirmed. Two Chairs Short (session 3) was a joint job, supporting Albus's Fair Return rather than a solo climb, so the earned move is a modest one rank. Real churn alongside it: the Fair Return gets a firm placement at 343rd, a real neighbor now, not a rumor; the Weatheralls' Kettle climbs one to 345th; Dov Errin's Steadfast Promise finally loses its charter outright after eleven months impounded; Rimmon Vex's Cutlass Sunday drops two full ranks to 346th, passed by both the crew and the Kettle in the same update. Let Finny throw the party he's been owed.

Where the hires are found

The Compass Rose board, a direct referral, or just dockside. Let the PCs' own approach decide this, it only changes how many show up and in what order.

Run it back-to-back

Three candidates per slot, not two long scenes. Nobody needs full depth live, each has one entrance beat and one follow-up moment, enough to improvise cold. Run as many as the table has patience for; the actual hire is whichever one or two land, not a predetermined pick. Any candidate not hired stays a real person the crew might cross again, not a discarded NPC.

Social: Aerion's Uncle, and the Carrow Letters

Both leads' validation beats live here, picking up what the cold open already set in motion (§02). Aerion's pays off here; Orena's comes sideways, through the Board pick and the bastion turn, not a separate scene this session, by design.

Vaelren, at the Compass Rose

Unannounced, when the crew comes through. Aerion's uncle, trainer, and the model for his own restraint, never actually played at the table before, only referenced. The visual is the whole joke before he says a word: a loud Hawaiian shirt, socks and sandals, nothing that reads as "space samurai" at all, a direct punchline against both the composed trainer Aerion remembers and the severe Vaelros the table just watched in the cold open.

Read aloud, Vaelren's entrance

He's not hard to spot. He's the only person at the Compass Rose in a shirt loud enough to be heard, socks pulled up under sandals, nothing about him reading as "space samurai" except, if you know to look for it, the economy of every small movement. He sees Aerion before Aerion sees him, and he grins like this is the best thing that's happened to him all week.

Vaelren"Your father called it getting eased out. I call it the best decision I ever made. There's no ceremony in it, kid, I just stopped showing up to things that made me miserable, one at a time, until none of them were left."
Vaelren, when pushed"Lost? No. I won. Nobody tells you winning looks like this, but it does."
Vaelren, quieter, the real point"You've stood like that since you were twelve, I know, shoulders squared like the whole house falls down if you ever once unclench. Nobody's going to blame you for setting it down a minute. Not even you, if you let yourself."
Vaelren"You don't have to win his argument, kid. You could just stop having it. Be whoever you actually are with that sword, not whichever version keeps the ledger looking good on paper."
The present-day payoff, now carrying the full weight of the cold open

The table just watched Vaelros call Vaelren a failure, that same night, years ago. Vaelren, sitting across from Aerion right now, genuinely happy, is the living, undeniable rebuttal to that framing, whether or not either of them says so out loud. Everyone at the table feels this now, not just Aerion, that's the whole reason the flashback opened the session instead of staying a private memory. Let it sit without over-explaining it. How Aerion actually reacts to Vaelren stays entirely open, don't script his words.

The Carrow letters, and Estra's own question

Estra already knows the Vellum note was a bluff. What's still unresolved: the Carrow/Amberwake letters themselves, and Estra's own pointed question, whether there wasn't something more Orena wanted to ask the dodecahedron. A joint Orena/Aerion scene, the two of them alone with what's still unshared.

Secrets, not stated outright

The letters tie House Carrow's fall directly to House Vellum profiting from it, decades before any of this. Estra's own locked star chart already carries unexplained Amberwake annotations. Handing this to her means handing her a piece of her own fleet's history she's never had. Not telling her means answering her direct question with another evasion, which she'll likely clock.

  1. Do they even agree with each other? Aerion's read (practical, political) and Orena's (instinctive, protective of Estra) don't have to land the same place.
  2. If they decide to tell her: a real scene, played straight. Her reaction is hers to have, not scripted here.
  3. If they wait, or answer some other way: a legitimate, standing choice, not a failure state. Still-open going into session 5.

The Job: the Board Pick

The Board always gets picked by pay, so only these three need full depth. All three are live, uncommitted, pick at the table. Everything else on the board this rotation is a one-liner, listed after the tabs.

Dressing the Part

A minor noble house needs a reputable, prestigious charter to carry the family to a grand event well off the common lanes: a confirmation gala, a houses' summit, a season ball where the guest list is the whole point.

Complication

The crew is 344th on a Board that counts, flying a hull under a borrowed name. The job is to pass: keep the rank buried, keep Tack from introducing Deborah to the valet, make the tattered flag read as heritage instead of salvage.

Qualifier (soft, and the whole premise)

The posting technically asks for "a reputable, established charter." The family's budget doesn't stretch to one, which is the only reason this crew got the call, and passing in person is the job.

Spotlight

Aerion's real poise, the family game he actually knows how to play, lands as pure competence this time rather than pain, a direct tonal counterweight to the Vaelren/Vaelros scene the same session. Oz's flawless manners carry the whole imposture, a rare real spotlight for him tonight. Good money for farce, with a real bite if the mask slips.

Last Known Position

An independent trading crew's ship went silent two days ago near a dead-hulk field, hasn't answered a single hail since. The client, an insurer or the ship's own worried partner outfit, pays well for someone to find out what happened and render aid if there's aid left to render. Not a social grudge, not another Manifest Board rival.

Open Water, Open Season

The posting comes directly from Aurel Delverane's own survey office at the Farthing Station, not an anonymous clerk. The Windward Edge's standing surf has been creeping inward, the Guttering wearing its most beautiful face, and it's made a specific, previously-reliable stretch of chart unreadable. Delverane wants to know whether the old charts can still be trusted, and Orena is the one who can actually answer that.

A standing CIV Open Season bounty rides alongside on any scavvers encountered (gray 5 gp, brown 250 gp, night 750 gp), incidental to the survey, not the job's point.

What's actually out there, by DC tier (Survival/Nature)

  • DC 10: an ordinary, usable lane, low value (50-300 gp).
  • DC 13: the lane brushes a patch of visibly thinning Tide, a small, real Guttering symptom, mid value (300-1,500 gp). Orena's own call whether the writeup is honest or softened.
  • DC 16+: the thinning traces to a physical marker, an old corridor signpost from a delisted route, unprecedented value (1,500-10,000+ gp). The same pattern Meech has been privately tracking, pure color unless the table pulls on it.

Aurel Delverane, her first real on-screen scene

Thirty-one, younger daughter of a founding house that's never forgiven her for choosing fieldwork over a boardroom. Runs survey and acquisition out of the Farthing Station personally, hands dirty, not delegated.

Voice tic: narrates her own observations out loud, half to herself. Physical tic: chart-ink on at least one finger, never quite washed off. Want: to be the one who's right about the Edge's surf before anyone senior takes it seriously. Flagged, per existing canon, as the single most likely person in Wildspace to become a real ally, they're neighbours and neither knows it yet.

One Last Job, in full
