## Legendary rewards, banked (Tier IV, never shop inventory)

The crew-pool shop (`ships/Ship Construction.md` &sect;"Crew-Pool Upgrades, Tiered", mirrored in `sessions/danforth-yard-shop.html`) stops at Tier III on purpose. These five are the Tier IV ceiling above it, designed but deliberately left out of every reference doc and the catalog itself. They're one-off DM rewards, never purchasable, never listed anywhere a player can see a price tag.

| Idea | Hook | Status | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Last Rite (Medic) | The Medic attunes to the spelljamming helm itself, a second bond alongside whoever flies her, and casts a Raise Dead-equivalent once per voyage | Mechanic designed in chat, not written anywhere | Unassigned, DM-awarded only | Sits directly above Tier III's Mender's Rite (real healing, no-death-save revival). Reuses the existing helm-attunement rule rather than inventing a new one |
| The Last Word (Helm) | Once per voyage, the Helm acts twice in the same round, full maneuvers both times | same | same | Sits above Tier III's Twinned Hands |
| The Captain's Voice (Coordinator) | The Coordinator's chosen benefit becomes passive for the rest of the encounter after it first triggers | same | same | Sits above Tier III's Unbroken Line |
| The Mending (Engineer) | Once per voyage, fully restores the hull to max HP as a bonus action | same | same | Sits above Tier III's Hull-Weaver's Rig |
| Bound Guardian Ward (Shipboard enchantment) | A permanently bonded elemental or spirit intercepts one killing blow against the hull per voyage, no action required | same | same | Sits above the Tier III vehicle-affecting castings (Translocation Working) |
