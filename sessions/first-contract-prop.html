Conditions & Endorsements

  1. Perishables. Lots I and V are carried at the Carrier's care; spoilage in transit is deducted from the Payout at the Consignee's assessment.
  2. Air & complement. The Carrier attests a breathable envelope adequate to the voyage and warrants it will not exceed its rated complement. The Registry assumes no liability for the air clock.
  3. Inspection. This carriage is subject to Route Warden inspection at any point without appeal. Delay so incurred is the Carrier's to bear.
  4. Settlement in kind. Where the Consignee cannot render coin, settlement may be tendered in refined ore at the prevailing Verge rate, at the Carrier's acceptance.
  5. Salvage & loss. Goods lost to the void without negligence are noted, not charged. Goods recovered from the void remain the Registry's to adjudicate.
  6. Course accuracy. Filed coordinates are provided in good faith and are not warranted current. The Registry does not guarantee that any route remains as charted. Carriers sail the Verge at their own reckoning.
  7. Reputation. Completion is scored to the Carrier's standing on the Manifest Board. Non-delivery is likewise recorded.
