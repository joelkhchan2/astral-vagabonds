The Windward Edge.

Open Water, Open Season finally launches. The crew sails out to the literal confluence where two currents collide, surveys the creeping surf for Aurel Delverane, and meets the void scavver that's been stalking the stalled kindori pod there, twice, once stalking, once for the hull. Two quiet plants ride along underneath all of it, and nobody at the table is told there's anything to notice yet.

The Edge is abundance, not a guttering. The teeth, when they come, come from one patient animal with a name and an old wound, not a swarm.

The one rule that outranks this page

Never decide for the players. Orena and Aerion are Gabrielle's and Priscilla's. This page gives situations, options, and spotlight opportunities, never what they buy, say, feel, or choose. Violet boxes flag exactly where the next move belongs to a player.

Cold Open: a Few Days In With Wyndham Aldane

Not a first sighting. Her active, on-screen observation stint started last session; this is a few days into it, past the opening pleasantries.

Read aloud

The galley's half full when she finds a seat, notebook open this time, actually writing, pen moving while she half-listens to whoever's talking. She's stopped pretending not to be working. Nobody's called her on it yet.

  • She asks one or two questions with real edges to them this time, nothing accusatory, just sharper than "how does a day aboard usually go." Open for the crew to decide how much they want to actually answer versus redirect.
  • The glasses tic still holds: on when she's being polite, off when something actually lands. If the table's caught the pattern by now, let a crew member notice it out loud, that's earned, not a spoiler.
  • Good spot for Flurry's running read on the passenger to develop further, whatever she decided last session, building rather than resetting.
  • Ends when Estra calls the course set and the ship actually gets underway. Cut there.

Wyndham Aldane, as played

Voice tic: talks like she's always mid-footnote, qualifying her own sentences before anyone else can. "Which is to say, probably, though I'd want to check that." Physical tic: glasses off when she's actually listening, on when she's being polite. One tic isn't a performance at all: chart-ink on at least one finger, never quite washed off, the one thing that's actually her.

Wyndham Aldane is Aurel Delverane herself, in person, under Disguise Self, not a double or an agent. Full identity, motive, and why it's held this long: npcs/Aurel Delverane.md. Nothing in this session should reveal this; it's a seed, not a reveal, see the ending hook, §9.

Main Content: Open Water, Open Season Launches

The survey job picked up at the end of session 4, finally flown. Full job terms, deliverable breakdown, and the complete encounter table live in Session 5 Prep.md §6, reproduced here so this page runs the whole session standalone.

BR-1252, the posting as readOpen Water, Open Season. Survey, a creeping current, plus standing bounty. Pay 3,100 gp, Days ~20. Requester Aurel Delverane, the Farthing Station survey office. Route Bral to the Windward Edge, the standing surf. Details: the Windward Edge's standing surf has crept inward, and a once-reliable stretch of chart has gone unreadable. The deliverable, two separate things

  • 3,100 gp flat, paid for completing the survey and reporting back, guaranteed regardless of what's actually found.
  • The navigation data itself, sellable separately, Orena's own call whether and to whom: DC 10 an ordinary lane (50-300 gp), DC 13 the lane brushing thinning water (300-1,500 gp, her own call whether the writeup is honest or softened), DC 16+ something genuinely unprecedented (1,500-10,000+ gp).
  • "Open Season" is the title's wordplay, not a requirement. The board text never mentions killing anything. The standing CIV bounty just happens to apply here too, pure incidental opportunity.
What the job actually is, and isn't

The posting is exactly what it says. What Aurel privately does with the returned data afterward, cross-checking the Edge's actual flow speed against her own years of readings, stays entirely off-page. Full reasoning, including why she sources this outside her own house: npcs/Aurel Delverane.md, "Why an outside crew, not her own." Read aloud, arriving at the standing surf

The standing surf comes into view before anything else does, a waterfall running the wrong way, real water blown upward off the collision of two currents and caught there, never quite falling. Where the spray catches the dark behind it, it streaks into shifting color, green and violet and silver-white, an aurora made of water instead of light. It hums, low, under everything, felt in the teeth more than heard. The air carries an unmistakable mineral-wet smell, rain on hot stone, water this far from anywhere water should be. Debris drifts along the boundary in slow spirals, caught between two different pulls. Somewhere past it, the chart just stops being reliable.

Why it's creeping inward: not one side dying outright, a confluence losing its balance. The charted lane's current held its ground against the deeper inflow because a resident kindori pod's regular passage kept reinforcing it. Once that pod stalled, the lane's own current weakened, and the boundary has been creeping into territory that used to belong to the stable, charted side.

Flurry's Spotlight: the Edge Itself

A scripted wonder beat, not a table roll, landing when the ship actually reaches the confluence. The "sky at its healthiest" tell, the warm register, not the eerie one, since the Windward Edge is abundance, not a guttering.

Read aloud

Flurry's at the forward viewport before anyone calls it. Paws up on the glass, breath already fogging it, chirping at something nobody else has spotted yet, the backward waterfall just now resolving out of the dark, color bleeding up through the spray like the sky itself is catching light from underneath.

  • She's unmistakably delighted, not spooked, the opposite of her guttering tell. Worth a line connecting it to Orena specifically, the dragon and the navigator both reading the same good water, one by blood, one by ear.
  • This is a tell, not an explanation. Don't have her do anything mechanical, don't name what she's sensing. If a player asks why she's acting like this, the honest answer is "she just does," same as always.
  • A clean spot for Aerion to have a small, private, undoted-on moment with her too, his choice entirely, nothing scripted.
Orena's growing attunement, tier 1

Estra asks her, half-curious, half-testing, whether the ship could actually navigate on the standing surf itself rather than just fly near it, real water, not void. DC 12 Nature. On a success, Orena recognizes something underneath the question: the way these two currents collide and flow mirrors the structure of the Astral currents she already reads by instinct, the same shapes at a different scale. A felt recognition, not an explanation, she couldn't say why it's true, only that it is. Mechanical payoff, standing from here on: advantage on one Nature or navigation check per day, specifically when reading currents or the Tide by feel rather than a chart. Tier 1 of a growing track; say nothing about future tiers at the table.

Sable's Spotlight: Splinter

A real individual scene, not just a line at dinner. Best trigger: Oz running gunnery drills with the three new hires, a banked idea nobody's paid off yet, letting Sable's bravado get tested in front of people.

Setup

The big game

Sable talks a big game before the drill even starts, "fastest turtle this side of space," taps her shell for luck. Judo, per the standing rookie-dare beat, is the one person aboard who'd actually call her on it.

Escalation

Doesn't land clean

She overcommits to something flashy, a trick shot, a stunt dodge, whatever the table generates live, and it doesn't land clean. Public, a little embarrassing, nothing that actually hurts her.

The turn

A real story slips

In the quiet after, to whoever's nearby (Orena and Aerion both work, or either alone), she lets a real story about Splinter slip, unprompted, not performed. Keep his actual nature genuinely unconfirmed, she believes every word of it, whether the table ever decides he deserved that belief is a question for much later, not this scene. Land it on her actual want: not to be the best, to have him mean it someday when he says he's proud of her.

The button

Back to bravado

Let her catch herself being sincere and immediately overcorrect back to bravado, a joke, a boast, taps her shell again. Sitcom rhythm, setup, escalation, a real feeling that gets one beat before the character defense mechanism kicks back in.

06

Battle: the Void Scavver

Entry 20's climax. Run as a direct assault on the Night Fury's hull through the ship combat rules, not individual attack rolls against the crew. Deliberately the hardest fight the table's faced since the Megapede nearly dropped Oz, calibrated that way on purpose, real stakes, not a token scuffle, but survivable.

Full ship-combat translation: run through rules/Rules & Systems.md §8 against the Night Fury's own AC 19 / threshold 25. Translate Longscar's Bite and Tail Slam as heavy single-target hull damage each round, roughly double a standard Gunnery exchange, and let a failed Helm check cost real ship HP the way a bad maneuver already would. If the table wants a fuller fight, back Longscar with two to four brown scavvers (CR 4) drawn in by the kill, not a second apex predator. Full stat block, Reference.

A legitimate outcome, not a cop-out

Longscar breaking off rather than fighting to the death is consistent with how void scavvers are framed generally, awe-and-flee, use sparingly, its scarcity is its meaning. If the fight goes well for the crew, letting it withdraw rather than die is a real, earned ending.

Wyndham's composure, the seed

Consider letting her reaction to this fight read as slightly too composed for a passenger who's never seen one of these before, a small, honest tell that she knows more than her cover requires, without saying so outright. Good seed for the ending hook, §9.

Banked, optional, not forced

If a non-climax combat on the table above genuinely endangers Wyndham personally (Thalassa's raid, entry 6, or the Githyanki patrol, entry 14, read best), the mask-slipping trigger is live: she can end it in one turn with Disintegrate, clean, no gore, then returns to ordinary composure immediately after, as if it cost her nothing. Full mechanics, Reference. This is not for the Longscar climax, that stays the PCs' own fight regardless. Equally fine if nothing this session calls for it.

The Farthing Station, Right Here, or Not

No voyage back to Bral needed. The Farthing Station is one of House Delverane's two survey outposts at the Windward Edge itself, not back at port, the crew is already where Aurel's office is. Whether they actually dock there to collect the 3,100 gp and hand off Orena's navigation data, or end the session on the immediate aftermath of the scavver fight, is the table's own pace to decide live. Both are legitimate endings; don't force the stop just to close the loop if the fight itself was the session's real ending beat.

Corwin Dunmore, the office proxy

Runs day-to-day operations at the Farthing Station. If the crew docks, he's who they actually deal with, the posting, the contract terms, the payout, never Aurel herself. Straight, no-nonsense, competent, the same restrained-practical register as Aerion. Voice tic: counts things out loud when making a point. "That's four this month. Four. I'm not saying it to complain, I'm saying it because it's the actual number." Physical tic: files and stacks everything with unnecessary, pointed precision. Want: the office run properly, on schedule, without personally absorbing the gap every time his captain disappears.

Quiet irony, GM-only

"Wyndham" is standing on the same ship pulling into her own office's dock, about as close to home turf as the disguise ever gets.

If anyone asks where Aurel is"Not here. Off on one of her detours, I expect. I stopped keeping a tally of how many a while back, the number stopped being useful."

This is a single payment, not two. The 3,100 gp survey fee lands here, official, on the books, exactly as posted. The Wyndham passage fee was already settled back in session 4 and doesn't resurface now.
