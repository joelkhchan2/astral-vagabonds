# Episode -- The International Space Dog Show (future hook, drop in whenever it's earned)

> **A short, warm, fully optional scene or half-session.** Not on the contract spine, no complication required. The lightest possible palate-cleanser: pure found-family payoff for a bit the table has already been building since session 1. Tone: PG, silly, and completely sincere about it. See `npcs/Flurry.md` for the standing hook and `npcs/Crew Comedy Bits.md` for the outfits gag it grows out of.

## Where this comes from

Session 1, at the Float Market: Aerion had a tailor at Bral making Flurry custom show outfits (glitter and non-glitter versions of a matching bow tie for himself), and floated, off-hand, entering her in the **International Space Dog Show.** Nobody has followed up on it yet. That's the point, it's been sitting there, warm and a little ridiculous, waiting for a session with room to breathe.

**Don't force it into a plot.** This isn't a mission and it doesn't need a complication bolted on. Run it when the table has just come off something heavy (the lockbox, a hard fight, a genuine loss) and needs one scene that is purely, uncomplicatedly good.

## The pitch

The Show comes to the Rock of Bral, or somewhere on the crew's route, once a year: a travelling exhibition of pets, mascots, and "companion creatures" from every corner of Wildspace, judged on categories that make no sense until you're standing in front of them (Best Void-Adapted Coat, Most Convincing Trick, Longest Serving Ship's Animal, and one category that's different every year because the last one caused an incident). It is exactly as self-important and exactly as beloved as a real dog show, transposed wholesale into the black.

**Flurry is, obviously, not a dog.** She is also, obviously, the most interesting thing anyone at this show has seen in years. Play the mismatch for comedy: judges who don't know the etiquette for addressing a silver dragon, other entrants (an astral hamster, somebody's void-adapted mastiff, a giff's ceremonial warhound) who either adore her or are extremely nervous, and Aerion, coldly insisting the whole time that entering her is "purely practical, for the tailor's investment."

## Running it

**Aerion leads.** This is his bit and his character beat: the shadow-king who does the ridiculous, sentimental thing and will not admit to a single feeling about it. Give Priscilla the negotiation with the judges, the paperwork (there is, delightfully, paperwork), and the moment someone calls his bluff kindly ("you knitted the bow tie yourself, didn't you") and he has to decide whether to lie about it.

**Orena and the rest of the crew are the gallery.** This is a spotlight-sharing scene by nature, everyone gets a beat: Oz intimidating a rival entrant's owner into a friendly truce, Finny running odds on the categories, Brass grudgingly admitting Flurry's coat looks good, Tack narrating Flurry's "training regimen" (there wasn't one). Let the table find the jokes; don't script them.

**Flurry cannot actually compete the way a real dog would**, she's two years old, easily overwhelmed, and the *Devoted*/non-combat rule in `rules/Stat Blocks.md` still applies (irrelevant here, but the "she's a baby, not a prop" spirit should govern the whole scene). The warmest version of this episode has her freeze up, get scared of the crowd or the judging platform, and the crew's real win is comforting her, not placing well. **A category she wins by accident, doing something unglamorous and true to herself, beats a category she wins by performing.**

## The beat to land on

Whatever else happens, end on Aerion. Whether Flurry places, panics, or simply refuses to leave Orena's side the whole time, give Priscilla one clean, quiet moment where the "purely practical" mask slips exactly once and the table sees, unmistakably, how much this actually mattered to him. Then let the crew never bring it up again, on purpose, the same way they never mention the outfits.

## Adjustable knobs

- **Standalone cold open or full half-session:** works as a five-minute scene between other business, or as the entire lightweight session if the table needs one.
- **A running rival:** if you want continuity, give Flurry a recurring show rival, a perfectly-groomed creature owned by someone insufferable, for future years' installments.
- **A soft plot hook, if you ever want one:** one of the judges or exhibitors is from a settlement the crew hasn't met yet, or recognizes a species/heritage marker on Flurry that raises a quiet question (never an answer) about her lunar blood. Entirely optional; the episode works with zero plot content at all.
