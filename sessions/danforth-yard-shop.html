Two things about the helm before you spend
  • A helm only answers to an attuned spellcaster, and flying takes their full concentration. Whoever flies is not doing much else while they fly.
  • Hiring a helmsman is the single biggest recurring cost you will carry, roughly 18,000 gp a year. If one of your own can fly, that money stays in your pocket. Your call to make, not mine.

Crew wages, once you are flying
