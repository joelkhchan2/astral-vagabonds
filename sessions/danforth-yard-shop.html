Hull, helm, and fittings, priced.

Reference catalog for buying or outfitting a spelljamming ship. Tap any price to add it to the running total on the right. Figures here are a planning tool; final numbers get settled at the table.

01

Costs outside the build

Not purchased here, not tracked line by line. The CIV charter, Pale Reach chart subscription, and Nightbreeze insurance exist in the world, but at the table they're folded into the standing monthly operating cost (rules/Rules & Systems.md §4: 500 gp/month for up to 6 crew, +100 gp/month per additional crew member). Surface them narratively, not as a spreadsheet.

The helm
  • A helm answers only to an attuned spellcaster, and flying takes their full concentration. Whoever flies isn't doing much else while they fly.
  • A spelljammer at the helm runs roughly 50 gp a day to hire (canon rate). If one of the crew can fly instead, that cost disappears.

06

Ship Combat Stations

Organized by station, matching the crew-pool rules (rules/Rules & Systems.md §8), not by part type. Every upgrade below lands on one of four axes: cost reduction (a station's crew cost drops), roll bonus (a flat bonus or advantage on that station's check), effect scaling (the maneuver's output improves), or pool expansion (the crew pool itself grows). That fourth axis isn't sold here: the pool grows by hiring, not by buying a part, see the crew-hiring economy instead. Tiered for the long haul: most tables below now run Tier I (affordable now) through Tier III (60,000-150,000 gp, rare and bespoke). There's no Tier IV on this lot, on purpose.

Helm

One body, flies the ship, picks one maneuver: Evasive Action, Full Burn, or Boarding Action. Costly helms (§04 above) change who can fly; these change what flying can do.

Signature quirk

Every ship gets one quirk, free, rolled or chosen at purchase. A used helm or part rolls its own quirk separately on first attunement.

d12Feature
1The float bay. One hold has a low-gravity anomaly nobody can fix. Fragile cargo. Also a court.
2She talks. Constant creaking. Whether it is structural is an unresolved argument.
3The ram fires itself. Once a voyage, unprompted, usually badly timed.
4A berth that stays empty. Complement is one higher than the crew. It is always made up.
5Warm plating. The hull runs a few degrees above ambient. Nice in the void.
6A stubborn door. One compartment opens for some people and not others.
7Load-bearing enchantment. Something in her is magical, structural, and undocumented.
8She lists to port unless someone is at the helm. Even docked.
9A previous name shows through the paint in certain light.
10Perfect acoustics in one unlikely compartment. Conversations carry somewhere else.
