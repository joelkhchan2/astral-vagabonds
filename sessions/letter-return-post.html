Sealed, Hand Delivery Only

For Sedge Okonkwo

captain, the Bright Arrears

Sedge,

I told myself I wasn't going to write this. I've torn up four letters already, which you'd find unbearably funny, so perhaps it's better you'll never see them.

Do you remember that ridiculous night at the old dock, half condemned, dancing under the stars like fools until the lanterns finally gave out, and you swore, right there, that you'd never fly again? You lied. I knew you were lying even then. I think I've always known more about you than you wanted me to.

Three months of silence, and excellent penmanship besides, is going to cost you. I haven't decided how yet.

There is something else I want to say, and I can't, not on paper, not yet. Come find me and ask properly, and perhaps I'll tell you.

Yours, more than I meant to be, D.
