# Two Chairs Short, a Ballad

*A recap of Session 3 in verse. For the actual-play record, see `sessions/Session 3.md`.*

A stone of twelve faces opened wide and showed them true:
a note that was a bluff, and a song Orena always knew.
Stowaways got fed, not thrown, a captain still their own.
The *Fair Return* fell in alongside, and neither flew alone.

Then clowns dropped from the rigging, and the guns held fast and clean,
not one hit landed on the hull, the fight stayed sharp and lean.
Two calves came home to a waiting pod, singing what they'd learned,
and the crew sailed back with coin in hand, and two more seats still to be earned.
