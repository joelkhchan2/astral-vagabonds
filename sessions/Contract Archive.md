# Contract Archive

A holding pen for retired job-board postings, same spirit as `Session Ideas.md`. These rotated off the live Charter Window board as of session 7 (none were ever taken), kept here in full rather than only surviving as a buried mention in an old prep doc. Any of these can come back verbatim, or seed a fresh posting built on the same premise, whenever it's wanted. Don't delete an entry here once it's reused, note where it went instead.

**The rest of the board (one-liners, no build needed)**

**The Standoff at Nine Locks** · 2,000 gp · 10 days · negligible. Two crews are locked in a standoff over a shared berth and cargo lock, both armed, both one wrong move from a real fight. The client doesn't want a winner, they want it defused, hiring an outside crew specifically to stand in the middle and not escalate. **Resolution:** a paperwork error double-booked the same lock to both crews, and neither trusts the other's account of it. An Investigation or Insight check (DC 12) finds the actual error and ends it cleanly; a Persuasion check (DC 13) brokers a practical compromise without settling who's "right"; failing both tips it into a brief, deliberately non-lethal shoving match. Whoever stays calmest wins this one regardless of path.

**One Last Job** · 1,900 gp · 11 days · 3 tons. An old independent crew's ship is dying for real, not just old, something structural actively failing, and they need it held together just long enough for one last run before she's properly retired. A hands-on engineering challenge, jury-rigged fixes under pressure, as much as it is the quiet weight of a crew finally letting a hull go. Full expanded version (two parts, a repair and the actual last job flown together): `Session 4 Prep.md`, "One Last Job, in full."

**The Vault at the Nine-Day Fair** · 1,500 gp · 6 days · negligible. A traveling fair's strongbox holds rigging, dye, and a half-built mechanical automaton joint, all bought on credit from a supplier who wants it back quietly before the fair leaves without ever paying. Getting in clean needs knowing who's talking to whom, who's bribable, who's distracted, except the fair runs on gossip too, and whoever's working this job is also the biggest risk to it going sideways. *A soft tie, not a threat:* one of the fair's current acts, a juggler or rigger, is a former Velvet Comet performer who left the troupe before it turned to raiding, not dangerous, just someone who recognizes the *Night Fury* by reputation and reacts to it, wary or quietly grateful nobody's come looking for revenge.

**Colors and Protocol** · 1,700 gp · 8 days · 2 tons. A giff mercenary company passing through needs a neutral, unaffiliated ship to ferry something, a gift, a wounded soldier, supplies, to a client under strict formal protocol. Doing it correctly, by the forms, matters more to the client than doing it fast. **Tied lightly to Oz:** the company's logistics officer is a giff who trained in the same era and tradition Oz did, a cultural peer, not anyone from his old unit, someone who speaks the same unspoken professional language without touching his actual wound.

**Off the Books** · 2,000 gp · 5 days · negligible. A client needs something removed, quietly, no registry, no paperwork, because the official channel would flag who did it and why: an old embedded tracking chip, a magical brand, shrapnel from a fight nobody wants on record. Brass's whole identity as a job, cheap, unlicensed, and reliable precisely because she's off the books.

**Last Known Position** · 2,800 gp · 14 days · real danger, a rescue, not a courier run. The *Second Wind*, a small independent trader captained by **Dez Harrow** (crew of four), went silent two days ago near a dead-hulk field, hasn't answered a single hail since. The client is **Oskar Venn**, Harrow's business partner on the mainland, worried and paying well for someone to find out what happened and render aid if there's aid left to render. **Not a social grudge, not another Manifest Board rival**, the danger here is a **Night Scavver** (CR 5, verified, `references/Astral Sea Bestiary.md`): AC 15, HP 105, Speed fly 60 ft, Shadow Hunter (advantage on Stealth in the dark, advantage against a surprised target), Multiattack two Bite (+7, 16 (2d10+5), DC 14 Strength or knocked prone). The stricken ship is mostly intact, but something's latched onto it and kept anyone aboard pinned down. Real mixed combat: ship-pool fire can drive it off from range, but finding and pulling any survivors means someone actually boarding the wreck while it's still circling. *Branch points:* loud (draw the scavver off first) or quiet (search before it notices), whether survivors are found at all, and the real, earned cost if the boarding goes wrong.

**Open Season, Three Rounds** · paid per kill (5-15 gp/head, scaling with size), no flat fee · same day · negligible. A nest of something small and nasty needs thinning out, fast. Payment is strictly per confirmed kill, however scored, and the clock is three rounds flat, not a round more, whether the kills come from the crew personally or the ship's guns. *(Deleted outright as of session 7, not just retired, the standing Open Season bounty already covers this ground permanently.)*
