# Session 4 -- Every Line in a Column

A homecoming session more than a job session: the flashback that opens the cold open, Brass's birthday cake, hiring at the Compass Rose, a string of small paid errands around the Rock of Bral, and a reunion with Uncle Vaelren that ends in a real campaign-level thread nobody had planned for. **The job itself, Open Water, Open Season, never actually launched this session**, it's picked and banked for session 5. See `sessions/Session 4 Prep.md` for what was planned going in; this doc is the record of what actually happened, reconciled from the full transcript.

## Cold open: the flashback

Played as the literal opening scene, the whole table watching, not a private memory. A Windsong gala, string trio, house banners nobody's replaced in a decade. Vaelros on the balcony, flat-voiced, severe, astral elf features sharp to a point, "quite, yes, it's a lovely night indeed" to anyone who approaches.

**Aerion's brothers, in play:**
- **Aeric**, the eldest, house champion, fighting a nervous 14-year-old noble boy named **Ryan** in his first bout. Aeric sheathes his own blade mid-fight to correct the boy's stance (a proper horse stance, "you'll feel you can thrust with the blade more confidently"), then draws again and wins clean. Kind in a way nobody in the room comments on.
- **Aeris**, fighting what's genuinely described as a minotaur, wins on a technical decision rather than a rout, visibly worked for it, sweating, the crowd reads it as closer than Aeris would like.
- **Aeravellis**, not fighting, not watching from the center either, slinking the edges of the crowd **actually running a book**, collecting and paying out side bets on the other bouts in real time. Confirmed on the page as more than "watching," he's working an angle the rest of the house doesn't acknowledge.

**Uncle Vaelren is physically present at the gala**, not just referenced. Drinking orange wine, half-swapping sea shanties with another house's trainer, draws his own sword as a joke mid-conversation and has to visibly rein it back in under his father's and the Gíthyanki commander judge's eyes. A Gíthyanki commander, floating level with the dueling rings, judges the formal bouts. Vaelren pulls Aerion aside for a quiet moment (the orange wine bit, "you're only 132, you're 18 years too short"), tells him their father wants a word, and sends him off to walk the room rather than hover.

**Aerion's mother, Aerynna Nightbreeze, confirmed on the page as already established** (the table repeatedly misheard and re-said her name as "Irina" live, Aerynna is the record). A cousin on her side, **Aerin Nightbreeze**, works the gala the way House Nightbreeze always does, informal deal-making disguised as small talk, caught mid-negotiation with **Factor Perrin Hale of House Vellum** over paperwork that hasn't crossed his desk yet, a pointed, polite threat about "our peaceful relationship" that Perrin visibly flinches at.

**Vance Orleth of House Darkshade** (no last name given, a Demi-Prince by title) goads Vaelros in front of the room: a wonderful showing from his two prized sons, "but what of your others," pointedly noting one slinking and one not even in contention. Vaelros, sharp, insists every son is prized and, cornered, offers Aerion up to fight. Aerion is handed the choice of opponent and picks the Darkshade retainer directly (not a house guard). **The retainer, formally announced as representing House Darkshade, draws a flaming blade he calls Smokehouse.**

**The bout itself played out very differently from how it was drafted.** Aerion won decisively: two solid hits plus Fighting Spirit for advantage and temp HP, then a critical second attack, more than enough to drop a 50 HP opponent outright. No drawn-out loss, no quiet insult absorbed. The retainer goes down holding his shin, the crowd actually applauds, Vaelren whoops loud enough to embarrass himself in front of the other trainers. **This replaces the previously drafted "clean, quiet loss" version of the bout as the record.**

Medals get pinned after, self-congratulatory, House Windsong honoring House Windsong. Vaelros's whispered lines land as drafted: to Aeric, "you held back today, we need to be severe"; to Aeris, "I could tell that was closer than you thought, you must train harder." To Aerion, no private line yet, just "we will have words, join me on the balcony in two minutes."

**The balcony, the ultimatum**, largely as scripted: *"Every guest in this room is a line in a column... We move goods through open space, and a man who can't defend what's his doesn't keep it very long. That's not philosophy, that's just math... Whatever you're doing with Vaelren's blade, it's not that... Look at where it got him. Do you want to inherit his same end?"* The new line, not previously recorded: the alternative to leaving isn't neutral, it's **"you will live in your brother's shadow, but you will support them from that shadow."** Aerion is told to take whatever he thinks fair from the house account, Vaelros's own guards will escort him to the edge of their system, and the parting line lands cold and conditional: *"If you do make something of yourself, then you'll truly have earned the name Windsong."*

## Brass's birthday

Played close to plan. Brass bakes her own birthday cake ("as is tradition"), the Tack/Finny/Oz/Estra debate over whether birthdays mean anything when nobody physically ages runs as drafted. **DC 13 Constitution save with disadvantage.** Orena failed hers (11), three days of food poisoning on the return leg. **Flurry also ate the cake and got sick, unplanned and a first**, dragons are normally poison-immune and this is the first time anyone's seen her actually ill, played for a real scare followed by a tiny claw held up in solemn recovery. Brass, crying, genuinely upset she made the crew sick, is the one who pushes to finally hire a real cook.

## Bastion downtime: Orena's research

Three questions, shorter transit allowed all three:
1. **Captain Estra's path as Commodore.** Low roll (6): confirms only what's on public record, a signed Vane Compact contract naming her Commodore. Nothing deeper.
2. **Do the houses each hold their own system?** Rolled a crit. Full answer: yes, ownership is enforced by military presence, patrols, and guard posts rather than any formal deed. This ties directly back to House Carrow: the Amberwake corridor was Carrow's, it's gone from all navigation data now, and the loss is confirmed directly tied to the house's collapse, not a coincidence.
3. **Where to find the best fabric in Bral for Orion's costumes.** Rolled 21: found **Sorcel the Sorcerer**, a scrapyard dealer who strips sailcloth off decommissioned Windsong hulls. **Grants Aerion advantage on his next costume-business roll.**

## Transit: the Murder Comet

A fleeing herd of pegasi (one, improbably, a polymorphed person named Barry) warns the crew off before "the eye" catches up. It does: a Murder Comet closes and attacks the ship directly, the first live ship-to-ship fight under the crew-pool system outside a planned encounter. The ship's 20-point damage threshold held clean through its slam attacks (no damage ever registered). **A real logistics wrinkle surfaced live: firing a ballista costs 2 crew, and the table only had 4 people actually trained as gunners**, a direct, practical reason for this session's hiring push, not just flavor. Three ballistae got crewed and fired; two solid hits plus a near-max third brought the comet down. It exploded into a tiny winged figure yelling "there is my ship!" before flying off, comic rather than threatening. No loot, pure color and crew-pool practice.

## Hiring, at the Compass Rose

Interviews ran through the original candidate roster with the table's own live choices layered on top:

**Cook-gunner:** the tabaxi ("Six") barely spoke and didn't land; Auguste, played as a fire genasi rather than a tiefling, openly admitted to burning down two of his last three ships and served deliberately burnt rice, declined for the obvious reason. **Tomlin Underhill** won it outright on a nat 20 shepherd's pie. **New, unplanned canon: Tomlin is family to Maude "Mod" Underhill**, called her "Grandmama" without anyone prompting it, and the table confirmed the obvious, with Mod's five hundred years alive, the family tree has had plenty of time to tangle.

**Scout-deckhand-gunner:** Rattle (kenku, never gave a name beyond what the table already called him) and Judo (a hadozee, not the previously drafted "Juno Vask," played as a bright, eager newcomer barely two days off a washout) and **Sable** (tortle, exactly as planned, nat 20 on an impromptu mop test) all interviewed. **The crew hired both Judo and Sable**, one scout, one deckhand, rather than picking a single winner.

**Three hires total, not two.** Crew moves from 7 to 10. Monthly operating cost rises to **900 gp/month**, not the previously projected 800.

**The declined candidates formed their own crew on the spot.** Six (the tabaxi), Auguste (the fire genasi), and Rattle (the kenku) left together, openly deciding to crew up as a group rather than disperse. A real, live thread: these three are now a named rival/sibling crew out there somewhere, not discarded NPCs.

## Aerion's errands

**Perrin's standing order came due.** Twenty-one costumes shipped at 10 gp each (210 gp), Perrin sent back 250 gp, generous, plus twenty-one professional photographs of Meep Meep modeling the full line (a Zorro mask and rapier prop among them). Flurry, watching, demanded her own shoot and got a flesh-toned bow tie for the trouble, visibly already planning a modeling career.

**The bowler hats ran into a real mechanical snag.** Aerion attempted to actually craft both hats himself (a sleight-of-hand check, no proficiency, with disadvantage), and failed, wasting 150 gp of material on the attempt. Turns out his own business doesn't craft, it sources. Sorcel didn't have a kindori-sized hat in stock (back-ordered), but did have a human-sized bowler Aerion talked down to 10 gp and repurposed. **One hat shipped, to Glimmer** (confirmed as "the one that ordered it," the kindori who traveled with Orion). Wes and Tess Leaftully sent back an exuberant letter, a photo of Glimmer and Hush wearing their marks, and 100 gp as thanks. **The second hat, for Hush, resolved off-screen**: Sorcel found and sold his own back-ordered stock, both kindori have theirs.

## The letters

Both ran essentially as scripted: With Apologies delivered discreetly (pinky toe caught in a mousetrap along the way, 75 gp paid out), Return, With Love delivered to **Sedge**, captain of the *Bright Arrears* (rank ~240-250, outside the crew's own neighborhood). **The scene expanded well past the original flavor**: Sedge broke down in front of Orion, confirmed the gossip is real, the "D" is **Dessa Kel, captain of the Compact Standard (rank 9)**, who's asked him not to fly anymore and he can't explain why he can't just stop. Orion pushed him to go find her and say it in person rather than keep hiding. Sedge tipped an extra 50 gp for discretion before sending her off.

## The brown scavver hunt

Orion chose a solo hunt (one brown scavver) over the group nest option, with Captain Estra flying support. The fight went sideways fast: a nat 1 swallow attempt went through, trapping Orion inside the scavver, blinded and restrained, taking poison damage each round. **Estra rammed the scavver with the ship to pull her out**, a full 5d10 hit that one-shot the thing and ended the fight. Meech paid out 250 gp for the kill.

## Best in Dough

Played out as its own full scene. Judges: **Mod (Maude) Underhill** herself, in person, five hundred years old and still running her Galactic Grandmas; **Anita Doe**; **Aerion**, roped in as a guest judge. Brass's entry (an octopus pie made with actual seawater in the dough) was bad by any honest read, but a strong Performance roll (15) got her third place anyway (25 gp). **Aerion and the crew pooled 100 gp each as a bribe for Brass to step down as the ship's cook permanently**, which she accepted immediately and gratefully. First place went to an unnamed member of the Dough family. **Second place went to Gary ("Sugar Gary"), formerly of Boy Boy McGee's crew**, confirmed he left and has been baking full-time since, a direct, warm callback.

## Uncle Vaelren, and the Fifth House

Vaelren is at the Compass Rose in person, loud Hawaiian shirt, drink in hand, trying to start a sea shanty with a group of out-of-work crew. **He has formally and completely left House Windsong**, not merely eased out, his own words: "I'm not a Windsong anymore." He has a pet he calls **Fury**, a unicorn phoenix, a clear, deliberate mirror of Flurry that nobody in the room comments on directly. He gives the sad-sack corner recruits his blessing and sends them Orion and Aerion's way, which is where this session's hiring candidates actually came from.

**The big new thread.** Aerion and Orion asked, half as a joke, whether Vaelren would be interested in starting a Fifth House of his own. He took it seriously. **A joint Persuasion check, DC 30 total, succeeded (24)**, and Vaelren agreed in principle to start the paperwork. Framed by the DM as a real, live possibility, not flavor: the Pale Reach is governed by the five founding houses, one seat (House Carrow's) has sat vacant for forty years, and becoming the fifth house isn't an election, it's proving yourself until the other four simply accept you. House Windsong is already publicly campaigning for that seat. **Aerion is now, openly, pursuing a path to take it instead, with his uncle, not his father's house.** Vaelren left the next morning, promising to keep an ear out for news of the Wayfinder Knot.

## Ship shopping

Bought, confirmed: **Juking Baffles** (2,600 gp, Evasive Action no longer costs the ship's own guns disadvantage), **Ablative Layer** (2,800 gp, absorbs the first 40 damage each fight, 600 gp to recharge), and a **Damage Threshold** upgrade (+5, bringing the threshold to 25). Reflex Coil's actual effect was clarified at the table rather than purchased fresh: once per long rest, not literally a reaction every time, an avoided boarding or ram attempt. Declined Plating II (7,000 gp) as too rich for now. Ending balance left comfortably above the crew's own informal floor.
