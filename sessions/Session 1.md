# Session 1 -- The Sealed Case

The first voyage. Establish the crew, the tone, and the money reality; run one clean voyage loop; plant the Guttering seeds (especially the human cost of navigation failing); and end on the cold open that launches the whole campaign. See `campaign/Story Spine.md` for what any of it means.

> **Played in full, session 1 (Sep 5).** The table did not take the Cold Run resupply job this doc originally planned around. They took **Sealed and Sworn** instead, a locked-case delivery with no questions asked, and the whole session ran end to end: the boot camp, the Rock of Bral, departure, transit, the handoff, an ambush that escalated into ship combat and a void scavver encounter much closer than planned, and the cold open. This doc is now a full record of what happened, not a plan. The ship is named. She's the ***Night Fury***.

## Goals

1. **Show the crew as a found family** doing a small, warm, funny job together. This is the 70%.
2. **Teach the loop:** take a contract, provision, fly out, one thing happens, arrive, deliver, get paid, the operating cost bites.
3. **Give both PCs a real moment.** Aerion: a logistics/negotiation/inventory problem solved with competence. Orena: navigation and first-contact observation (and, if Gabrielle wants it, a fight).
4. **Plant the Guttering seeds**, played as mild unease, never alarm (see "Seeds" below). Seed 3 (the colony body-count beat) didn't fit this job and is deferred, see below.
5. **End on the cold open:** Orena on watch, a star gutters, the Vocalise.

## The job, as actually taken

Off the board at the Rock of Bral, Estra passed on the escort contract (two haulers through the Tollmarch, hazard-rated, the crew is a hundred ranks below the required record) and the table picked **Sealed and Sworn** over the Cold Run, Ashfall Passage, and Best in Show: a locked case, moved with no questions asked, to an address in the **Sallow Chain**, owned by the largest family in House Vellum. 1,800 gp payout, 12 days, 2 tons, net 1,170 gp after the standard deductions. Legal, on the books, and deliberately boring on paper, which is exactly why it reads as a job someone wants kept quiet rather than hidden.

**What's actually in the box stays unopened**, on purpose, per Estra's own orders (she was explicit: don't look, don't ask whose it is, and the CIV charter protects them precisely because they don't know). The whole table spent real time being curious about it, wondering if it's cash, debating x-ray vision jokes, resisting the urge to pick the lock. Play that curiosity for comedy as long as it lasts. Whether the box is ever actually opened, this session or five tiers from now, or never, is a call for later, not something to resolve now.

**Why this still works for the session's job:** it's warm, funny, low-stakes competence work exactly like the Cold Run was meant to be, just shaped as a discretion problem instead of a triage problem. Aerion's spotlight becomes managing the money and the ledger around a client who wants to stay invisible; Orena's spotlight (still ahead) is flying the actual route.

**A live thread this opened, worth remembering:** Aerion's family, House Windsong, doesn't sit on the Board of Ledgers. Vellum does. The crew now knows, cold, that they just quietly moved something for one of the four houses actually in the room Windsong is trying to buy a seat in. Whether that ever matters is open, but it's now true.

## The beats

**0. Opening flashback: the boot camp. PLAYED.** Ran close to scripted: the paired obstacle wall (Athletics/Acrobatics, both passed cleanly), a nonlethal bout against a pair of child-soldier rivals (won without real difficulty, played for a little "you might have picked better" ribbing), and the bunk chemistry beat over a novel called *Dragon Masters* rather than an in-universe chart or book. New, good material that surfaced at the table and should be kept canon:
- **Vaelren's line to Orena, in front of Aerion:** *"I haven't seen Orion let anyone stick around for as long as he's let you."* A good beat to have Vaelren echo later if he ever reappears.
- **A friendly wager between the two kids**, an obstacle-course rematch for gold, which Orena lost outright. Minor, warm, and a nice callback if Vaelren or the camp ever comes up again.
- **Colin**, a fellow camper who also loves *Dragon Masters*, is a light, disposable one-scene friend, no need to carry him forward unless he's fun to.

**1. Casting off from the Rock of Bral. PLAYED.** Ran as a full homecoming loop, not just a quick beat:
- Docked at berth 40, 30 gp berth fee deducted, Estra handed over 150 gp from the prior contract's earnings.
- **The Manifest Board, checked in person at the Charter Window.** Confirmed at 348th. Confirmed live this quarter: rank 10 is pending review; **Jumper** jumped from ~12 to 6; **Consequence** slid two ranks; **Tollkeeper** and **Farthing** each climbed one. Finny reacted hardest to the Jumper news and ran off to "make some calls."
- **Halvar Meech**, half-orc Deputy Registrar at the Charter Window, stamping contracts. Asked about the Wayfinder Knot again: still nothing, multiple quarters running.
- **Sprat**, the gantry kid, for three gold (one question free): confirmed the Wayfinder Knot search is still a dead end, and dropped the Hallow's Rest rumor, a hauler limped in from there last week completely empty, with what looked like a giant bite taken out of the hull, before it was scrapped. Orena floated void scavvers; Aerion noted scavvers usually only go after much bigger ships or ones that read as threatened. Good, this stays a live, unresolved thread.
- **Senna Oric** at the Delverane window: sold her a set of Aerion's personal navigation charts. Talked her from an initial 50 gp up to 125 gp with a Persuasion assist from Orena. Confirmed on the record: **crew side hustles are a real, normal thing on this ship.** This 125 gp is Aerion's personal money, not the ship's.
- **The Compass Rose:** met **Madam Ove**, the plasmoid bartender (crackling green energy through an android-ish frame, hair like a net of green lightning), and clocked a few regulars: an old man with a thousand-yard stare over his mug, an off-shift Warden watching the dice game hungrily, and **old Pemberley**, a retired adventurer who holds court in the back when anyone gets too close.
- **Bex Halloway**, captain of the *Grendel's Wake* and their berth neighbor, confronted Orena directly, having heard (wrongly) that the crew was looking for her. It went sideways fast and Orena fumbled the exchange badly. What came out of it, and should stay canon: **Bex and Estra have real history.** Bex once wanted a job that Estra's crew took instead, and by Bex's account it "ended with a burning ship, and it wasn't ours." Estra, asked about it later, deflected hard: *"She's no one to judge anyway. Look at her, she fumbled from 330 to the mid-340s."* There's a real story under this someone should eventually pull on.
- **The Float Market:** the ropes-and-rigging tailor (name TBD, use Ilsa or invent one) is now, for real, making Flurry a set of custom show outfits, at Aerion's request, for an **International Space Dog Show** he mentioned off-hand. This is now a live, silly, warm hook, worth paying off in a future session. Also handled: a bow tie for Aerion (glitter and non-glitter versions), and a butcher's steak for Flurry.
- **Finny** came back from his own errands with a bloodied hand and a flat "don't ask." Keep it a running mystery alongside the Sef-and-the-foundling-house thread; it doesn't have to be the same errand.
- **Brass** went to go meet her guy, as established behavior, no incident this time.
- **Dinner aboard, before departure:** Brass rolled a natural 20 on the cook's d4. Octopus, incredible, universally loved, in front of witnesses, so the "her food is a gamble" bit can be undercut for one glorious meal before it resumes being a bit. Good beat: Aerion, unprompted, offered to cover future octopus nights specifically. Estra gave the crew the actual briefing on Sealed and Sworn here: legal, boring, don't look in the box, don't ask whose house it's for, that's the whole job.
- **Money, tracked in play:** 14,350 gp starting reference plus the 150 gp just paid in, minus the 30 gp berth fee. Treat the ship's ledger as Aerion's character sheet from here forward rather than re-deriving it from this doc each time.

**2. Departure. PLAYED, cut off mid-beat.** Launched from berth, Orena at the helm for real for the first time this session, Tack reassuring Deborah (the mast) on the way out, Oz up in the scout's tower, Finny sticking his landing off the bow doing a somersault for no reason anyone asked for. Into the open dark, then into hyperdrive: Orena held concentration on the flight (an Intelligence save, houseruled on the spot; a headband of intellect is now something Priscilla is eyeing for exactly this). **The session ended here**, right as the ship settled into transit and just before the next beat would normally trigger.

**3. Transit -- the chwinga, played in full. PLAYED.** Two days out, Orena hit a scheduled break at the helm and found a single fat space guppy alongside the hull with a blue-flamed chwinga astronaut riding it, fishing rod in hand. It cast its line and a whole school, a hundred guppies, three feet long each, came swirling around the ship. Flurry (Dex 13) dashed out and tackle-caught one; Finny, gunning a ballista at the same moment out of pure reflex, **crit** a Performance check stabbing his own catch, which is now just canon. The chwinga boarded when invited (Oz on guard, Estra hand-on-scabbard, everyone tense for about a second), sent calming images (a grassy field, a mother and child) to both leads' minds, and gave each of them **advantage on their next saving throw**. It stayed aboard for the rest of the leg, fishing off the hull, occasionally hauling up strange things (a boot, once), Flurry slowly warming to it and eventually gifting it one of her own scales before it and its guppy peeled off and left. **Bonus wonder, unplanned:** the chwinga's images included an actual **pod of kindori flying past, eating guppies as they went**, more of them together than the crew has ever seen at once. Log this as a real sighting, not just a vision. **Seed 1 confirmed here:** the extra passenger and the distraction cost a real day, on top of what the charts already say the route should take, Aerion's supply math caught it, Orena's course-correction caught it too.

**4. Arrival at Reefside, the Sallow Chain handoff. PLAYED.** They came up on a titanic dead astral coral, fossilized grey, lit from within, an unnamed checkpoint port called **Reefside**. Orena docked her (13 on a Survival check, aided). Waiting: **Factor Ren Calder**, House Vellum's steward in formal gold-and-maroon robes, and **Warden Rhiz Kellan** of the checkpoint. Estra handled the whole exchange personally and refused the ship inspection on contractual grounds, unopened seal and all, which Kellan visibly didn't love. Payment: **1,800 gp on the contract**, plus **Calder quietly slid Estra a separate, smaller bag "for your discretion"** after the official handoff closed, roughly 400 gp, which she pocketed wordlessly and never explained. **Finny picked Calder's pocket for the lockbox's key as he turned to leave**, entirely on his own initiative, over Aerion's objection ("bad idea") and everyone else's mild curiosity; the crew debated turning him in and didn't. Aerion, notably, wanted no part of looking in the box, he "just wants his money." This didn't matter in the end, see beat 6. **Seeds 1 and 2 both landed as planned**, mild and unremarked: the day-long delay from the transit, and Orena noting small persistent inconsistencies in the star charts on the approach that Estra had to correct for by feel rather than instrument, "the map is telling me to go somewhere, and it's wrong."

**5. "Vagabonds."** Landed here, from a hostile mouth rather than a dockside one: in the middle of the ambush (beat 6), Bex Halloway used the name at them mid-attack. Rougher and earlier than the plan called for, but it stuck exactly the way it was supposed to.

**6. The ambush, the fight, and the void scavver. PLAYED, and much bigger than planned.** As the *Night Fury* tried to leave Reefside, **Captain Bex Halloway's *Grendel's Wake* opened fire without warning**, Bex herself on the bow, having tailed them since the Compass Rose confrontation and made good on it. Full ship combat: the *Night Fury*'s ballistae hit hard early (23 damage through the threshold on the first shot); Orena and Aerion **personally boarded** the *Grendel's Wake* (a running leap, both succeeded), cut through her crew, and put Bex herself on death's door (Aerion's smite, then Oz finishing the intimidation with a two-handed hammer-swing of his own reversed pistol). The crew intimidated her remaining eight crew into surrender, looted the wreck (**1,021 gp, an oil of slipperiness, a potion of frost giant strength, and a javelin of lightning**, which Aerion kept), and let them go with their sidearms but not much else, taking food instead of leaving them stranded. **Bex's own fate is deliberately left open**, she was on death's door but not confirmed dead; don't retcon this either way without deciding it on purpose.

As the *Night Fury* disengaged, **Reefside's own defense cannons opened fire on the drifting *Grendel's Wake* and destroyed her outright**, a founding house protecting its checkpoint from a firefight on its doorstep. The explosion drew something out of the dark: a **void scavver**, easily large enough to be mistaken for terrain, rammed the ruined port directly, tore loose a chunk of the coral structure, and began feeding on the wreckage and the dead. Orena made direct eye contact with its single glowing red eye. Estra named it out loud, unprompted ("void scavver, full burn, now") and reacted with real, private history, badly shaken, hand at her chest, murmuring that this wasn't her first time and it was "a lifetime ago." **This is a much closer encounter than the original plan called for** (a distant, unnamed glimpse); it's now a named, close, violent first sighting, see the note below on `Episode - The Quiet Hull.md`.

**The crew has the lockbox.** In the chaos, the delivered lockbox came loose from the wrecked port and drifted free. Finny lunged for it first and missed; **Aerion caught it.** It's aboard the *Night Fury* right now, unopened. Finny's Arcana check (18) confirmed a **glyph of warding** is still active on it, 5d8 damage to whoever opens it carelessly, and the crew is actively working out how to get past it rather than just triggering it. **This is a live, ongoing problem, not a lost thread**, an already-delivered, already-paid-for parcel that a founding house's own steward would very much like to know is back in the hands of the people who dropped it off.

They fled at full burn. Cold open, immediately after, played exactly as planned: Flurry wouldn't settle, pacing, refusing food, staring out one stretch of dark; Orena, alone on watch at the same hour, caught a star drifting off its charted position, rechecked her instruments twice, watched it dim and go out. Both leads rolled Perception (15 each) at the same moment. **Cut to black. End of session.**

## Seeds planted (confirmed, not projected)

- **Seed 1 -- sluggish routes:** confirmed. The transit ran a full day long, inside the Sallow Chain, on Vellum's own lane.
- **Seed 2 -- ports out of position:** confirmed, lightly. Orena's charts kept drifting a hair off reality on the approach to Reefside; she corrected by feel.
- **Seed 3 (the important one) -- navigation failing has a body count:** **still deferred.** This job never took them anywhere with a struggling colony to carry it. Plant it properly on the first real Verge run, Hallow's Rest is ready and waiting.
- **The whales:** delivered, and bigger than planned, a full pod of kindori seen at once, via the chwinga's shared vision. Save the *close, personal* whale beats for later; this one was awe at a remove.
- **The Vocalise:** the cold open landed exactly as designed and ties the world mystery to Orena's personal loss on night one.
- **New seed, unplanned and big: the void scavver is not a rumor, it's confirmed alive, close, and violent**, and it just fed on the wreckage of a founding house's own checkpoint. This is a bigger, earlier escalation than Tier 1 was designed to deliver in session 1. See the Quiet Hull note below.
- **New seed, unplanned: the crew has the lockbox, unopened, on the ship right now.** Warded (5d8, confirmed), and they're actively trying to work out how to get past the glyph. A live, present-tense problem, not a someday-mystery: Vellum thinks this parcel was delivered clean, and it's sitting in the *Night Fury*'s hold instead.

## A note on `Episode - The Quiet Hull.md`

That episode was written to be the crew's **first close encounter** with a void scavver, the moment the dread gets a shape and a name, after only a distant, unnamed glimpse in session 1. That plan is overtaken: this session's encounter was already close, violent, and named out loud by Estra. Before running Quiet Hull, decide whether it's now the *second* encounter (raise the stakes, since they've seen one and lived), or reframe it around the night scavver and the derelict with the void scavver only distantly implied, since the "first sighting" beat has already been spent at closer range than intended.

## Running it, if you're prepping the next one

- **Keep it warm and funny, most of the time.** This session spent more of its dark budget than planned (a real firefight, a close void scavver, a lost warded box); balance session 2 back toward the 70% found-family warmth.
- **Competence was the reward, and it showed.** The boarding action, the intimidation checks, the ballista math, all real player wins. Keep rewarding cleverness over hand-holding.
- **Money is real, and there's more of it now.** 2,200 gp plus a 1,021 gp hoard plus three items is a genuinely good haul after a thin start. Let the crew feel richer without breaking the thin-margins genre; a big hit to the account (repairs, a bribe, an emergency) lands better now that there's something to lose.
- **Both PCs led.** Aerion ran the port, the ledger, and boarded the fight; Orena ran the helm, the transit, the docking, and the cold open. Good balance, worth naming to the table if it comes up.
- **Do not explain the ending, or the scavver, or the box.** Three open mysteries now instead of one. Let the table sit with all of them.

## Where it goes next

Session 2+ picks up with the *Night Fury* fleeing Reefside, richer, rattled, and holding a fresh unexplained star going dark. Live threads to seed forward: the deferred Seed 3 on the first real Verge run, Bex Halloway's unconfirmed fate, **an unopened warded lockbox sitting in their own cargo hold that a founding house believes was delivered**, and the void scavver sighting now sitting in Estra's history rather than a mystery still ahead of her. Tier 1's job is still to accumulate symptoms into a pattern the crew *notices*, and to let Orena's private thread start pulling them toward the edge, this session just handed it three more symptoms at once.
