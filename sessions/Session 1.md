# Session 1 -- The Sealed Case

The first voyage. Establish the crew, the tone, and the money reality; run one clean voyage loop; plant the Guttering seeds (especially the human cost of navigation failing); and end on the cold open that launches the whole campaign. See `campaign/Story Spine.md` for what any of it means.

> **Played, session 1 of 1+ (Sep 5).** The table did not take the Cold Run resupply job this doc originally planned around. They took **Sealed and Sworn** instead, a locked-case delivery with no questions asked. This doc is rebuilt around what actually happened. Everything through "casting off" below is now history; the transit, arrival, and cold open are still ahead, reworked to fit the job they actually took. The ship is named. She's the ***Night Fury***.

## Goals

1. **Show the crew as a found family** doing a small, warm, funny job together. This is the 70%.
2. **Teach the loop:** take a contract, provision, fly out, one thing happens, arrive, deliver, get paid, the operating cost bites.
3. **Give both PCs a real moment.** Aerion: a logistics/negotiation/inventory problem solved with competence. Orena: navigation and first-contact observation (and, if Gabrielle wants it, a fight).
4. **Plant the Guttering seeds**, played as mild unease, never alarm (see "Seeds" below). Seed 3 (the colony body-count beat) didn't fit this job and is deferred, see below.
5. **End on the cold open:** Orena on watch, a star gutters, the Vocalise.

## The job, as actually taken

Off the board at the Rock of Bral, Estra passed on the escort contract (two haulers through the Tollmarch, hazard-rated, the crew is a hundred ranks below the required record) and the table picked **Sealed and Sworn** over the Cold Run, Ashfall Passage, and Best in Show: a locked case, moved with no questions asked, to an address in the **Sallow Chain**, owned by the largest family in House Vellum. 1,800 gp payout, 12 days, 2 tons, net 1,170 gp after the standard deductions. Legal, on the books, and deliberately boring on paper, which is exactly why it reads as a job someone wants kept quiet rather than hidden.

**What's actually in the box stays unopened**, on purpose, per Estra's own orders (she was explicit: don't look, don't ask whose it is, and the CIV charter protects them precisely because they don't know). The whole table spent real time being curious about it, wondering if it's cash, debating x-ray vision jokes, resisting the urge to pick the lock. Play that curiosity for comedy as long as it lasts. Whether the box is ever actually opened, this session or five tiers from now, or never, is a call for later, not something to resolve now.

**Why this still works for the session's job:** it's warm, funny, low-stakes competence work exactly like the Cold Run was meant to be, just shaped as a discretion problem instead of a triage problem. Aerion's spotlight becomes managing the money and the ledger around a client who wants to stay invisible; Orena's spotlight (still ahead) is flying the actual route.

**A live thread this opened, worth remembering:** Aerion's family, House Windsong, doesn't sit on the Board of Ledgers. Vellum does. The crew now knows, cold, that they just quietly moved something for one of the four houses actually in the room Windsong is trying to buy a seat in. Whether that ever matters is open, but it's now true.

**4. Arrival -- reworked for the Sallow Chain. NOT YET RUN.** The destination is now a Vellum family address in the Chain, not a struggling Verge colony, so the shape of this beat changes:
   - **Play the Chain as exactly what the setting says it is: fine.** Stable, bright, boring, the kind of traffic where nothing should be able to go wrong. That's the point, land it as ordinary before anything else happens.
   - **Seed 1 and 2 still fit here, and land harder for it.** A run that takes a day longer than the charts allow, or an arrival point a few hours out of position, is supposed to be a Verge problem. If it happens **in the Chain**, on Vellum's own well-managed lane, that's a stronger, earlier, subtler version of the same unease: even here. Play it exactly as mild as originally intended, a shrug, a recheck, nothing alarming, just wrong enough for Orena to clock it.
   - **The handoff itself is a discretion problem, not a triage one.** Whoever receives the case on Vellum's behalf should be controlled, transactional, faintly unnerving in how incurious they are, the exact opposite of a grateful colony. No warmth from this NPC; the warmth of the beat comes from the crew's own banter and relief at getting paid, not from the client.
   - **Aerion's competence beat:** something about the payment, the paperwork, or the discretion terms needs a cool head and a ledger (underpayment, a request to sign something vague, a factor trying to renegotiate on delivery), his to solve.
   - **Seed 3 is deferred, not lost.** "The colony's own supply runner couldn't find the depot" needs a struggling Verge settlement to land on, which this job doesn't visit. Plant it properly the first time the crew actually flies to a colony like Hallow's Rest, still built out and ready to go in `Worlds & Places.md` and the session-1 companion whenever that trip happens.

**5. "Vagabonds." NOT YET RUN.** Somewhere here, an NPC dismisses them, "Estra's vagabonds", half insult. Let it stick.

**6. The cold open (the point of the whole session). NOT YET RUN.** Night, ship quiet, heading home. Orena alone on watch, updating her charts. A constellation she steers by is *wrong*, off by a hair. She rechecks her instruments twice; the instruments are fine, the sky is wrong. And as she watches, one star, dimming, **goes out.** No sound, except, if she strains, the faint edge of a note she has heard exactly once before, the night she lost her crew. She doesn't tell anyone yet. **Cut to black. End of session.**

## Running it

- **Keep it warm and funny.** The dread is one image at the very end. Everything before it is the crew being the crew.
- **Competence is the reward.** Let the players *solve* the discretion problem, the arrival wrongness, the fight, don't hand them answers. Wins should feel earned.
- **Money is real.** Deduct the operating cost and berth fees as they come up. Repairs are 20 gp/HP. Let the thinness bite; it's the genre.
- **Both PCs lead once.** Aerion ran the port and the ledger this time; Orena's big lead moment (the transit, the arrival, the cold open) is still ahead of her.
- **Do not explain the ending.** Orena keeping it to herself for now is correct; let the table sit with it.

## Where it goes next

Once the transit, arrival, and cold open finally run, session 2+ is more of the same voyage loop with the pattern thickening (more dark routes, more whales, Meech's "three frontier routes delisted with no incident reports" if a player is polite to him, and the deferred Seed 3 the first time they actually reach a struggling colony). Tier 1's job is to accumulate symptoms into a pattern the crew *notices*, and to let Orena's private thread start pulling them toward the edge. The full Tier 1 shape is in `campaign/Story Spine.md`.
