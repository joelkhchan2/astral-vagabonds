# Session 2 Prep

Working doc, not a script. Session 1 ran hot and specific; this session should let some of that settle while opening the two threads it left ticking. Cross-references point at where each piece actually lives now.

## Where things stand, going in

- **The crew is 347th of 350, not 348th of 351.** Not from climbing, the *Grendel's Wake* is gone from the Board entirely, destroyed at Reefside. See `factions/The Manifest Board.md`. Play the discomfort in this, especially for Estra.
- **Bex Halloway stays offscreen.** Fate deliberately unresolved. Don't retcon it either way this session.
- **The void scavver stays offscreen too.** Session 1 already spent the close encounter; let it be aftermath (mood, repairs, a wide berth around that stretch of the Verge) rather than a second sighting yet. See `campaign/Story Spine.md`.
- **The lockbox is aboard, unopened, in Aerion's newly-formal Storehouse.** 5d8 glyph, confirmed. See below.
- **Sealed and Sworn is done and off the job board.** Three new postings are in rotation: Quiet Water, The Ledger Run, Two Chairs Short. See `rules/Contracts.md`.

## The fallout beat (open the session here)

Session 1 ended in a fight, a loss nobody's processed, and a Board number that moved for the wrong reason. Give that a scene before introducing anything new:

- **Estra and the Board.** She's now ranked above where she was, and it isn't earned. This is the one wound she can't be dry about (`factions/The Manifest Board.md`). A quiet moment, not a speech, works better than a confrontation.
- **Repairs and money.** The Reefside fight cost hull HP; settle the bill (20 gp/HP) and the month's operating cost before anything else, in character, at the Charter Window or over the ledger. This is Aerion's natural spotlight and a chance to check the account against the 1,800 gp contract plus discretion tip and the 1,021 gp hoard from session 1 (`ships/Night Fury.md`).
- **The crew, off duty.** A short galley or Compass Rose scene, no plot, just the found family checking on itself after a fight that drew real blood. Session 1 already flagged this as a table strength, feed it another one before the next thing lands.

## The lockbox thread

The centerpiece, if the table wants a spine for tonight.

- **What's actually inside, locked in:** Vellum correspondence proving the house knew about route failure before the Guttering had a name, and specifically knew what happened to House Carrow's Amberwake in real time, and quietly profited from staying silent about it. See `campaign/Story Spine.md` and `factions/Houses & Holdings.md`.
- **How it opens:** not a lockpick, a dispel magic. **Wren Ostler**, new at the Compass Rose this quarter, does exactly this kind of work, and she wants the story of the ward, not gold, as payment for a job this size. See `locations/The Bral Reach.md`. Playing that negotiation, how much truth the crew gives her, is the scene. She'll know immediately this isn't a cheap job.
- **What it costs to open it.** Once Wren hears the real story, she's heard it too. What she does with that afterward is left open on purpose, don't resolve it this session unless the table wants to.
- **The clock underneath it.** A Vellum courier is already asking after a delivery that "never showed" at the far end. See the new rumours in `rules/Tables.md` §4. This doesn't need to escalate to a confrontation tonight, a rumour landing is enough to make the box feel urgent again.

## Orena's navigation data check

A concrete beat that's hers specifically, parallel to Aerion's ledger work on the lockbox rather than competing with it.

**The idea:** Orena has been selling survey data to Senna at the Delverane Window since session 0 (`rules/Rules & Systems.md` §5, `locations/The Bral Reach.md`). Give her a reason to pull her own back-catalog of route logs and cross-check them, on her own initiative, against two things now in play: Meech's three quietly-delisted routes (`locations/The Bral Reach.md` §2) and whatever heading the lockbox's Vellum correspondence references. **She's not solving the Guttering tonight.** She's noticing that a route she personally logged as fine six months ago doesn't match what the chart says now, which is the same kind of wrongness Meech already flagged and nobody's connected yet. Play it as her instinct, the navigator's habit of reading what's actually there, catching something before anyone tells her to look. This is also a natural place to let her private search (the Wayfinder Knot, the Vocalise) brush up against the world-plot without resolving either.

## Change of pace

Session 1 ran hot by the end (a real ambush, a boarding fight, a violent void scavver reveal). This session should counterweight toward warmth per the standing note in `CLAUDE.md`. Options, pick one, don't stack them:

- **The Compass Rose, properly.** Let the crew just be at the bar. New faces (Wren, plus whatever's rolled from the session 2 rumour crop in `rules/Tables.md`), a dice game, gossip. Low stakes, high texture.
- **The Dog Show hook, if the timing's right.** Fully written and ready: `sessions/Episode - The International Space Dog Show.md`. Best used as a five-minute cold open or a short back half, not the whole session, but it's exactly the kind of pure-warmth beat this session wants after session 1's ending.
- **A job board run, played light.** Any of the three new postings in `rules/Contracts.md` can be the actual "session" if the table wants a mission-shaped night instead of a talky one. Quiet Water is the lowest-danger option and doubles as a change of pace in its own right.

## Suggested shape for the night

1. **Cold open:** fallout, short and warm (Estra/the Board, or the galley scene).
2. **Homecoming beats at Bral:** repairs, payroll, the ledger, Senna's window (Orena's nav-data check can surface here).
3. **The Compass Rose:** Wren Ostler introduced, the lockbox negotiation scene, new rumours seeded.
4. **Optional:** the dog show as a closing palate-cleanser, or roll straight into whichever job board posting the table bites on.

Nothing here is scripted for the players. Present the situations (the ledger, the ward, the chart discrepancy, the show) and let Gabrielle and Priscilla decide what Orena and Aerion actually do with them.
