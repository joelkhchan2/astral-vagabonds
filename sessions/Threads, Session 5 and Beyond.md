Open hooks banked out of session 4, not yet built into any prep doc. Scannable, not prose, pull from here when shaping `Session 5 Prep.md` or later. Once a thread gets built into a real scene, delete it here and let the real file be the only copy.

## Dessa and Sedge, the love letter drama

**Live, not resolved.** Dessa Kel (Compact Standard, rank 9) and Sedge (Bright Arrears, rank ~240) are confirmed mutually in love. She's asked him not to fly anymore and won't explain why on paper; he can't bring himself to face her. Orion pushed him to go find her in person and say it himself.
- **The actual reason she won't let him fly is still undecided.** Options to pick from when it's time: a past accident or near-death she can't watch happen again, a debt or bond tied to his flying specifically, a prophecy or omen she's taken too seriously, or something about his piloting that puts her own crew or rank at risk if it ever came out.
- **Does he go find her, or does the crew end up playing messenger again?** Either is fine, don't force it.
- A good candidate for a short standalone scene or its own mini episode rather than a full job.

## The new crew: Tomlin, Judo, Sable

Hired, not yet fleshed past their interview beats. Each needs a real want, a voice tic, a physical tic, and at least one scene establishing how they actually fit (or chafe) with the existing five.
- **Tomlin Underhill**, cook, confirmed family to Maude "Mod" Underhill (calls her Grandmama). What does that relationship actually mean to him, pride, distance, an expectation he's running from? Replaces Brass as cook, a natural scene: does Brass resent losing the job she loved being bad at, or is she relieved?
- **Judo**, hadozee scout-gunner, barely two days off a washout when hired. What did he wash out of, and does it catch up with him? Glide membranes he hasn't grown into yet, a good excuse for a comic near-miss.
- **Sable**, tortle deckhand-gunner, claims to be the fastest turtle this side of space, trained by someone she calls Splinter. Worth deciding who or what Splinter actually was, and whether that past ever reappears.
- **Crew dynamics to seed:** how do the five originals actually receive three new hires at once? Tack's reaction to new engineering hands, Finny's reaction to new gossip sources, Oz training new gunners. A full-crew scene (not just Aerion/Orena spotlight) would pay this off well.
