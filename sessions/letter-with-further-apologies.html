Delivered at Further, Entirely Avoidable Expense

To Ansel

regarding the matter of the garden gnome
This Escalation Has Been a Long Time Coming

I had, in good faith, considered the teapot matter closed. I want that on the record, same as you wanted your strainer on the record, because apparently that is simply how we do things now.

My gnome is missing. The one with the little lantern, the one you have called "hideous" to my face no fewer than four times, which I now understand was not an opinion, it was reconnaissance.

I do not call this theft. I call it what it is: a man who could not simply let a warm teapot go, finding a new and smaller object to be aggrieved about on my behalf, as a kind of gift. I am not thanking you for this gift.

Return the gnome. Return it with its lantern intact, or I will be forced to describe, in writing, to people who did not ask, exactly how "slightly warmer than received" a teapot can actually get.

I remain, as ever, the wronged party.

Thistle

With Further Apologies · round two, 125 gp, considerably less well spent
