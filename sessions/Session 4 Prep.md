## 3. Social beat: Aerion's, at the Compass Rose

**Vaelren is at the Compass Rose, unannounced, when the crew comes through.** Aerion's uncle, trainer, and the model for his own restraint, never actually played at the table before, only referenced (`factions/House Windsong.md`). **The visual is the whole joke before he says a word:** a loud Hawaiian shirt, socks and sandals, nothing that reads as "space samurai" at all, a direct punchline against both the composed trainer Aerion remembers and the severe, buttoned Vaelros scripted below. He tells Aerion plainly that he wasn't driven out for failing to measure up to anything, he left on purpose, and he's happier for it.

**His actual lines, scripted, landing the takeaway directly at Aerion rather than just describing his own choice:** *"Your father called it getting eased out. I call it the best decision I ever made. There's no ceremony in it, kid, I just stopped showing up to things that made me miserable, one at a time, until none of them were left."* And when Aerion pushes back: *"Lost? No. I won. Nobody tells you winning looks like this, but it does."* Then, quieter, the real point: *"You've stood like that since you were twelve, I know, shoulders squared like the whole house falls down if you ever once unclench. Nobody's going to blame you for setting it down a minute. Not even you, if you let yourself."* And: *"You don't have to win his argument, kid. You could just stop having it. Be whoever you actually are with that sword, not whichever version keeps the ledger looking good on paper."* **Both Vaelros and Vaelren consider the outcome a total victory, just defined completely differently**, worth letting that sit unresolved rather than picking a winner.

**The flashback, triggered by the reunion, private to Aerion's own head, not shared with the table the way Orena's vision was.** The night he actually chose to leave the family business, same night, two beats:

**Beat one, the function.** A real House Windsong event, board members or allied houses present, a sparring showcase at its center. Aeric and Aeris both fight, Champion-clean, exactly the display the house wants. Aerion isn't on the roster; if he pushes to be included, Vaelros allows one token bout, against a house guard or a hired second, no real stakes, no real respect, a courtesy match, not a test. Being offered the undercard is the insult, he doesn't need to lose badly for it to land.

**Beat two, the ultimatum, same night, right after, still raw.** Guests gone, Vaelros finds him alone. No shouting, flat and final: *"We move goods through open space. A man who can't defend what's his doesn't keep it long, that's not philosophy, that's arithmetic. Your brothers understand that. Whatever you're doing with Vaelren's blade, it isn't that."* And then, sharper, aimed at the one thing Aerion actually chose for himself: *"And look at where it got him. We eased him out for the house's own good, and you want to inherit his same end?"* The ultimatum: give it up, commit fully to the business, or lose his place in it.

**What's scripted versus what's open.** Vaelros's words in both beats, the brothers' postures (Aeric uncomfortable with a win he didn't ask for, Aeravellis visibly struggling, whichever of them actually fights Aerion if anyone does), the room itself, all written above, usable as-is. **Everything Aerion actually says back, in both beats, and how he reacts to Vaelren in the present, stays open.** Don't script his words.

**The present-day payoff:** Vaelros called Vaelren a failure that night. Vaelren, sitting across from Aerion right now, genuinely happy, is the living, undeniable rebuttal to that framing, whether or not either of them says so out loud. Let that sit without over-explaining it.

### The top 3 (full depth)

**A. Returned, Unopened · 2,400 gp · 16 days · 2 tons**
**AGNES HOLLIS**, a retired independent trader, late seventies, dying slowly of ordinary lung-rot from decades of bad recyclers, not anything dramatic. Decades ago she and her business partner, **Corwin Hale**, co-owned a hauler; caught in a storm that should have killed him, she forged his signature and took sole title while everyone assumed him lost. He wasn't lost. He surfaced too late, with no proof and no standing anyone would believe over a licensed captain, and simply stopped trying. **Voice tic:** answers every question with a story instead of a fact. **Physical tic:** keeps touching the parcel's wax seal like it might still be warm. **Want:** to not die with this undelivered. The parcel holds Corwin's half of the original ship's deed, worthless now, the ship's long since scrapped, and a letter that's a plain confession, not an excuse.

**Where he is: Hallow's Rest**, the Verge mining colony from session 1's first contract. He's been there for years, quiet, unremarkable, doing repair work nobody asks too many questions about. **Complication:** he may not want to be found, and finding out your worst year is suddenly, belatedly, being apologized for isn't automatically a gift. His reaction (gracious, furious, something harder to name) is his to have, not scripted here. *Spotlight:* whoever on the crew reads people best gets the actual human moment at the door; good ground for an NPC crew member's own reaction to watching someone else's reconciliation land, or not. A real callback, not a cold new location: the colony already knows the crew's faces.

**B. Settling Accounts · 2,600 gp · 18 days · real danger, Rimmon Vex**
The Cutlass Sunday finally commits. Vex has been shadowing couriers and testing this crew's reputation since the Bex fight; this time he's not bluffing. A Delverane data-courier run gives him a real target and a real reason to stop circling.

**THE CUTLASS SUNDAY, stats bumped for a level-6 solo fight.** The original session-3 numbers (AC 14, HP 190, threshold 14, crew ~6) were calibrated for a joint fight with the *Fair Return* backing the crew up; this is a 1-on-1, and both PCs are level 6 now, so: **AC 15, HP 220, Speed 55 ft, damage threshold 15, two ballistae, crew ~7.** First real test of the crew-pool ship combat rules against a single committed enemy rather than a swarm.

**RIMMON VEX, personal stats, also bumped.** Reskinned **Veteran** (CR 3) instead of the originally-flagged Bandit Captain, which reads too weak against two level-6 PCs with full kit: AC 17, HP 58, Speed 30 ft, Multiattack (two melee), longsword +5 to hit (7, 1d8+3 slashing) or heavy crossbow +3 to hit (6, 1d10+1 piercing), Athletics +5, Perception +2. **Vex's hail, scripted, before anything fires:** *"Vane's boat. Heard you beat a boarding crew twice your tonnage and I've been wondering ever since whether that was skill or luck. Hold your heading, or don't. Either way I find out today."*

**Branch points:** the hail above, Vex sizing up whether this crew's reputation is earned, a real fight if talk fails, and a real, earned dent to his standing at the Compass Rose if he loses outright, not a wipe either way, he's a recurring face, not a boss fight.

**C. Open Water, Open Season · 3,100 gp · 20 days · survey plus bounty**
A joint Pale Reach survey contract with an Open Season bounty riding alongside it. Chart an unmarked stretch past the Windward Edge (real use for Orena's cartography table, the value-follows-the-leg system from `rules/Bastions.md`), with a standing CIV bounty on any scavvers encountered along the way (gray 5 gp, brown 250 gp, night 750 gp, per the Contracts board). Not a hunting trip by design, the bounty is incidental to the survey, not the job's point.

**What's actually out there, concrete, by DC tier (Survival/Nature, per `Bastions.md`'s cartography rules):**
- **DC 10:** an ordinary, usable lane, low value (50-300 gp).
- **DC 13:** the lane brushes a patch of visibly thinning Tide, a small, real Guttering symptom, mid value (300-1,500 gp). Orena's own call whether the writeup she sells is honest or softened.
- **DC 16+:** the thinning traces to a physical marker, an old corridor signpost from a route the Charter Window delisted, unprecedented value (1,500-10,000+ gp). Quietly the same pattern Meech has been privately tracking (`locations/The Bral Reach.md`), pure color unless the table chooses to pull on it, never forced this session.

**Why this one's interesting:** it's the first job built specifically to let Orena's new Library mechanics (Research and the cartography table) actually carry the session's main content instead of being a bastion-turn aside. A real DC-scaled charting roll, a real payout tied to what's actually out there, and her own call on whether to sell the data or hold it.
